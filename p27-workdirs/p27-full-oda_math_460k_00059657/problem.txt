Given a triangle \(ABC\) and two points \(P\) and \(Q\), let \(Pa, Pb, Pc\) be the reflections of \(P\) in \(BC, CA, AB\) respectively, and \(Qa, Qb, Qc\) be the reflections of \(Q\) in \(BC, CA, AB\) respectively. Let \(Oa, Ob, Oc\) be the circumcenters of triangles \(PaQbQc\), \(PbQcQa\), and \(PcQaQb\) respectively. Determine the locus of \(P\) such that triangles \(ABC\) and \(OaObOc\) are orthologic when \(Q\) is fixed.
