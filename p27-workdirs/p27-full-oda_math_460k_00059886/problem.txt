Let \( D \) be an open bounded subset with a smooth boundary in \( \mathbb{R}^3 \) and \( g \in L^3(D) \). Consider the equation \( -\triangle u + \frac{1}{4}u^4 = g \) with weak solutions in \( W^{1,2}_0(D) \). Define \( f(g) \) as the \( L^2 \)-norm of the unique weak solution \( u \) to this equation. Calculate \( f(g) \) for \( g(x) = x_1^2 + x_2^2 + x_3^2 \) in the unit ball \( D = B(0,1) \).
