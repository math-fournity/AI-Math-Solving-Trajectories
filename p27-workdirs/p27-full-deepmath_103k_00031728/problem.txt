In an acute-angled triangle ABC, point B' is located on the perpendicular bisector of AC on the opposite side of AC to B such that \( \angle AB'C = 2\angle A \). Points A' and C' are defined similarly, with \( \angle CA'B = 2\angle C \) and \( \angle BC'A = 2\angle B \). The lines AA' and B'C' intersect at A". Similarly, define points B" and C". Calculate the value of \( \frac{AA'}{A"A'} + \frac{BB'}{B"B'} + \frac{CC'}{C"C'} \).
