In an online chess tournament with 2005 chess players, each played against every other player once. Each game resulted in a win, a loss, or a draw. It is given that if any two players $A$ and $B$ finished their game in a draw, then every other player in the tournament lost to either $A$ or $B$. Let $d$ be the number of draws in the tournament. Determine the maximum possible value of $d$ such that it is possible for no sequence of players $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_{2005}$ to exist where each $A_i$ defeated $A_{i+1}$ for all $i=1, 2, \dots, 2004$.
