Let \((X_i)_{i\in \mathbb{N}}\) be iid random variables such that there exists \(\alpha>0\) where \(\mathbb{P}\left(X_1\in [x,x+1]\right)\leq \alpha\) for all \(x\in \mathbb{R}\). Assume \(\alpha\) is small enough. Does there exist a universal constant \(C>0\) such that \[\mathbb{P}\left(\sum_{i=1}^N X_i\in [x,x+1]\right)\leq \frac{C\alpha}{\sqrt{N}}\] for all \(x\in\mathbb{R}\) and \(N\in \mathbb{N}^*\)?
