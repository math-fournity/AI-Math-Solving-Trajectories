Find the last three digits of the product of all positive integers \( n \) between 1 and 1983, inclusive, for which there are no halfthink sets containing both \( n \) and \( n+1 \). A 992-element subset \( A \) of the set \( S = \{1, 2, 3, \ldots , 1984\} \) is called a halfthink set if the sum of the elements in \( A \) is equal to half of the sum of the elements in \( S \), and exactly one pair of elements in \( A \) differs by 1.
