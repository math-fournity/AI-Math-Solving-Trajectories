In triangle \(ABC\), let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(BC\). Let \(P\) be a variable interior point of the triangle such that \(\angle CPM = \angle PAB\). Let \(\Gamma\) be the circumcircle of triangle \(ABP\). The line \(MP\) intersects \(\Gamma\) a second time at \(Q\). Define \(R\) as the reflection of \(P\) in the tangent to \(\Gamma\) at \(B\). Calculate the length \(|QR|\) in terms of the side lengths of triangle \(ABC\).
