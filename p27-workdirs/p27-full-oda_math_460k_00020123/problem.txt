Let \(ABC\) be an acute, scalene triangle with orthocenter \(H\) and circumcircle \(\Gamma\). Let \(D\) be the intersection point of \(AH\) and \(BC\). Let \(E (\neq B)\) and \(F (\neq C)\) be the intersection points of \(\Gamma\) and \(BH\), \(CH\) respectively. Let \(P (\neq F)\) be the intersection point of \(\Gamma\) and the circumcircle of \(DFH\), and let \(Q\) be the intersection of the circumcircle of \(DEH\) and \(PH\). If \(A, P, D, Q\) are concyclic, find the measure of \(\angle ACB\) in degrees.
