Given a natural number \( k \geq 1 \), determine if there always exists an odd natural number \( n > k \) such that for any \( k \) pairwise different boolean vectors \( v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_k \in \mathbb{Z}_2^n \) with Hamming distance \( d(v_i, v_j) < n \) for all \( i \neq j \), there is a vector \( w \in \mathbb{Z}_2^n \) with \( 0 < d(w, v_i) \leq \frac{n-1}{2} \) for all \( i \).
