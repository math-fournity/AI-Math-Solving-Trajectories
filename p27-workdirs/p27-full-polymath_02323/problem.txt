In an acute triangle $ABC$, $M$ is the midpoint of side $BC$, $AB = 13$, $AC = 15$, and $BC = 14$. The incircle of triangle $ABM$ is tangent to side $AB$ at point $D$. The incircle of triangle $ACM$ is tangent to side $AC$ at point $E$. Point $F$ is such that the quadrilateral $DMEF$ is a parallelogram. Let $L$ be the point where the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ intersects $BC$. If the distance from $A$ to $F$ is $d$, and the length of the angle bisector $AL$ is $v$, find the ratio $d/v$.
