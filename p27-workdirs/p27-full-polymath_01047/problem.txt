The king assembled 300 wizards and gave them the following challenge. There are 25 colors available for hats. Each wizard receives a hat of one of those colors. It is known that the number of hats of each color is distinct, and the wizards are aware of this. Each wizard sees the colors of the hats of all other wizards but does not see his own. Simultaneously, each wizard reports the color of his own hat. Let $N$ be the maximum number of wizards that can be guaranteed to report their color correctly if they coordinate their actions beforehand. Find $N$.
