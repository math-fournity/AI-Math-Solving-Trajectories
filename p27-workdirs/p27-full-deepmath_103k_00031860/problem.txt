In a parallelogram \(ABCD\), where \(AB < AC < BC\), points \(E\) and \(F\) are selected on the circumcircle \(\omega\) of triangle \(ABC\) such that the tangents to \(\omega\) at these points pass through point \(D\). Additionally, segments \(AD\) and \(CE\) intersect, and it is given that \(\angle ABF = \angle DCE\). Determine the measure of angle \(ABC\).
