Confirm that the construction for a Steiner triple system with \( v \equiv 19 \pmod{24} \) (where \( v = 24k + 19 \)) works by verifying that all possible differences are covered by the given blocks. Specifically, check that the blocks \([i, i + 2k + 1, i + 8k + 5]\), \([i, i + 3k + 2, i + 12k + 8]\), and \([i, i + 3k + 3, i + 12k + 10]\) (for \( i = 0, 1, \dots, 24k + 18 \)), along with the additional blocks for \( k \geq 1 \), account for all differences \( 1, 2, \dots, 12k + 9 \).
