In a sprawling digital social network, there are exactly $2023$ user profiles. The platform's security architecture ensures that every profile is "directly connected" to at least $42$ other profiles. The network is fully integrated, meaning that for any two distinct profiles $X$ and $Y$, there exists a chain of profiles $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_{n+1}$ where $A_1=X$ and $A_{n+1}=Y$, and every adjacent pair in the chain ($A_i, A_{i+1}$) consists of two profiles that are directly connected.
