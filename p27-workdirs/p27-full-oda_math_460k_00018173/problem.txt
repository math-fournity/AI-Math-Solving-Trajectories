Given an acute, non-isosceles triangle \(ABC\) with circumcircle \((O)\), orthocenter \(H\), and feet of the altitudes \(E\) and \(F\) from \(B\) and \(C\) respectively. \(AH\) intersects \((O)\) at \(D\) (\(D \ne A\)). The lines \(DE\) and \(DF\) intersect \((O)\) at \(P\) and \(Q\) respectively (\(P \ne D, Q \ne D\)). The circle \((AEF)\) meets \((O)\) and \(AO\) at \(R\) and \(S\) respectively (\(R \ne A, S \ne A\)). Let \(X\) be the point of concurrency of \(BP\), \(CQ\), and \(RS\). If \(AX\) intersects \((O)\) again at \(T\), find the ratio \(\frac{AT}{AX}\).
