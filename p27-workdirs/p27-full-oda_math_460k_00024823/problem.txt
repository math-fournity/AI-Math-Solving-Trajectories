Creative Math and Pepemath are playing a game on the unit circle. Initially, Creative Math places 2022 frogs on the circumference. On each turn, Pepemath removes one frog and makes another frog jump 60 degrees clockwise. Then, Creative Math adds a new frog to the circumference. Pepemath wins if, at the end of any turn, the center of mass of the 2022 frogs is less than $\frac{1}{1000}$ unit distance from the origin. If Pepemath cannot win within 69420 turns, Creative Math wins. Who has a winning strategy?
