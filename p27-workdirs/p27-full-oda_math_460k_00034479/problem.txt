Given a triangle \(ABC\) with \(N\) and \(O\) as the centers of the Euler circle and circumcircle, respectively. Let \(A^*B^*C^*\) be the antimedian triangle of \(ABC\). Define \(B''\) and \(C''\) as the feet of the perpendiculars from \(O\) to \(A^*C^*\) and \(A^*B^*\), respectively. Let \(O_a\) and \(H_a\) be the circumcenter and orthocenter of triangle \(A^*B''C''\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{ON}{O_aN}\).
