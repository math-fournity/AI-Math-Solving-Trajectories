Let $\pi:Y\rightarrow \mathbb{P}^3$ be the blow-up of two points $p,q\in\mathbb{P}^3$, and then of the strict transform of the line $L$ spanned by them. Let $E_p,E_q, E_{p,q}$ be the exceptional divisors over $p,q$, and $L$, respectively. $E_p$ and $E_q$ are isomorphic to the blow-up of a point in $\mathbb{P}^2$, and $E_{p,q}\cong \mathbb{P}^1\times\mathbb{P}^1$. Let $\sigma_{p,q}\subset E_{p,q}$ be the class of the $\mathbb{P}^1$ which is not contracted by $\pi$. Given that $E_p\cdot \sigma_{p,q} = E_q\cdot\sigma_{p,q} = 1$, find the intersection number $E_{p,q}\cdot \sigma_{p,q}$. Provide your answer as a single number.
