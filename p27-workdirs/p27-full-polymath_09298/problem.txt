In the futuristic city of Neoterra, a massive power grid is organized into a perfect square array of $100 \times 100$ individual solar tiles. To manage energy distribution, the city council has mandated that every single tile must be assigned a specific "Frequency Channel" (a color code). However, to prevent signal interference, there is a strict regulatory constraint: no single Frequency Channel can be assigned to more than $104$ tiles across the entire grid.
