We say that a positive integer \( n \) is divable if there exist positive integers \( 1 < a < b < n \) such that, if the base-\( a \) representation of \( n \) is \(\sum_{i=0}^{k_{1}} a_{i} a^{i}\), and the base-\( b \) representation of \( n \) is \(\sum_{i=0}^{k_{2}} b_{i} b^{i}\), then for all positive integers \( c > b \), we have that \(\sum_{i=0}^{k_{2}} b_{i} c^{i}\) divides \(\sum_{i=0}^{k_{1}} a_{i} c^{i}\). Find the number of non-divable \( n \) such that \( 1 \leq n \leq 100 \).
