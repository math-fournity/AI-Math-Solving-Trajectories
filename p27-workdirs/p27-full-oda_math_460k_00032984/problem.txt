Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ circumscribed around a circle $\omega$. Circle $\Omega$ passes through $A, C$ and is tangent to the incircles of triangles $ABC$ and $CDA$. Let $X$ be the point on line $AC$ such that there exists a homothety with center at $X$ taking $\omega$ to $\Omega$. If $AC = 10$ and the ratio of the radius of $\Omega$ to the radius of $\omega$ is $3:1$, find the length of segment $AX$.
