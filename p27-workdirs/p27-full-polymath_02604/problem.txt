Twelve musicians $M_1, \ldots, M_{12}$ gather at a week-long chamber music festival. Each day, there is one scheduled concert, and some of the musicians play while the others listen as members of the audience. For $i=1,2,\ldots,12$, let $t_i$ be the number of concerts in which musician $M_i$ plays, and let $t = t_1 + t_2 + \cdots + t_{12}$. Determine the minimum value of $t$ such that it is possible for each musician to listen, as a member of the audience, to all the other musicians.
