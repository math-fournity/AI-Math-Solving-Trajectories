Does there exist a continuous function \( F: [a, b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \) on a set \( [a, b] \subseteq \mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty, \infty\} \) such that the derivative \( F'(x) := \lim_{h\rightarrow 0}\frac{F(x+h)-F(x)}{h} \) exists in \( \mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty, \infty\} \) for every \( x \in (a, b) \) and the set \( \{F(x): x \in (a, b) \land |F'(x)|= \infty\} \) has positive Lebesgue outer measure?
