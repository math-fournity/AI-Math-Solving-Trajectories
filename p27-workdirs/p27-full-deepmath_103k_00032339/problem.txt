In a country where gold and platinum sand are valuable, gold can be exchanged for platinum and vice versa at an exchange rate determined by natural numbers $g$ and $p$. Specifically, $x$ grams of gold sand is equivalent to $y$ grams of platinum sand if $xp = yg$. Initially, the banker has one kilogram each of gold and platinum sand, with $g = p = 1001$. The state will decrease one of the numbers $g$ or $p$ by one each day, so that in 2000 days, both will become one. The sequence of decreases is unknown. Can the banker exchange the sand each day to ensure that in the end, he has at least 2 kg of each type of sand?
