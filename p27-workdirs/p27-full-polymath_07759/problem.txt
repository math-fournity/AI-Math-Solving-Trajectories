In the city of Aethelgard, two logistics managers, Alistair and Beatrice, are competing to manage a dwindling supply of rare crystals. They begin with a storage crate containing exactly $T$ crystals. On each turn, the active manager must remove a batch of crystals from the crate. The allowed batch sizes are fixed at either $a$ crystals or $b$ crystals, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. Alistair always takes the first turn, followed by Beatrice, and they continue alternating. The first manager who is forced to make the crystal count negative loses the game (meaning the last person to leave exactly zero or a positive number in the crate wins).
