Let $n \gg 1$ be a fixed number (e.g., $n=10^{10}$), and $k \gg 1$ a natural number. Let $a$ and $b$ be two permutations from the symmetric group $S_k$. Suppose that for every word $w(x,y)$ of length $\le n$, the permutation $w(a,b)$ has a fixed point. Is it true that every permutation in the group generated by $a$ and $b$, denoted $\langle a, b \rangle$, has a fixed point?
