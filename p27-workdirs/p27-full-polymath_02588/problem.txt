Let $n$ be a given positive integer. In the Cartesian plane, each lattice point with nonnegative coordinates initially contains a butterfly. The neighborhood of a lattice point $c$ consists of all lattice points within the axis-aligned $(2 n+1) \times (2 n+1)$ square centered at $c$, excluding $c$ itself. A butterfly is called lonely, crowded, or comfortable if the number of butterflies in its neighborhood $N$ is less than, greater than, or equal to half of the number of lattice points in $N$, respectively.
