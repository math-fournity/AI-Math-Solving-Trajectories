A specialized circular irrigation system is centered at a central hub $O$. Four monitoring sensors, $A, B, C$, and $D$, are positioned along the outer circular boundary $\Omega$. The straight-line distance between sensor $A$ and sensor $B$ is 12 meters, while the distance between sensor $A$ and sensor $D$ is 18 meters. The underground data cables connecting $A$ to $C$ and $B$ to $D$ are laid in straight lines that intersect at a perfect $90^\circ$ angle.
