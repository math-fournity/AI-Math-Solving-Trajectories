Consider triangle \(ABC\) with circumcircle \((O)\) and anti-median triangle \(A'B'C'\). Let \(A_1B_1C_1\) be the triangle formed by the tangents to circle \((O)\) at \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\). If \(P\) is the orthopole of the Euler line of triangle \(ABC\) with respect to triangle \(A_1B_1C_1\), find the ratio of the circumradius of triangle \(A'B'C'\) to the distance from \(P\) to the center \(O\) of the circumcircle \((O)\).
