Consider a triangle \( ABC \) with an arbitrary point \( D \) on side \( AB \). Let \( E \) be the interior point where \( CD \) intersects the external common tangent to the incircles of triangles \( ACD \) and \( BCD \). As \( D \) moves along \( AB \), the point \( E \) traces an arc of a circle. Find the radius of this circle if \( AB = 10 \), \( AC = 12 \), and \( BC = 14 \).
