A table with 2 rows and 13 columns is given. In the bottom row of the table, 13 pawns are placed, numbered sequentially from left to right with the numbers from 1 to 13. In one move, a pawn can be moved to an adjacent empty square. How many moves are needed at a minimum for the pawns to be arranged in the bottom row of the table, numbered sequentially from left to right with the numbers from 13 to 1?
