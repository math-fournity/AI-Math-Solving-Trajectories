In a specialized logistics network, a protocol $f$ maps each positive integer shipment ID to a new positive integer destination ID. A protocol is classified as "Synchronized" if it satisfies a strict balance requirement: for any two shipment IDs $x$ and $y$, the greatest common divisor of the destination of the destination of $x$, and the destination of the sum of $x$ and $y$, must be exactly equal to the greatest common divisor of the original IDs $x$ and $y$.
