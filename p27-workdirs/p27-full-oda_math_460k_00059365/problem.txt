Given a scalene triangle \(ABC\), let \(D\) and \(E\) be points on segments \(AB\) and \(AC\) respectively, such that \(CA = CD\) and \(BA = BE\). Let \(\omega\) be the circumcircle of triangle \(ADE\) and \(P\) be the reflection of \(A\) across \(BC\). Lines \(PD\) and \(PE\) meet \(\omega\) again at \(X\) and \(Y\) respectively. Let \(Z\) be the intersection of \(BX\) and \(CY\). If \(Z\) lies on \(\omega\), calculate the value of \(\frac{AZ}{AP}\).
