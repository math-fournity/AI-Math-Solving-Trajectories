Let \( p \geq 7 \) be a prime number, \( \zeta \) a primitive \( p \)th root of unity, and \( c \) a rational number. Consider the additive group generated by the numbers \( 1, \zeta, \zeta^2, \zeta^3 + \zeta^{-3} \). Calculate the number of distinct elements in this group whose norm in the \( p \)th cyclotomic field is equal to \( c \), given \( c = 1 \).
