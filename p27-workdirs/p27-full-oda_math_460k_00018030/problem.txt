Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with circumcircle \(\omega\). The tangent to \(\omega\) at \(A\) intersects \(BC\) at \(T\). Let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(BC\), and \(TS \perp AM\) where \(S \in AM\). Let \(TS \cap AD = X\). A circle \(\gamma\) with center \(X\) and radius \(XA\) intersects \(AD\) at \(Y\). Let \(J\) be a point on the minor arc \(AC\) such that \(JC = AB\). Let \(DJ \cap AM = R\). It is known that \(YR \parallel BC\). Let \(\omega \cap \gamma = Q\) other than \(A\). Let \(AQ \cap BC = K\) and \(AT \cap \gamma = P\) and \(RY \cap AK = U\). Calculate the value of \(\frac{PU}{PA}\).
