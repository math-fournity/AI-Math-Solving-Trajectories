In an election with 300 people voting for one of three candidates \(A\), \(B\), or \(C\), the first 200 voters gave 90 votes to \(A\), 60 votes to \(B\), 40 votes to \(C\), and 10 votes were invalid. To be elected in the first round, a candidate must have at least 127 votes. How many ways can the remaining 100 voters cast their votes to ensure that candidate \(A\) is elected in the first round?
