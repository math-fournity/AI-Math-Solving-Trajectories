Let $X$ and $Y$ be metric spaces, with $X$ being complete, and let $Z$ be a Hausdorff space. Consider functions $f, g: X \times Y \to Z$ such that both $f$ and $g$ are continuous in $x \in X$ for each fixed $y \in Y$, and continuous in $y \in Y$ for each fixed $x \in X$. If $f = g$ on a dense subset of $X \times Y$, prove or disprove that $f = g$ on the entire space $X \times Y$. \( \text{True or False?} \)
