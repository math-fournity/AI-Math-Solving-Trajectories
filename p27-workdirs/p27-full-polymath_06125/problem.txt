A master glassblower is commissioned to create a large rectangular stained-glass window. The client’s unique specification requires the window to be partitioned into exactly $n$ smaller rectangular panes, where $n$ is an integer greater than 1. To maintain aesthetic harmony, every individual pane must be a scaled version of the original window's dimensions (i.e., similar to the original rectangle). Furthermore, to ensure artistic variety, no two panes in the window can be identical in size; they must be pairwise noncongruent.
