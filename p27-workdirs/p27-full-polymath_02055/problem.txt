Consider a partition of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard into $p$ rectangles whose interiors are disjoint, such that each rectangle has an equal number of white and black cells. Let $a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_p$ denote the number of white cells in the $i$-th rectangle. Let $P$ be the maximum possible value of $p$, and let $S$ be the set of all possible sequences $(a_1, a_2, \dots, a_P)$ for this maximum value. Calculate the number of sequences in $S$ multiplied by the sum of all elements in all sequences in $S$.
