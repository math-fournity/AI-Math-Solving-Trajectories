Consider the second order gradient-like system with linear damping given by the equation \( \ddot{x}+\dot{x}+\nabla f(x)=0 \), where \( x(0)=x_0 \) and \( \dot{x}(0)=0 \). Suppose \( f\in C^2(\mathbb{R}^n) \) and \( \inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}^n}f(x)>-\infty \). The solution \( x:[0,\infty)\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^n \) is known to be bounded. If the parameter in front of the gradient term is changed from 1 to a positive constant \( g \), i.e., the equation becomes \( \ddot{x}+\dot{x}+g\nabla f(x)=0 \), is the solution to this new equation still bounded?
