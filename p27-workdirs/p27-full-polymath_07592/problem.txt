In a remote sector of the galaxy, a research station is protected by a security grid powered by 3 energy conduits. Each conduit can be tuned to exactly one of 8 distinct resonance frequencies. Under normal conditions, all three conduits must be set to their specific secret frequencies to activate the station. However, due to a recent solar flare, the security system’s sensitivity has dropped: the grid will now activate as long as at least two of the three conduits are tuned to their correct secret frequencies.
