Find all polynomials \(P(x)\) with integer coefficients and odd degree \(d\) such that for each positive integer \(n\), there exist \(n\) different positive integers \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\) such that for any pair of indices \(i, j\) \((1 \leq i, j \leq n)\), the inequality \(\frac{1}{2} < \frac{P(x_i)}{P(x_j)} < 2\) holds, and \(\frac{P(x_i)}{P(x)}\) is a rational number to the \(d\)-th power.
