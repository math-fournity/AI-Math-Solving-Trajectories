Find the largest value of \( n \) for which there exists a sequence \((a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n)\) of nonzero digits (that is, \(a_i \in \{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9\}\)) such that, for every \(k\), \(1 \leq k \leq n\), the \(k\)-digit number \((a_{k-1} a_{k-2} \ldots a_0) = a_{k-1} 10^{k-1} + a_{k-2} 10^{k-2} + \ldots + a_0\) divides the \((k+1)\)-digit number \((a_k a_{k-1} a_{k-2} \ldots a_0)\).
