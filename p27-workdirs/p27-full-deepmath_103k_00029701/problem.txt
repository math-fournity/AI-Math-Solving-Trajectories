In a one-dimensional lattice $\mathbb{Z}^1$, a thief starts at the origin (0) and $N$ policemen are initially positioned at 2. All participants perform independent random walks, where each step to the left or right occurs with equal probability (1/2). Let $\tau_N$ denote the first encounter time between the thief and a policeman. Given that $E\tau_1=\infty$, find the smallest $N$ for which $E\tau_N<\infty$. Provide your answer as a single integer.
