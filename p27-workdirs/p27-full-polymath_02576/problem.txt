Let \( n \geq 3 \) be an odd number. We play a game on an \( n \times n \) grid over \( n^2 \) turns. In each turn, we choose an empty square and write an integer from 1 to \( n^2 \) (each integer used exactly once). For each row and column containing the chosen square, if the sum of the integers in that row or column is a multiple of \( n \), we get 1 point (2 points if both row and column sums are multiples of \( n \)). Determine the maximum possible total points that can be obtained by the end of the game.
