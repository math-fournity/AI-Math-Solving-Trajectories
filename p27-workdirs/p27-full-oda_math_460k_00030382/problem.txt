Let \(ABC\) be a scalene triangle with incenter \(I\), circumcenter \(O\), and orthocenter \(H\), where \(OI \parallel BC\). The line through \(H\) parallel to \(BC\) intersects \(AO\) at \(T\). Let \(M\) and \(N\) be the midpoints of \(\overline{AH}\) and \(\overline{IT}\), respectively. Let \(K\) be the symmetric point of \(M\) with respect to \(N\). The tangent line at \(A\) to the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\) intersects \(OI\) at \(S\). \(BT\) and \(CT\) intersect \(AC\) and \(AB\) at \(E\) and \(F\), respectively. Calculate the ratio \(\frac{KS}{EF}\).
