Suppose $A \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ is a symmetric matrix with non-negative entries. The eigenvalues of $A$, denoted as $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots, \lambda_n$, satisfy $\lambda_1 > 0 \geq \lambda_2 \geq \dots \geq \lambda_n$. Determine if there exists another symmetric matrix $B \in M_n(\mathbb{R})$ with positive entries, such that the eigenvalues of $B$ are $\mu_1 > 0 > \mu_2 \geq \dots \geq \mu_n$ and the distance between $A$ and $B$ is small, i.e., $\sum_{i,j}(a_{ij}-b_{ij})^2<\epsilon$, where $\epsilon > 0$.
