In triangle \(ABC\) with incenter \(I\) and excenters \(I_a, I_b, I_c\) opposite to \(A, B, C\) respectively, let \(H_a\) and \(H_a'\) be the orthocenters of triangles \(IBC\) and \(I_aBC\). Define \(H_b, H_b', H_c, H_c'\) similarly. Let \(L_a\) be the line through \(H_a\) and \(H_a'\), and define \(L_b\) and \(L_c\) similarly. If \(P\) is the point of concurrency of \(L_a, L_b,\) and \(L_c\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{[PI_aI_bI_c]}{[ABC]}\).
