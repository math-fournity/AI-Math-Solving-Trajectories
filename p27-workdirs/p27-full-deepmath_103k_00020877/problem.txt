Suppose $X$ is a topological space where any two distinct points $x_0, x_1 \in X$ are connected by an immersive path, i.e., a locally homeomorphic embedding $\gamma: [0,1] \to X$ with $\gamma(0) = x_0$ and $\gamma(1) = x_1$. Does it follow that $X$ is arc-connected, meaning that every immersive path can be chosen to be a homeomorphic embedding? Assume $X$ is compact and covered by finitely many arcs. Additionally, consider if $X$ is Hausdorff, where path-connectedness implies arc-connectedness.
