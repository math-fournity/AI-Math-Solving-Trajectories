A team of civil engineers defines a bridge design as "perfectly balanced" if they can select a sequence of $n$ load-bearing pillars, where $n$ is a positive integer, such that the sequence of integer weight capacities $(a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n)$ assigned to these pillars satisfies a unique harmonic property: the sum of the capacities must exactly equal the product of the capacities, and both must be equal to the total number of pillars $n$.
