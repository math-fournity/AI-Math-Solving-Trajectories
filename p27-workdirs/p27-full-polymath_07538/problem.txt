An international logistics company operates a massive circular delivery route consisting of 77 distinct depots, labeled $D_1, D_2, \dots, D_{77}$ in order. The path between any two consecutive depots $D_i$ and $D_{i+1}$ is a perfectly straight road of length $a_i$ kilometers (where $D_{78} = D_1$). To ensure the route is as symmetrical as possible, the city planners mandated that the turning angle at every single depot must be identical.
