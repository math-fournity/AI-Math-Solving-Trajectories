Given triangle \(ABC\) and an arbitrary point \(P\). Let \(AP\), \(BP\), and \(CP\) intersect \(BC\), \(AC\), and \(AB\) at \(D\), \(E\), and \(F\) respectively. Let \(X\), \(Y\), and \(Z\) be the reflections of \(P\) with respect to \(EF\), \(FD\), and \(DE\) respectively. If \(AX\), \(BY\), and \(CZ\) are concurrent at a point \(Q\), find the ratio \(\frac{[XYZ]}{[ABC]}\), where \([XYZ]\) and \([ABC]\) denote the areas of triangles \(XYZ\) and \(ABC\) respectively.
