An international culinary institute is designing a master curriculum consisting of 8 distinct specialized stations. To earn a certification, a student must complete a specific sequence of 8 courses, one from each station. The number of available course options at each station is strictly regulated: Station 1 has 2 options, Station 2 has 3 options, Station 3 has 4 options, and so on, with Station $i$ offering exactly $i+1$ options (up to Station 8, which has 9 options).
