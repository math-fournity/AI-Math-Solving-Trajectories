Let  $EF$  meet  $(O)$  at  $M,N$ . The line that is perpendicular to  $PM$ ,  $PN$  at  $M,N$  respectively intersect  $(O)$  at  $S, T$  different from  $M,N$ . Prove that  $ST \parallel BC$ .
