Olya wrote fractions of the form \( \frac{1}{n} \) on cards, where \( n \) includes all possible divisors of \( 6^{100} \). She then arranged these cards in some order. After that, she wrote on the board the number on the first card, then the sum of the numbers on the first and second cards, then the sum of the numbers on the first three cards, and so on, finally the sum of the numbers on all the cards. Each sum Olya wrote on the board as an irreducible fraction. What is the minimum number of different denominators that could appear on the board?
