On each cell of a $2005 \times 2005$ square table, there is one token. In each turn, it is allowed to remove a token from the table if at that moment the number of its neighboring tokens is even and greater than 0. By neighboring tokens, we mean tokens that are in cells with at least one common vertex (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal). Let $n$ be the smallest possible number of tokens to which the table can be reduced. Find $n$.
