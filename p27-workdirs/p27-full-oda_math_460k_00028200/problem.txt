Let \( c \ge 1 \) be a real number. Let \( G \) be an Abelian group and let \( A \subset G \) be a finite set satisfying \( |A+A| \le c|A| \), where \( X+Y := \{x+y \mid x \in X, y \in Y\} \) and \( |Z| \) denotes the cardinality of \( Z \). Calculate the maximum value of \( \frac{|\underbrace{A+A+\dots+A}_5|}{|A|} \).
