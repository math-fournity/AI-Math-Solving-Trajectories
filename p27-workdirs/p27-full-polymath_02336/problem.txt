Given \( n \) coins of pairwise different weights and \( n \) balance scales, \( n > 2 \). In each weighing, you may choose any one scale, place one coin on each of its pans, observe the reading, and then remove the coins. Some of the scales (unknown which) are broken and can give random results, both correct and incorrect. What is the minimum number of weighings needed to definitely find the heaviest coin?
