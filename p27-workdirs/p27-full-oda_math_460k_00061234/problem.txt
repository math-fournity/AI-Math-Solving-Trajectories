A parabola $y=ax^2+bx+c$ is rolled along a straight board $PQ$. The rolling occurs in such a way that at each point of contact the slope of the parabola equals the slope of the line passing through the points of contact of the two branches of the parabola. If the starting point $A$ of the parabola is $(0,1)$ and the initial slope at this point is $0$, determine the equations of the trajectories of the vertices of the parabolas. The parabola starts rolling at the vertex, i.e., at the point $(0,1)$.
