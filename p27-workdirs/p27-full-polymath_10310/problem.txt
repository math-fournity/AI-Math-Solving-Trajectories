A specialized logistics warehouse contains 33 shipping containers, each assigned a unique identification code from the set $\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 33\}$. The warehouse manager executes a consolidation protocol: she selects a group of at least two containers such that the product of their identification codes is a perfect square. These containers are then merged into a single new container, which is assigned an identification code equal to the square root of that product.
