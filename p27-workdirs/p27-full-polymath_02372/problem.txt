Given a prime \( p \) and positive integer \( k \), an integer \( n \) with \( 0 \leq n < p \) is called a \((p, k)\)-Hofstadterian residue if there exists an infinite sequence of integers \( n_{0}, n_{1}, n_{2}, \ldots \) such that \( n_{0} \equiv n \) and \( n_{i+1}^{k} \equiv n_{i} \pmod{p} \) for all integers \( i \geq 0 \). If \( f(p, k) \) is the number of \((p, k)\)-Hofstadterian residues, then compute \(\sum_{k=1}^{2016} f(2017, k)\).
