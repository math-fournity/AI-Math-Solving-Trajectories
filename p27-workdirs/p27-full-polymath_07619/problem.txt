In the remote logistics hub of Delta-Region, three primary outposts—Alpha ($A$), Bravo ($B$), and Charlie ($C$)—form a triangular supply network. A central circular depot, Sector $\omega$, is positioned such that it is equidistant from the supply roads connecting $A$ to $B$ and $A$ to $C$. The center of this depot is designated as Command Post $I$. Sector $\omega$ is tangent to the road $CA$ at checkpoint $E$ and tangent to the road $AB$ at checkpoint $F$.
