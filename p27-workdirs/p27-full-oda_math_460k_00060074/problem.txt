Let \( n \geq 2 \) be an integer. There are \( n \) distinct circles in general position, meaning any two circles intersect in two distinct points and no three circles intersect at the same point. These circles divide the plane into regions bounded by circular arcs, with each circle having exactly \( 2n-2 \) vertices. Each vertex is temporarily colored alternately black and white for each circle passing through it. If the two temporary colorings of a vertex coincide, the vertex is definitively colored with that common color; otherwise, it is colored gray. Let \( G(n) \) be the minimum number of gray points on a circle such that all vertices of some region are gray. Find \( G(10) \).
