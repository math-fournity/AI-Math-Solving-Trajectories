In a remote desert, two survey teams are mapping a region shaped like a quadrilateral kite, $ABCD$. The primary base camp is at vertex $B$. Two outposts, $A$ and $C$, are established such that the distance from the base camp to $A$ is exactly the same as the distance from the base camp to $C$ ($AB = BC$). Two further research stations are positioned at $D$, such that the distance from $A$ to $D$ is identical to the distance from $C$ to $D$ ($AD = CD$).
