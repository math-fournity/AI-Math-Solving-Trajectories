Given an acute triangle \(ABC\) with orthocenter \(H\), let \(D_1\) and \(D_2\) be two isogonal points with respect to \(ABC\). Let \(A_1B_1C_1\) and \(A_2B_2C_2\) be the pedal triangles of \(D_1\) and \(D_2\) with respect to \(ABC\). Define points \(U\) and \(V\) as the intersections of \(AA_1\) and \(CC_1\), and \(BB_2\) and \(CC_2\), respectively. Let \(Z\) be the intersection of \(D_1U\) and \(D_2V\). Points \(X\) and \(Y\) are defined similarly. Determine the ratio of the area of triangle \(XYZ\) to the area of triangle \(ABC\).
