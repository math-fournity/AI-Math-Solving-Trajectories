In a futuristic data center, a security encryption protocol uses a secret polynomial $P$ with integer coefficients to manage access levels. Two distinct groups of servers, the "Alpha Nodes" and the "Beta Nodes," are identified by unique integer IDs. There are $k$ distinct Alpha Nodes, with IDs $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_k$, and $n$ distinct Beta Nodes, with IDs $y_1, y_2, \dots, y_n$. All $k+n$ IDs are unique integers.
