Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with \(B\) and \(C\) fixed. Let \(D\) be the midpoint of \(BC\) and \(E, F\) be the feet of the perpendiculars from \(D\) to \(AB\) and \(AC\), respectively. Let \(O\) be the circumcenter of triangle \(ABC\) and \(M = EF \cap AO\), \(N = EF \cap BC\). If the circumcircle of triangle \(AMN\) passes through a fixed point \(P\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{OP}{OD}\).
