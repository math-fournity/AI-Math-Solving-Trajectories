Let \(ABC\) be a triangle inscribed in circle \((O)\). Bisectors \(BE\) and \(CF\) intersect at \(I\) and cut \((O)\) again at \(M\) and \(N\). Let \(K\) and \(L\) be the incenters of triangles \(CME\) and \(BNF\), respectively. Points \(P\) and \(Q\) are the reflections of \(B\) and \(C\) through \(KL\). Suppose circles \((IPN)\) and \((IQM)\) intersect at a point \(R\) on \((O)\). Calculate the value of \(\angle PRQ - \frac{\angle BAC}{4}\).
