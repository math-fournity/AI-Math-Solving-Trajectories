The inscribed circle of triangle ABC touches its sides at points  $A_1$ ,  $B_1,$    $C_1$ , and the exscribed circles touch its sides at points  $A_2$ ,  $B_2$ ,  $C_2$  respectively. Circle  $a_1$  passes through points  $A$ ,  $B_1$  and  $C_1$ , and circle  $a_2$  passes through  $A$ ,  $B_2$  and  $C_2$ . These circles intersect for the second time at point  $A_3$ . Circles  $b_1$ ,  $b_2$  and  $c_1$ ,  $c_2$ , as well as points  $B_3$  and  $C_3$ , are constructed similarly. Prove that lines  $AA_3$ ,  $BB_3$ ,  $CC_3$  are parallel.
