Given an acute triangle \(ABC\) with \(AB < AC\), let \(H\) be the orthocenter, \(PQR\) the orthic triangle, \((O)\) the circumcircle, \((Oa)\) the A-Carnot’s circle (which passes through \(B\), \(H\), and \(C\)), and \((N)\) the nine-point circle. Let \(A^*\) be the intersection point of \((Oa)\) and \((N)\) near \(B\), and \(S\) be the intersection point of \(RQ\) and \((O)\) in the order \(R\), \(Q\), \(S\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{HS}{HA^*}\).
