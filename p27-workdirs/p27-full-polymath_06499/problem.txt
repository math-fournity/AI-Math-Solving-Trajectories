In a specialized physics laboratory, researchers are studying "Quantum Particles" that occupy discrete energy states on a one-dimensional energy spectrum. A "Stable Configuration" is defined as a finite set $M$ of at least two distinct energy states such that for any two different states $a$ and $b$ in $M$, the resulting interaction energy calculated by the formula $a^3 - \frac{4}{9}b$ is also an energy state present within the same set $M$.
