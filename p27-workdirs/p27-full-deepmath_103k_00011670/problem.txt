Consider a connected, planar, directed graph G where each vertex has either degree 1 or 3. At each degree 3 vertex, either (1) exactly two of the three adjacent edges are oriented inwards and the third edge points outwards, or (2) two edges point outwards and one inwards. Assign a label of 1 or 2 to each degree 3 vertex based on which case applies. If such a labeling exists such that no two adjacent vertices have the same label, this provides a 2-coloring of G. Is it true that if such a graph G is 2-colorable, then there exists a 2-coloring that arises in this way?
