Consider all ordered pairs $(a, b)$ of positive integers such that $\frac{a^2 + b^2 + 2}{ab}$ is an integer and $a\le b$. We label all such pairs as $P_1, P_2, P_3, \dots$ in increasing order of their distance from the origin. It is given that no two pairs have the same distance. Thus $P_1 = (1, 1)$, $P_2 = (1, 3)$, $P_3 = (3, 7)$, and so on. If $P_{2020} = (x, y)$, compute the remainder when $x + y$ is divided by $2017$.
