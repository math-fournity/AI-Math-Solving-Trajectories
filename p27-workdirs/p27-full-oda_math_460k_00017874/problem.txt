Let \(ABCDEF\) be a hexagon inscribed in a conic. Define the points \(P = AE \cap BD\), \(Q = AC \cap FD\), and \(R = FB \cap EC\). Let \(X = PQ \cap AD\), \(Y = QR \cap FC\), and \(Z = PR \cap EB\). If the coordinates of \(X\), \(Y\), and \(Z\) in barycentric coordinates are \((x_1, y_1, z_1)\), \((x_2, y_2, z_2)\), and \((x_3, y_3, z_3)\) respectively, find the value of \(\frac{x_1y_2z_3}{x_2y_3z_1} + \frac{x_2y_3z_1}{x_3y_1z_2} + \frac{x_3y_1z_2}{x_1y_2z_3}\).
