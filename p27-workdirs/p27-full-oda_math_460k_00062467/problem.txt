Given $\triangle ABC$ with arbitrary isogonal conjugate points $(P, P^*)$. Let the circumcevian triangle and pedal triangle of $P$ with respect to $\triangle ABC$ be $\triangle A_1B_1C_1$ and $\triangle A_2B_2C_2$, respectively. If the ratio of similarity between $\triangle A_1B_1C_1 \cup P^*$ and $\triangle A_2B_2C_2 \cup P^*$ is denoted as $k$, find $k^2$ when $P$ is the orthocenter of $\triangle ABC$.
