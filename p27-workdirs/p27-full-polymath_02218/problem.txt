The mouse Jerry decided to give the cat Tom a pie in the shape of a square $8 \times 8$. In three pieces marked with the letter "R", he put fish, in two pieces marked with the letter "K", he put sausage, and in one piece, he added both, but that piece was not marked (all other pieces are unfilled). Jerry also informed Tom that in any $6 \times 6$ square, there are at least $2$ pieces with fish, and in any $3 \times 3$ square, there is at most one piece with sausage.
