Consider a natural number \( N \) that can be expressed as \( N = a_1 - a_2 = b_1 - b_2 = c_1 - c_2 = d_1 - d_2 \), where \( a_1 \) and \( a_2 \) are squares, \( b_1 \) and \( b_2 \) are cubes, \( c_1 \) and \( c_2 \) are fifth powers, and \( d_1 \) and \( d_2 \) are seventh powers of natural numbers. Determine if it is necessarily true that among the numbers \( a_1, b_1, c_1, \) and \( d_1 \), there will be at least two equal ones.
