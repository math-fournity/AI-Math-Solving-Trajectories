Let $S$ be a smooth projective surface over $\mathbb{C}$ with Picard number $\rho(S) = 1$, and let $L$ be an ample line bundle on $S$. For a positive integer $d$ such that $dL$ is very ample, consider a very general member $D$ of the linear system $|dL|$. Let $T_D \rightarrow S$ be the degree $d$ cyclic cover branched along $D$. Is the Picard number $\rho(T_D)$ equal to 1 for a sufficiently large integer $d$?
