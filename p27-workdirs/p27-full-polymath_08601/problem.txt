A specialized satellite command center is housed within a massive architectural structure shaped like a regular dodecahedron. A maintenance drone is currently docked at a power port located at vertex \(P_{1}\) of a specific pentagonal exterior panel. This panel is defined by its five corner vertices \(P_{1}, P_{2}, P_{3}, P_{4},\) and \(P_{5}\). The total length of the perimeter of this individual pentagonal panel is exactly 5 units.
