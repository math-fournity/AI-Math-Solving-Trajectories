Consider a velocity field $v \in \mathcal{C}^1(\mathbb{R}^n,\mathbb{R}^n)$ for $n \geq 1$, where every solution $(x_t)_{t \geq 0}$ of the differential equation \( \frac{d}{dt}x_t = v(x_t) \) is periodic. For a non-stationary point $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ (i.e., $v(x) \neq 0$), let $T(x)$ denote the period of the solution $(x_t)_{t \geq 0}$ with initial condition $x(0) = x$. Is the function $T$ continuous over the set of non-stationary points?
