A high-security vault is protected by a sequence of nine digital keys, represented by positive integers $a_{1}, a_{2}, \dots, a_{9}$. These keys are not necessarily distinct. The vault’s safety protocol dictates a strict "Quartet Sum Rule": for every possible combination of three distinct keys chosen from the sequence (indices $i, j, k$ such that $1 \le i < j < k \le 9$), there must exist a fourth key in the sequence (index $l$, where $1 \le l \le 9$ and $l$ is not equal to $i, j,$ or $k$) such that the sum of the values of these four keys is exactly 100.
