In a specialized laboratory, an automated storage system manages 32 chemical canisters. These canisters are organized into three primary cooling racks—the Crimson Rack, the Saffron Rack, and the Azure Rack—each containing 10 canisters labeled with potency levels $k = 1, 2, \dots, 10$. Additionally, there are two auxiliary stabilizer tanks, the "Alpha Stabilizer" and the "Beta Stabilizer," both assigned a potency level of $k = 0$.
