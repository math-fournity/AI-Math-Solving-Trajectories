Given an angle \(0^\circ < \phi \le 180^\circ\) and a circular disc, an ant starts from an interior point and travels in a straight line. Upon reaching the edge, it turns clockwise by the angle \(\phi\). If the new direction does not point towards the interior, it continues turning by \(\phi\) until it faces the interior. The ant then continues in this new direction and repeats the process. For what values of \(\phi\) will the ant eventually return to its starting position for any starting point and initial direction?
