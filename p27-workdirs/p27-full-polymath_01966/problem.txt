For $0<q<1$, a $q$-Fibonacci spiral is constructed as follows: an arc of radius 1 is first drawn inside a $1 \times 1$ square. A second arc is drawn in a $q \times q$ square, then a third in a $q^{2} \times q^{2}$ square, and so on ad infinitum, to create a continuous curve. Let the first square have its bottom-left corner at $(0,0)$ and its top-right corner at $(1,1)$. Let $P_q$ be the limiting endpoint of the $q$-Fibonacci spiral. Calculate the distance from $P_q$ to the center of the initial $1 \times 1$ square.
