In an acute triangle \(ABC\) with incenter \(I\), a line through \(I\) parallel to \(AC\) intersects \(AB\) at \(M\), and a line through \(I\) parallel to \(AB\) intersects \(AC\) at \(N\). The line \(MN\) intersects the circumcircle \(\gamma\) of \(\Delta ABC\) at points \(X\) and \(Y\). Let \(Z\) be the midpoint of the arc \(BC\) of \(\gamma\) that does not contain \(A\). If the circumradius of \(\Delta XYZ\) is \(R\), find the value of \(\frac{R}{OI}\), where \(O\) is the circumcenter of \(\Delta ABC\).
