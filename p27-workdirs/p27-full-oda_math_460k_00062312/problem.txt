Consider the set \( A \) of all polynomials \( f(x) \) of order 3 with integer coefficients and cubic coefficient 1, such that for every \( f(x) \) there exists a prime number \( p \) which does not divide 2004 and a number \( q \) which is coprime to \( p \) and 2004, so that \( f(p) = 2004 \) and \( f(q) = 0 \). Let \( B \subset A \) be an infinite subset such that the function graphs of the members of \( B \) are identical except for translations. Find the smallest possible value of \( k \) such that there exist \( k \) distinct polynomials in \( B \) with distinct constant terms.
