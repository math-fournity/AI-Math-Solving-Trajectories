Let \( G \) be an abelian group with a collection of subgroups \( H_i \) for \( i \in \mathbb{Z} \), satisfying the following conditions: Each \( H_i \) is a complete topological group, \( H_i \supset H_{i+1} \), \( G = \bigcup_{i \in \mathbb{Z}} H_i \), and \( 0 = \bigcap_{i \in \mathbb{Z}} H_i \). The direct limit topology is defined on \( G \) such that a subset is open if its intersection with each \( H_i \) is open. Determine if every open neighborhood of \( 0 \) in \( G \) necessarily contains some \( H_i \).
