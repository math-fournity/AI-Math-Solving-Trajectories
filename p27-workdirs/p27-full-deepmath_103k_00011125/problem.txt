Let $G = P \ltimes Q$ be a finite group where $P$ is a maximal subgroup of $G$, non-normal, cyclic, and satisfies $P = C_G(P) = N_G(P)$. The derived subgroup $G' = Q \cong C_q \times C_q \times C_q$, with $p$ and $q$ being distinct primes. Assume that for any two distinct non-normal non-cyclic subgroups $H$ and $K$ of $G$, they are conjugate in $G$. There exists a homomorphism $\varphi : P \to \text{Aut}(Q) \cong GL(3,q)$, and $\text{Ker}(\varphi) = C_P(Q) = Z(G)$. Prove that $P \cong C_p$, where $p = q^2 + q + 1$ is a prime.
