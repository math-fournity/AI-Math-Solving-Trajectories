Consider a triangle \(ABC\) and a circle \(\omega_A\) through \(BC\) and tangent to the incircle of \(ABC\). Let \(A'B'C'\) be the points where the incircle of \(ABC\) touches its sides. Let \(\omega_A\) meet \(AB\) and \(AC\) at \(P\) and \(Q\). Let \(I_b\), \(I_c\), and \(J_a\) be the incenters of \(\triangle BPQ\), \(\triangle CPQ\), and the \(A\)-excenter of \(\triangle APQ\), respectively. Calculate the ratio of similarity between \(\triangle I_bI_cJ_a\) and \(\triangle A'B'C'\).
