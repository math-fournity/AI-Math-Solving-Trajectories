In the plane Cartesian coordinate system, consider the hyperbola \(\Gamma: \frac{x^{2}}{3} - y^{2} = 1\). For any point \(P\) that does not lie on \(\Gamma\), let \(\Omega_P\) be the set of all lines passing through \(P\) and intersecting \(\Gamma\) at two points. For any line \(l \in \Omega_P\), let \(M\) and \(N\) be the two intersection points of \(l\) and \(\Gamma\). Define \(f_{P}(l) = |PM| \cdot |PN|\). If there exists a line \(l_0 \in \Omega_P\) such that the two intersection points of \(l_0\) with \(\Gamma\) lie on opposite sides of the \(y\)-axis, and for any line \(l \in \Omega_P, l \neq l_0\), we have \(f_{P}(l) > f_{P}(l_0)\), then \(P\) is called a "good point". Find the area of the region formed by all good points.
