Given \( M \), \( 1 < M < \infty \), let \( S^*(M) \) be the class of starlike univalent functions \( f \) in \( \mathbb{D} \) with \( f(0) = 0 \), \( f'(0) = 1 \), and \( |f(z)| \leq M \) for \( |z| < 1 \). If \( f(z) = z + \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} a_n z^n \), find \(\sup\{|a_3| : f \in S^*(M)\}\) for \( \varepsilon < M < 5 \). Barnard and Lewis [78] have found the supremum for other values of \( M \).
