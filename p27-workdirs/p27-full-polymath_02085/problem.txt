Two persons $A$ and $B$ play a game with two identical sets of nine cards, each containing the numbers $\{2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10\}$. $A$ starts by placing one of his cards on the table, then $B$ places one of his, and they continue alternating. The game ends when the sum of all cards on the table exceeds 99. The player who makes the sum greater than 99 loses.
