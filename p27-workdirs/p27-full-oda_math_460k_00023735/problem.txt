Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with circumcenter \(O\). Let \((J)\) be the circle through \(B\) and \(C\) that intersects \(AB\) and \(AC\) at \(D\) and \(E\), respectively. Let \(BE \cap CD = M\). The circumcircle of triangle \(JDE\) intersects the opposite ray of \(MA\) at \(N\). The circumcircle \((O)\) intersects \(DE\) at \(F\) and \(G\) (such that \(D \in EF\)). \(JF\) intersects \(MG\) at \(X\). If \(R\) is the radius of the circumcircle \((JDE)\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{JX}{RX}\).
