Let \((E,d)\) be a metric space, \(\mathscr E\) be its Borel \(\sigma\)-algebra, and \(\mu\) be a \(\sigma\)-finite measure on \((E,\mathscr E)\). Consider a non-negative function \(p:E\times E\to\mathbb R_+\) that is jointly measurable: \(p\in\mathscr E\otimes \mathscr E\). Assume that for any compact set \(A\subset E\), there exists a constant \(\lambda_A\) such that \( |p(x'',y) - p(x',y)|\leq \lambda_A\cdot d(x',x'')\) for all \(x',x''\in A, y\in E\). Also assume that \( \int\limits_E p(x,y)\mu(\mathrm dy) = 1 \) for all \(x\in E\). If \(A\) is compact, then \(P(x,B):=\int\limits_B p(x,y)\mu(\mathrm dy)\) is Lipschitz on \(A\) whenever \(\mu(B)<\infty\): \( |P(x'',B) - P(x',B)|\leq \lambda_A\cdot\mu(B)d(x'',x')\). Does the Lipschitz continuity of \(P(x,B)\) also hold on \(A\) if \(\mu(B)=\infty\)?
