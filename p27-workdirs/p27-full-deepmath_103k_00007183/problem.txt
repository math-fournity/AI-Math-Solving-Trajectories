In a permutation search game, the natural numbers $1$ through $n$ are arranged in a random order, which is unknown and has a uniform distribution. You make a sequence of guesses, one number and one slot at a time, and are told after each guess whether it is correct. The game ends when the order of the $n$ numbers is determined. Is there a strategy that takes fewer guesses than the trivial elimination in both the worst case and the average case?
