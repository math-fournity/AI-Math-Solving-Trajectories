Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with orthocenter \(H\) and incenter \(I\). Points \(A_1, A_2, B_1, B_2, C_1, C_2\) are on the rays \(AB, AC, BC, CA, CB\) respectively such that \(AA_1 = AA_2 = BC\), \(BB_1 = BB_2 = CA\), and \(CC_1 = CC_2 = AB\). Suppose that \(B_1B_2\) intersects \(C_1C_2\) at \(A'\), \(C_1C_2\) intersects \(A_1A_2\) at \(B'\), and \(A_1A_2\) intersects \(B_1B_2\) at \(C'\). If the area of triangle \(ABC\) is \(K\), find the maximum possible value of the ratio \(\frac{\text{Area of } \triangle A'B'C'}{K}\).
