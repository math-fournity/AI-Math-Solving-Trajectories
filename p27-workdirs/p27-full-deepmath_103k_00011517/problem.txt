Let $D \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be the closed unit disk with boundary $\mathbb{S}^1$. Suppose $a, b \in \mathbb{S}^1$ are two distinct points, and $A, B \subset D$ are two disjoint closed sets such that $A \cap \mathbb{S}^1 = \{a\}$ and $B \cap \mathbb{S}^1 = \{b\}$. Determine whether any distinct points $x, y \in \mathbb{S}^1 \setminus \{a, b\}$ can be connected by a continuous path lying entirely in $D \setminus (A \cup B)$. In other words, does there exist a continuous function $f: [0, 1] \to D \setminus (A \cup B)$ such that $f(0) = x$ and $f(1) = y$?
