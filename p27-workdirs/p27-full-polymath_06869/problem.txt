A secret government encryption protocol relies on a prime number $p$ and a specialized "efficiency function" $P(x)$, which is a monic polynomial of the smallest possible degree. The protocol's stability is governed by a recurrence relation across its first $p-1$ security tiers. Specifically, for every integer tier $k$ from $1$ up to $p-1$, the ratio of the efficiency at tier $k$ to the efficiency at tier $k+1$ is exactly equal to the ratio of $k$ to $k^2 + 1$.
