Given a triangle \( T[0] = ABC \), and by induction, \( T[n+1] \) is the contact triangle of the incircle of \( T[n] \). The circumradius of \( T[n+1] \) is at most half the circumradius of \( T[n] \). Determine the point to which the vertices of \( T[n] \) converge and whether this point has rational, algebraic, or analytic barycentric coordinates in terms of the side lengths \( a, b, c \) or the angles \( A, B, C \) of the original triangle \( ABC \).
