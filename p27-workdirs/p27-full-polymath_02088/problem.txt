Let $S_n$ be the set of sequences of length $n$ with terms from $\{-1, +1\}$. Define the function $f: S_n \setminus \{(-1, 1, 1, \dots, 1)\} \to S_n$ as follows: if $(a_1, \dots, a_n) \in S_n \setminus \{(-1, 1, 1, \dots, 1)\}$ and $k = \max \{j \in \{1, \dots, n\} : a_1 a_2 \dots a_j = 1\}$, then $f(a_1, \dots, a_n) = (a_1, \dots, a_{k-1}, -a_k, a_{k+1}, \dots, a_n)$. Let $f^{(m)}$ denote the $m$-fold iteration of $f$. Find the value of $m$ such that $f^{(m)}(1, 1, \dots, 1) = (-1, 1, \dots, 1)$ for a sequence of length $n = 10$.
