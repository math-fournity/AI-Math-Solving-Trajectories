Let \( f(x) = x^2 - \frac{3}{4} \). Let \( f^{(n)}(x) \) denote the composition of \( f \) with itself \( n \) times. Let \( R \) be the set of complex numbers that is the union of the roots of the polynomials \( f^{(n)}(x^2 + \frac{3}{4}) \) over positive integers \( n \). Let \( B \) be the smallest rectangle in the complex plane with sides parallel to the real and imaginary axes that contains \( R \). What is the square of the area of \( B \)?
