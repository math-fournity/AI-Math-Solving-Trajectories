Let \(ABCD\) be a trapezoid with \(AD \parallel BC\) and \(\angle BCD < \angle ABC < 90^\circ\). Let \(E\) be the intersection point of the diagonals \(AC\) and \(BD\). The circumcircle \(\omega\) of \(\triangle BEC\) intersects the segment \(CD\) at \(X\). The lines \(AX\) and \(BC\) intersect at \(Y\), while the lines \(BX\) and \(AD\) intersect at \(Z\). If the line \(EZ\) is tangent to \(\omega\) and the line \(BE\) is tangent to the circumcircle of \(\triangle BXY\), find the value of \(\frac{EZ}{BE}\).
