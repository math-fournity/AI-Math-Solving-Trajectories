If $A \subseteq \mathcal{N} \times \mathcal{N}$ is a $\Delta_{1}^{1}$ set (i.e., both $A$ and its complement are $\Sigma_{1}^{1}$) and all sections $A_{x} = \{y : (x, y) \in A\}$ are compact, is the projection $\pi(A) = \{x : \exists y (x, y) \in A\}$ also $\Delta_{1}^{1}$? Further, does there exist a $\Delta_{1}^{1}$ uniformization of $A$ (i.e., a $\Delta_{1}^{1}$ subset of $A$ that intersects each non-empty section $A_{x}$ in exactly one point)?
