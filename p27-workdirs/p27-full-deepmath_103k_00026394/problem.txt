In the $n$-dimensional non-negative orthant $\mathbb{R}_+^n$ with $n \geq 1$, consider an $(n-1)$-simplex $S$ defined by \[ S = \left\{x \in \mathbb{R}_+^n : \sum_{i=1}^n x_i = 1\right\} \] and its relative interior $\mathring{S}$. Let $E$ be a linear subspace of dimension $n-2$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ such that $E \cap \mathring{S} \neq \emptyset$. The intersection $E \cap \mathring{S}$ is a convex polyhedron with a relative interior of dimension $n-3$ and has at least $n-2$ extremal points. Determine the maximum possible number of extremal points for $E \cap S$.
