Let \( n \) and \( k \) be positive integers satisfying \( k \leq 2n^2 \). Lee and Sunny play a game with a \( 2n \times 2n \) grid paper. Lee writes a non-negative real number no greater than 1 in each cell such that the sum of all numbers on the paper is \( k \). Sunny then divides the paper into pieces, each consisting of several complete and connected cells, such that the sum of all numbers on each piece is at most 1. Let \( M \) be the number of pieces. Lee wants to maximize \( M \), while Sunny wants to minimize \( M \). Find the value of \( M \) when both play optimally.
