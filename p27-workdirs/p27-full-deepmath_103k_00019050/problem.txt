Let $\Bbbk$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>0$. Let $G$ be a connected affine algebraic group over $\Bbbk$, and write $\Bbbk[G]$ for the coordinate ring of $G$. Let $I\subseteq\Bbbk[G]$ denote the nilradical of $\Bbbk[G]$. Determine if it is true that if $f\in\Bbbk[G]\setminus I$, then $f$ is not a zero divisor in $\Bbbk[G]$. Provide a proof or counterexample.
