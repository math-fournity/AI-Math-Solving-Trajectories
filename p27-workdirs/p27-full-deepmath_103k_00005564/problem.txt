Let \( f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \) be a function such that \( f \in L^1[-a,a] \) for all \( a > 0 \), and define \( g(x) = \lim_{r \to \infty} \frac{1}{r} \int_{x-r}^{x+r} f(t) \, dt \) for every \( x \in \mathbb{R} \). Is it true that \( g \) is an affine map, i.e., \( g(x) - g(0) = x(g(1) - g(0)) \) for all \( x \in \mathbb{R} \)?
