Let \( n \) be a fixed positive integer, and let \( S \) be the set of points \((x, y)\) on the Cartesian plane such that both coordinates \( x \) and \( y \) are nonnegative integers smaller than \( 2n \) (thus \(|S| = 4n^2\)). Assume that \(\mathcal{F}\) is a set consisting of \( n^2 \) quadrilaterals such that all their vertices lie in \( S \), and each point in \( S \) is a vertex of exactly one of the quadrilaterals in \(\mathcal{F}\).
