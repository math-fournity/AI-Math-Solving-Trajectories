Consider the differential equation \( \frac{d^2}{dx^2}w + \{u(x) + k^2\}w = 0 \) with the asymptotic condition \( \lim_{x\rightarrow \infty}w(x)e^{ikx}=1 \). Given that \( u \in L^1_1(\mathbb{R}) = \{f: \int_\mathbb{R}(1+|x|)|f|dx<\infty\} \) and \( k > 0 \), determine if the solution \( w \) is unique.
