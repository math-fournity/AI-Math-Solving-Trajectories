Let $H_n = 1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \dots + \frac{1}{n} = \frac{p_n}{q_n}$ be the $n$-th harmonic number, where $p_n$ and $q_n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Let $S$ be the set of all positive integers $n < 125$ such that the denominator $q_n$ is not divisible by 5. Find the sum of the elements in $S$.
