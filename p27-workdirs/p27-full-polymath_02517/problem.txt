Let \( \triangle ABC \) be a triangle with symmedian point \( K \), and let \(\theta = \angle AKB - 90^\circ\). Suppose that \(\theta\) is both positive and less than \(\angle C\). Consider a point \( K' \) inside \(\triangle ABC\) such that \( A, K', K, \) and \( B \) are concyclic and \(\angle K'CB = \theta\). Consider another point \( P \) inside \(\triangle ABC\) such that \( K'P \perp BC \) and \(\angle PCA = \theta\).
