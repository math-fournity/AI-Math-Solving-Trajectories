Let \( f \) and \( g \) be two analytic real functions defined on \( (0,1) \). Define the curve \( \gamma : (0,1) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^3 \) such that \( \gamma(x) = (x, f(x), g(x)) \). Consider \( \Omega \) the union of all possible lines passing through each pair of different points of \( \gamma \). Suppose \( \gamma \) is not contained in a plane. Calculate the volume of the smallest open cube in \( \mathbb{R}^3 \) that is guaranteed to be contained in \( \Omega \).
