Joy has a square board of size $n \times n$. At every step, he colours a cell of the board, ensuring no cell is coloured more than once. He starts with $0$ points. After colouring a cell $c$, he takes the largest possible set $S$ that forms a "$+$" sign where all cells are coloured and $c$ lies in the centre. He earns the size of set $S$ as points. After colouring the entire $n \times n$ board, what is the maximum possible amount of points he can get?
