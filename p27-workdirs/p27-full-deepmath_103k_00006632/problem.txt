Determine whether there exists a Banach space $X$, along with elements $x_i \in X$ and radii $r_i > 0$ indexed by some set $I$, possibly infinite, such that for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the intersection \( \bigcap_{i \in I} B(x_i, (1+\tfrac{1}{n})r_i) \neq \varnothing \) holds, but \( \bigcap_{i \in I} B(x_i, r_i) = \varnothing \). Here, $B(x,r)$ denotes the closed ball centered at $x$ with radius $r$. Note that $X$ cannot be isometric to a dual space.
