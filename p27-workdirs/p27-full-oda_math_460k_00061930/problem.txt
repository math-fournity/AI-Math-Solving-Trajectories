Let \((O)\) and \((I)\) be the circumcircle and incircle of triangle \(ABC\). The B-mixtilinear incircle contacts \(AB\) and \((O)\) at \(X\) and \(P\) respectively, and the C-mixtilinear incircle contacts \(AC\) and \((O)\) at \(Y\) and \(Q\) respectively. Suppose that \(XY\) intersects \(PQ\) at \(M\). Let \(N\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) to \(OI\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{AM}{AN}\).
