Consider the two-dimensional lattice $G=(\mathbb{Z}^2,\mathbb{E}^2)$, where the edge set $\mathbb{E}^2$ consists of pairs of nearest neighbors in the $\ell^1$ norm in $\mathbb{Z}^2$. Let $V\subset\mathbb{Z}^2$ be an infinite subset such that the induced subgraph $G[V]$ is connected. Define $\Lambda_n=([-n,n]\times[-n,n])\cap \mathbb{Z}^2$ as a sequence of squares on the lattice. Suppose that $$ \limsup_{n\to\infty} \frac{|V\cap \Lambda_n|}{|\Lambda_n|}=0. $$ Is it true that for independent bond percolation with parameter $p$ on $G[V]$, there is almost surely no infinite cluster for any $p\in [0,1)$? Provide a justification for your answer.
