In a circle \(\omega\), a quadrilateral \(ABCD\) is inscribed such that the center of \(\omega\) lies on side \(AB\). Another circle \(\omega_1\) is externally tangent to \(\omega\) at point \(C\). A third circle \(\omega_2\) is tangent to both \(\omega\) and \(\omega_1\) at points \(D\) and \(E\) respectively. The line \(BD\) intersects \(\omega_2\) again at point \(P\), and the line \(AC\) intersects \(\omega_1\) again at point \(Q\). Determine the measure of angle \(PEQ\).
