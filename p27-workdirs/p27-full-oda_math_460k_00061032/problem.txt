Consider a point \(D\) such that \(\frac{DB}{DC} = \lambda\). Denote the points \(P \in AD \cap EF\) and \(T \in (BC)\) such that \(TB = DC\). If \(PA = PD\), find the value of \(\lambda\) given that \(a = 10\), \(b = 12\), \(c = 15\), and \(TA = 14\).
