Find all integers $n \geqslant 2$ for which there exists a real number $r>0$ and $n$ real numbers $a_{1}<a_{2}<\cdots<a_{n}$ such that the $n(n-1) / 2$ differences $a_{j}-a_{i}$ obtained when $1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant n$ are equal, up to order, to the numbers $r^{1}, r^{2}, \ldots, r^{n(n-1) / 2}$.
