In the capacitated dial-a-ride problem, we are given a metric \((V, d)\), a vehicle of capacity \(C\), a starting point \(r \in V\), and \(k\) source-sink pairs \(s_i-t_i\) for \(i = 1, \ldots, k\), where \(s_i, t_i \in V\). The vehicle must deliver each item from its source to its destination without exceeding its capacity \(C\) at any time, and the goal is to find the shortest possible tour starting and ending at \(r\).
