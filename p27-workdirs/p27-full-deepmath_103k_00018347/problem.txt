Determine whether there exists a real analytic diffeomorphism $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(x_i) = a_i$ for a given finite set $F = \{x_1, \dots, x_k\} \subset \mathbb{R}$ with $x_i < x_{i+1}$ and numbers $a_1, \dots, a_k \in \mathbb{R}$ with $a_i < a_{i+1}$. Provide a justification for your answer.
