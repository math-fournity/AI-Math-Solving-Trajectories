Solve the nonhomogeneous Riemann-Hilbert problem \(\boldsymbol{\Phi}^{+}(k) = m(k) \boldsymbol{\Phi}^{-}(k) + f(k)\) on \(-\infty < k < \infty\), where \( m(k) \) and \( f(k) \) satisfy Hölder conditions, \( m(k) \neq 0 \) on the real axis, and the index \(\operatorname{ind}(m(k)) = \kappa\). Assume that \(\boldsymbol{\Phi}(k)\) is bounded at infinity and \( m(\pm \infty) = 1 \), \( f(\pm \infty) \) is bounded.
