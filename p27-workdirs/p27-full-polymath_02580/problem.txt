Let \( n \) be a positive integer. What is the largest \( k \) for which there exist \( n \times n \) matrices \( M_{1}, \ldots, M_{k} \) and \( N_{1}, \ldots, N_{k} \) with real entries such that for all \( i \) and \( j \), the matrix product \( M_{i} N_{j} \) has a zero entry somewhere on its diagonal if and only if \( i \neq j \)?
