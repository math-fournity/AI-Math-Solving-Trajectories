A cosmic engineer is tasked with building a digital transmission sequence of length $n+1$. The possible positions for data pulses are indexed from $0$ to $n$. To create a unique signal, the engineer chooses exactly 6 distinct positions from the set $\{0, 1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ at random to place a "1", while all other positions remain "0". This signal is represented as a bit-string, which can be modeled as a polynomial $P(x)$ where the coefficients are 1 for the chosen positions and 0 otherwise.
