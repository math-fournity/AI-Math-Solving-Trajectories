Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcenter \(O\) and incenter \(I\). Let \(A', B', C'\) be the points symmetric to \(A, B, C\) with respect to \(BC, CA, AB\) respectively. Define \(P\) as the intersection of the radical axes of the circles \((OAA')\) and \((OBB')\), and \(Q\) as the intersection of the radical axes of the circles \((IAA')\) and \((IBB')\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{OP}{OQ}\).
