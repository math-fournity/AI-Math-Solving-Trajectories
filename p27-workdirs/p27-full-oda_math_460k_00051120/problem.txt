Assume \(a\) and \(b\) are real numbers such that \(0 < a < b\), and let \(\textsf{K}_{a,b}\) be the set of all non-negative, monotonically decreasing functions \(f\) satisfying \(\int_a^b f(t) \, dt = 1\) and \(af(a) = bf(b)\) on the interval \([a, b]\). Find the value of \(\sup \left\{ \int_a^b \max\{f(t), g(t)\} \, dt \, : \, f, g \in \textsf{K}_{a,b} \right\}\).
