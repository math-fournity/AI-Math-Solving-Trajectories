Let \( G \) be a simple group with a maximal subgroup of the form \( p:q \) (a semidirect product of \( \mathbb{Z}_p \) and \( \mathbb{Z}_q \)), where \( p \) and \( q \) are primes and \( q \mid (p-1) \). Suppose every Sylow \( p \)-subgroup of \( G \) is \( \mathbb{Z}_p \) and each is contained properly in exactly one maximal subgroup of \( G \). Prove that \( G \cong L_2(p) \).
