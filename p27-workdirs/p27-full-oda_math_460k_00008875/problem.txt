Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle inscribed in circle \(\Gamma\) with \(\overline{AB} > \overline{AC}\). Let \(D\), \(E\), and \(F\) be the feet of the perpendiculars from \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) to \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\), respectively. The midpoint of \(BC\) is \(M\), and line \(EF\) and \(AM\) meet at \(P\). Let \(Q\) be the foot of the perpendicular from the intersection of lines \(BE\) and \(CF\) to line \(EF\), and \(R\) be the intersection of line \(BC\) and \(EF\). \(G (\neq A)\) is the intersection of line \(AD\) and circle \(\Gamma\), and \(K (\neq G)\) is the intersection of line \(GR\) and circle \(\Gamma\). Let \(S\) be the intersection other than \(K\) of the line \(DK\) and the circle \(\Gamma\). Calculate the value of \(\angle ASP\) in degrees.
