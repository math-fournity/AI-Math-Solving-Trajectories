For $\alpha > 0$, consider a sequence of independent random variables $\left \{ {X_{n}}^{(\alpha )} \right \}_{n\geq 1}$, where $P({X_{n}}^{(\alpha )} = 1) = \frac{1}{n^{2\alpha}}$ and $P({X_{n}}^{(\alpha )} = 0) = 1 - \frac{1}{n^{2\alpha}}$. Define $S = \left \{ \alpha >0 :{X_{n}}^{(\alpha )} \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0, \ n\to \infty \right \}$. Determine the infimum of $S$ using the Borel-Cantelli lemma.
