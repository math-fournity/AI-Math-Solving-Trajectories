In triangle \(ABC\), let \(X\) and \(Y\) be two points not lying on its circumcircle. Let \(A_1\), \(B_1\), \(C_1\) be the projections of \(X\) onto \(BC\), \(CA\), \(AB\), respectively, and \(A_2\), \(B_2\), \(C_2\) be the projections of \(Y\) onto \(BC\), \(CA\), \(AB\), respectively. Let \(P\) be the point of concurrency of the perpendiculars from \(A_1\), \(B_1\), \(C_1\) to \(B_2C_2\), \(C_2A_2\), \(A_2B_2\), respectively, if they concur. If the line \(XY\) passes through the circumcenter \(O\) of \(ABC\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{OP}{OX}\).
