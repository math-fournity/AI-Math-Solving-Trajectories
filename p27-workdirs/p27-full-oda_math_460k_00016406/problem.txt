In triangle \(ABC\), let \(M_a, M_b, M_c\) be the midpoints of \(BC, CA, AB\), respectively. The incircle \((I)\) of triangle \(ABC\) touches the sides \(BC, CA, AB\) at points \(A', B', C'\). The line \(r_1\) is the reflection of line \(BC\) in \(AI\), and line \(r_2\) is the perpendicular from \(A'\) to \(IM_a\). Denote by \(X_a\) the intersection of \(r_1\) and \(r_2\), and define \(X_b\) and \(X_c\) analogously. If the coordinates of \(X_a, X_b, X_c\) are such that they lie on a line tangent to the incircle of triangle \(ABC\), find the value of the expression \(\frac{AX_a + BX_b + CX_c}{AI + BI + CI}\).
