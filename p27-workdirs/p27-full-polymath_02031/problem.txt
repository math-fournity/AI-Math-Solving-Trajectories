Let $ABC$ be a triangle where $A = (0, 12)$, $B = (-4, 0)$, and $C = (6, 0)$. Let $G$ be the centroid of $\triangle ABC$. Let $(d)$ be the line perpendicular to the median $AG$ that passes through the point $(0, -5)$. Let $M$ be an arbitrary point on $(d)$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the midpoints of $MB$ and $MC$ respectively. The line through point $E$ perpendicular to $(d)$ meets $AB$ at $P$. The line through point $F$ perpendicular to $(d)$ meets $AC$ at $Q$. Let $(d')$ be the line through point $M$ perpendicular to $PQ$. It is known that $(d')$ always passes through a fixed point $(x_0, y_0)$ regardless of the choice of $M$ on $(d)$. Find the value of $x_0 + y_0$.
