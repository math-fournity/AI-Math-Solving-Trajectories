Determine whether the following statement is true or false: For any monic polynomial \(f^{(t)}(z)=a_0^{(t)}+\dotsb+a_{n-1}^{(t)}z^{n-1}+z^n\in\mathbb C[z]\) with coefficients \(a_0^{(t)},\dotsc,a_{n-1}^{(t)}\colon\mathbb R\to\mathbb C\) continuous on \(t\), there exists a complex-valued continuous function \(\phi^{(t)}\colon\mathbb R\to\mathbb C\) such that \(f^{(t)}(\phi^{(t)})=0\) for all \(t\in\mathbb R\).
