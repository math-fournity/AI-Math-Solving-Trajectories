Let \(ABCDE\) be a square pyramid with \(ABCD\) as the base square and \(E\) as the apex. Each side length of \(ABCDE\) is 6. Let \(ABCDDD'C'B'A'\) be a cube, where \(AA', BB', CC', DD'\) are edges of the cube. Andy the ant is on the surface of \(EABCDDD'C'B'A'\) at the center of triangle \(ABE\) (call this point \(G\)) and wants to crawl on the surface of the cube to \(D'\). What is the length of the shortest path from \(G\) to \(D'\)? Write your answer in the form \(\sqrt{a+b \sqrt{3}}\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive integers.
