Let $p \ge 3$ be a prime number. Suppose you are given $N$ lines $l_1, \dotsc, l_N \subset \mathbb{F}_p^2$. You can translate these lines to obtain new lines $l_1', \dotsc, l_N'$ (which are either parallel or identical to the original lines) such that the entire vector space $\mathbb{F}_p^2$ is covered by these new lines: $l_1' \cup \dotsb \cup l_N' = \mathbb{F}_p^2$. Is it always possible to achieve this if $N \ge 2p - 1$? More generally, given $(p-1)n+1$ affine hyperplanes in $\mathbb{F}_p^n$, can you always translate them so that the resulting translates cover the whole space $\mathbb{F}_p^n$?
