Given a positive integer \(\ell\), define the sequence \(\{a_{n}^{(\ell)}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\) such that \(a_{n}^{(\ell)}=\left\lfloor n+\sqrt[\ell]{n}+\frac{1}{2}\right\rfloor\) for all positive integers \(n\). Let \(S\) denote the set of positive integers that appear in all three of the sequences \(\{a_{n}^{(2)}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\), \(\{a_{n}^{(3)}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\), and \(\{a_{n}^{(4)}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\). Find the sum of the elements of \(S\) that lie in the interval \([1,100]\).
