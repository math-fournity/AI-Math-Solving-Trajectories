For each positive integer \( n > 1 \), if \( n = p_1^{\alpha_1} p_2^{\alpha_2} \cdots p_k^{\alpha_k} \) where \( p_i \) are pairwise different prime numbers and \( \alpha_i \) are positive integers, define \( f(n) \) as \( \alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + \cdots + \alpha_k \). For \( n = 1 \), let \( f(1) = 0 \). Find all pairs of integer polynomials \( P(x) \) and \( Q(x) \) such that for any positive integer \( m \), \( f(P(m)) = Q(f(m)) \) holds.
