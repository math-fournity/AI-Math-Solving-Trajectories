Determine the value of $f(d)$, the smallest possible size for an efficient substitute for $\Omega = \{0,1\}^d$, where $d$ is an integer $\geq 2$. An efficient substitute $\Omega'$ satisfies $p(\Omega',i,j,A)=p(\Omega,i,j,A)$ for all $i,j$ and $A$, with $p(\Omega,i,j,A)$ being the probability that $(x_i,x_j) \in A$ for a randomly selected $(x_1,x_2, \ldots, x_d) \in \Omega$. The trivial bound is $f(d) \leq 2^d$, and it is known that $f(3)=4$. What is $f(d)$ in general?
