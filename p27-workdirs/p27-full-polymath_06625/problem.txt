In the competitive world of high-end restaurant management, a culinary network is established among a group of $N$ elite chefs. We define a network as "$n$-synergetic" (where $n \geq 2$ is a fixed integer) if the following condition holds: for every single male chef in the network, if he is temporarily removed from the kitchen, one can always identify a set of $n$ other chefs among the remaining staff where every individual in that set has a pre-existing professional partnership with every other individual in that same set.
