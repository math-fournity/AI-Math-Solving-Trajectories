Let circles \(\omega_{1}\) and \(\omega_{2}\) intersect at points \(P\) and \(Q\). The common external tangent \(\ell\) to the two circles, closer to \(Q\), touches \(\omega_{1}\) and \(\omega_{2}\) at points \(A\) and \(B\) respectively. Line \(AQ\) intersects \(\omega_{2}\) at \(X\) while \(BQ\) intersects \(\omega_{1}\) again at \(Y\). Let \(M\) and \(N\) denote the midpoints of \(\overline{AY}\) and \(\overline{BX}\), respectively. If \(AQ = \sqrt{T}\), \(BQ = 7\), and \(AB = 8\), find the length of \(MN\). If the answer is of the form of an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, compute the value of $a + b$.
