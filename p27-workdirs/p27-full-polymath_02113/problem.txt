Four people A, B, C, and D are stationed in four consecutive houses around a circle of 12 houses. Each person can move to an empty house that is five away either clockwise or anticlockwise. After a sequence of moves, the four people are again in four consecutive houses. Let $S$ be the set of all possible permutations of the four people (ordered clockwise) in these four consecutive houses. How many elements are in the set $S$?
