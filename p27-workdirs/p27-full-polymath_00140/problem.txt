There are $n$ students standing in a circle, one behind the other. The students have distinct heights $h_1 < h_2 < \dots < h_n$. If a student with height $h_k$ is standing directly behind a student with height $h_{k-2}$ or less, the two students are permitted to switch places. Let $M(n)$ be the maximum possible number of such switches that can be made before reaching a position in which no further switches are possible. Find $M(20)$.
