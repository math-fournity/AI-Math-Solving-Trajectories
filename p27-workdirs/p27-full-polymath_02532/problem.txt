Let $n = 10$ be a natural number. A cube of dimensions $n \times n \times n$ is composed of $n^{3}$ unit cubes, each of which is colored in some color. For each $n \times n \times 1$ parallelepiped of the cube (in each of the three possible orientations), we consider the set of colors in which the $n^2$ unit cubes are colored. In this way, we obtain $3n$ sets of colors, divided into three groups according to the orientation of the parallelepipeds. It turns out that for every set of colors in each group, the same set appears in the other two groups. Find the maximum number of colors that could have been used.
