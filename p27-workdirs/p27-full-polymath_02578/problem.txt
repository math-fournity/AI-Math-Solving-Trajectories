Let $n = 10$ be a natural number. In the middle of a circular garden stands a watchtower. At the edge of the garden stand $n$ evenly distributed garden gnomes. Each watchman monitors an area of the garden that is bounded by two different garden gnomes. We say that watchman $A$ controls watchman $B$ if the entire area of $B$ is strictly contained within that of $A$. Among the watchmen, there are two groups: apprentices and masters. Each apprentice is controlled by exactly one master and controls no one, while masters are controlled by no one.
