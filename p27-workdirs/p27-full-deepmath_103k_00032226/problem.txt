In an inscribed quadrilateral \(ABCD\), diagonals intersect at point \(O\). Inside triangle \(AOB\), point \(K\) is chosen such that \(KO\) bisects angle \(CKO\). Ray \(DK\) intersects the circumcircle of triangle \(COK\) again at \(L\), and ray \(CK\) intersects the circumcircle of triangle \(DOK\) again at \(M\). Determine the ratio of the areas of triangles \(ALO\) and \(BMO\).
