In a specialized data-processing facility, an engineer is monitoring a sequence of energy outputs generated by a system. The baseline energy levels follow the Lucas sequence $l_n$, where the initial pulse $l_0$ is 2 units, the second pulse $l_1$ is 1 unit, and every subsequent pulse $l_{n+2}$ is the sum of the previous two pulses ($l_{n+1} + l_n$).
