Let \( E \) and \( F \) be real or complex normed linear spaces. Let \( T_n: E \to F \) be a sequence of continuous linear transformations such that \( \sup_n ||T_n|| < \infty \). Let \( M = \{ x \in E \ | \ \{ T_n(x) \} \text{ is a Cauchy sequence} \} \). If \( x_0 \in E \setminus M \) and \( d(x_0, M) \) denotes the distance from \( x_0 \) to the set \( M \), find the value of \( d(x_0, M) \) when \( ||x_0|| = 1 \) and \( \sup_n ||T_n|| = 2 \).
