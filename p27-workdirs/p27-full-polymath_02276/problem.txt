The field is a $41 \times 41$ grid, with a tank camouflaged in one of the cells. A fighter jet can shoot at one cell with each shot. If the shot hits the tank, the tank moves to an adjacent cell; if not, it stays in place. After each shot, the pilot does not know whether he hit the tank or not. To destroy the tank, it must be hit twice. What is the minimum number of shots required to guarantee that the tank is destroyed?
