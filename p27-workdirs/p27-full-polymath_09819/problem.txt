A specialized robotics company has designed a testing facility consisting of a rectangular grid of 12 docking stations. These stations are arranged in two parallel rows: Row 0 and Row 1. Each row contains 6 stations, indexed from column 0 to column 5. Each station is identified by a coordinate $(i, j)$, where $i \in \{0, 1\}$ represents the row and $j \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ represents the column.
