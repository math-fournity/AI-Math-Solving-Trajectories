Given a triangle \(ABC\) with circumcircle \((O)\) and incircle \((I)\), let \(I_a\) be the \(A\)-excenter. Let \(P\) and \(D\) be the second points of intersection of \(AI\) and \(AO\) with \((O)\), respectively. Let \(Q\) be the second point of intersection of \(PO\) with \((O)\). Let \(M\) be the point of intersection of \(QI_a\) and \(AD\). Let \(X\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(A\) to \(BC\), and let \(N\) be the midpoint of \(IX\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{ON}{MX}\).
