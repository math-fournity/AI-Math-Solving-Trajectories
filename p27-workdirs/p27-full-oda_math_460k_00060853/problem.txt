Consider a convex $n$-gon where all its sides and diagonals emanating from one vertex are colored, resulting in $2n-3$ colored segments. Positive numbers are written on these colored segments. In one move, we can take a quadrilateral $ABCD$ such that $AC$ and all sides are colored, remove $AC$, and color $BD$ with the number $\frac{xz+yt}{w}$, where $x, y, z, t, w$ are the numbers on $AB, BC, CD, DA, AC$ respectively. After a series of such moves, if all colored segments are the same as they were at the beginning, find the sum of all possible values of $n$ for which the numbers on the segments can remain unchanged after the moves.
