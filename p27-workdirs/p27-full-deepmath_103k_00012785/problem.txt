Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a Banach $^*$-algebra and $a = a^* \in \mathcal{A}$. Suppose that the spectrum of $a$, $\sigma_{\mathcal{A}}(a)$, is contained in $\{0\} \cup [C,D]$ with $D > C > 1$. Determine whether the sequence $x_n = a(1-a^n)^{-1}$ converges to zero in $\mathcal{A}$. Note that the sequence of polynomials $p_n(z) = \frac{z}{1-z^n}$ converges uniformly to zero on the spectrum of $a$ but not on any compact subset of an open neighborhood of the spectrum.
