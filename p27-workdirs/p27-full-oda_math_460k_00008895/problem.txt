Two players, I and II, alternatively select countable ordinals. After \( \omega \) steps they consider the set of all selected ordinals, and II wins if it is an initial segment, otherwise I wins. \n\n(a) There is a winning strategy for II.\n\n(b) There is no such winning strategy if the choice of II depends only on the set of ordinals selected before (by the two players).\n\n(c) Even such a strategy exists if II is allowed to select finitely many ordinals in every step.
