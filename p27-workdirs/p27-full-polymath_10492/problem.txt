In a specialized chemistry lab, a technician is organizing a horizontal rack containing $2n$ test tubes. The rack contains $n$ vials of a dense violet catalyst and $n$ vials of a clear distilled solvent, arranged in an arbitrary initial sequence. The technician's goal is to reorganize the rack so that all $n$ violet catalyst vials are positioned at the leftmost end of the rack, followed by all $n$ clear solvent vials.
