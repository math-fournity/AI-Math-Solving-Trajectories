Determine the limiting value of \( \mathcal{P}_\infty \) as \( n \) approaches infinity, where \( \mathcal{P}_n \) is the ratio of twin primes \((p_1, p_2)\) with \( p_2 = p_1 + 2 \) and \( p_1 - 1 = a_1 \times b_1 \), \( p_2 + 1 = a_2 \times b_2 \), \( p_1 + 1 = p_2 - 1 = a \times b \) such that \( \min\{|b_1 - a_1|, |b - a|, |b_2 - a_2|\} \neq |b - a| \). Exclude the pair \((2, 3)\) from consideration.
