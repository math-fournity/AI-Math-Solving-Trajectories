In a circular orbital space station, there are $n$ independent airlock modules arranged in a ring. Each airlock can be in one of two states: "Pressurized" or "Depressurized." Due to the station's safety protocols, a technician is permitted to toggle the status of two adjacent airlocks simultaneously (changing Pressurized to Depressurized and vice versa), but only if both airlocks currently share the same status.
