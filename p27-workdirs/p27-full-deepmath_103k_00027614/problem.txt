Let \( X \) be a Banach space and define the set \( L = \{f:[0,1]\to X\vert\, f \text{ is Borel-measurable and } \int_0^1 \Vert f \Vert < + \infty \} \), where \( \int \) denotes the Lebesgue integral. Define the operator \( T:L \to X^{**} \) by \( (Tf)(x^*) = \int_0^1 x^*\circ f \) for all \( x^* \in X^* \). The operator \( T \) is well-defined, meaning \( x^*\circ f \) is integrable and \( Tf \in X^{**} \). Let \( i: X \to X^{**} \) be the canonical embedding, defined by \( i(x)(x^*) = x^*(x) \). Determine whether for every \( f \in L \), there exists an \( x \in X \) such that \( Tf = i(x) \).
