Let \( A_0B_0C_0 \) and \( A_1B_1C_1 \) be two acute-angled triangles. Consider all triangles \( ABC \) that are similar to triangle \( A_1B_1C_1 \) (so that vertices \( A_1 \), \( B_1 \), \( C_1 \) correspond to vertices \( A \), \( B \), \( C \), respectively) and circumscribed about triangle \( A_0B_0C_0 \) (where \( A_0 \) lies on \( BC \), \( B_0 \) on \( CA \), and \( C_0 \) on \( AB \)). Of all such possible triangles, determine the one with maximum area, and construct it.
