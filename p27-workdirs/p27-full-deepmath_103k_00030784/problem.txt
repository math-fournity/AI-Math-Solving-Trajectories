In the game of *Ring Mafia*, there are 2019 counters arranged in a circle, with 673 being mafia and 1346 being town. Tony and Madeline take turns, with Tony going first. Tony does not know which counters are mafia, but Madeline does. On Tony's turn, he can remove any subset of counters. On Madeline's turn, she removes a town counter adjacent to a mafia counter. The game ends when all mafia or all town counters are removed. Can Tony guarantee that at least one town counter remains at the end of the game, regardless of the initial placement of mafia counters and Madeline's actions?
