Side \(\overline{AB}\) of \(\triangle ABC\) is the diameter of a semicircle. If \(AB = 3 + \sqrt{3}\), \(BC = 3\sqrt{2}\), and \(AC = 2\sqrt{3}\), then the area of the shaded region can be written as \(\frac{a + (b + c\sqrt{d})\pi}{e}\), where \(a, b, c, d, e\) are integers, \(e\) is positive, \(d\) is square-free, and \(\gcd(a, b, c, e) = 1\). Find \(10000a + 1000b + 100c + 10d + e\).
