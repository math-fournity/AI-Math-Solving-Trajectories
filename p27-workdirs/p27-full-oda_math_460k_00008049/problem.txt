A circle inscribed in triangle \( ABC \) with radius 3 touches the side \( AC \) at point D. Points K and L are taken on the extensions of sides \( AC \) and \( BC \) beyond point C, respectively, such that \(\angle CKL = 30^{\circ}\). The lengths of segments \( AK \) and \( BL \) are equal to the semiperimeter of triangle \( ABC \). A point M is chosen on the circumcircle of triangle \( ABC \) so that \( CM \parallel KL \). Find the cosine of angle \( ACB \) if the length of segment \( DM \) is 6. (16 points)
