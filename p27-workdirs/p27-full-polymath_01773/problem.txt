Problem 7. (12 points) Around a round table sit $N$ knights. Every morning, the wizard Merlin arranges them. From the second day onwards, after arranging them, Merlin allows the knights a certain number of swaps: two knights sitting next to each other can swap places if they did not sit next to each other on the first day. The knights aim to sit in the same order as they were on one of the previous days; then the meetings will end. What is the maximum number of meetings that Merlin can definitely hold? (Arrangements that are obtained by rotation are considered the same. Merlin does not sit at the table.)
