In a finite affine plane over the finite field $\mathbb{F}_q$ where $q = 2^n$, consider the case where $q = 4$. Is it possible to find a set of 6 points $\{P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_6\}$ such that no three points lie on the same line, and for any odd $i$, the vectors $\overrightarrow{P_iP_{i+1}}$ and $\overrightarrow{P_1P_2}$ are equal? It is known that this is not possible for $q = 4$. What is the maximum number of points with these properties, and how does this relate to hyperovals in the projective plane $PG(2,q)$?
