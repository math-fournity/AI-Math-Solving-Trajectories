For a convex $n$-gon $P$ with vertices $V_1, V_2, \ldots, V_n$ and the set $S_P$ of points inside $P$ but not on any diagonal, define a function $f: S_P \rightarrow \{0, 1, \ldots, n-3\}^n$ such that $v \mapsto (a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n)$ where $a_i = x$ if and only if $v$ is inside $\angle V_{i+1+x}V_iV_{i+2+x}$ (subscripts are understood modulo $n$). Define $g(v) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i$. Determine the value domain of $g$ for every $n$ and convex $n$-gon $P$.
