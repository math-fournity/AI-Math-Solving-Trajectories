Let \(AD\), \(BE\), and \(CF\) be the altitudes of \(\triangle ABC\) which intersect the circumcircle \(\odot (ABC)\) at points \(D_1\), \(E_1\), and \(F_1\) respectively. Let \(\odot (D_1EF)\), \(\odot (E_1FD)\), and \(\odot (F_1DE)\) intersect \(\odot (ABC)\) again at \(D_2\), \(E_2\), and \(F_2\) respectively. Calculate the value of \(\frac{AD_2 \cdot BE_2 \cdot CF_2}{R^3}\), where \(R\) is the circumradius of \(\triangle ABC\).
