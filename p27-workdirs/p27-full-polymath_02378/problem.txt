A positive integer $k$ is given. Initially, $N$ cells are marked on an infinite checkered plane. The cross of a cell $A$ is the set of all cells lying in the same row or in the same column as $A$. A turn consists of marking an unmarked cell $A$ if the cross of $A$ contains at least $k$ marked cells. It is given that every cell on the plane can be marked in a sequence of such turns.
