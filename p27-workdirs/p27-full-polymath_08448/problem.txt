In a specialized digital archive, there are 2003 consecutive storage slots labeled $1, 2, \ldots, 2003$. A security protocol requires the selection of a subset of these slots, denoted as $S$, to store sensitive data packets. To prevent cross-interference, a selection is deemed "interference-free" for a pair of integer distance constraints $(a, b)$ if no two selected slots $s_1$ and $s_2$ have a distance $|s_1 - s_2|$ equal to exactly $a$ or exactly $b$.
