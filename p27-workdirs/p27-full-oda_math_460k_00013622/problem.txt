Suppose that \( n \) is odd and let \(-x_n\) denote the only real root of the equation\n\n\[\n1 + \frac{x}{11} + \frac{x^2}{21} + \cdots + \frac{x^n}{n!} = 0 \quad [V 74].\n\]\n\nAs \( n \to \infty \), \( x_n \) is asymptotically given by\n\n\[\nx_n = \xi n + \alpha \log n + \beta + o(1),\n\]\n\nwhere \(\xi\) is the only real root of the equation \( e^{1+\xi}\xi = 1 \) and \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are given by\n\n\[\n\alpha = \frac{1}{2} \frac{\frac{1}{\xi}}{1+\xi}, \quad \beta = \frac{\xi}{1+\xi} \log \left( \sqrt{2\pi} \frac{1+\xi}{\xi} \right).\n\]
