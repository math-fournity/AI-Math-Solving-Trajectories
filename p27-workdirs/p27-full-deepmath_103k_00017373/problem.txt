Given a natural number $k$, consider a function $f: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ that is lower semicontinuous. Does there exist an increasing sequence of $k$-times continuously differentiable functions $(g_n)_n \subset C^k(\mathbb{R}^d ; [0, \infty))$ that converges pointwise to $f$, satisfying $g_n(x) \leq g_{n+1}(x)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, as well as $\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} g_n(x) = f(x)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$? Provide a justification for your answer.
