In a specialized logistics hub, two circular transport tracks, Track 1 ($\omega_1$) and Track 2 ($\omega_2$), intersect at two specific transfer junctions, Junction A ($A$) and Junction B ($B$). Two straight maintenance pathways, Pathway PQ ($PQ$) and Pathway RS ($RS$), are built such that they are perfectly tangent to both circular tracks. Pathway PQ touches Track 1 at point $P$ and Track 2 at point $Q$, while Pathway RS touches Track 1 at point $R$ and Track 2 at point $S$.
