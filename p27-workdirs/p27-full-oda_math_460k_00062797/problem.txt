Find the greatest positive integer \( n \) for which there exist \( n \) nonnegative integers \( x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \), not all zero, such that for any sequence \( \varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, \ldots, \varepsilon_n \) of elements \(\{-1, 0, 1\}\), not all zero, \( n^3 \) does not divide \( \varepsilon_1 x_1 + \varepsilon_2 x_2 + \cdots + \varepsilon_n x_n \).
