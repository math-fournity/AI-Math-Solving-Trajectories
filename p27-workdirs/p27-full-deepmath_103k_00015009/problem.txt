Consider the extension \( \mathbb{F}_2(t)(\alpha) \) of degree 4 over \( \mathbb{F}_2(t) \), where the minimal polynomial of \( \alpha \) is given by \( \alpha^4 + a\alpha^2 + b = 0 \) with \( a, b \in \mathbb{F}_2(t) \). Determine whether \( \mathbb{F}_2(t)(\alpha) \) always contains an intermediate field \( L \) such that \([L: \mathbb{F}_2(t)] = 2\) and \( L \) is inseparable over \( \mathbb{F}_2(t) \).
