An infinite square grid is colored in a chessboard pattern with squares of unit area. For any pair of positive integers $m, n$, consider a right-angled triangle whose vertices are grid points and whose legs, of lengths $m$ and $n$, lie along the grid lines. Let $S_b$ be the total area of the black part of the triangle and $S_w$ be the total area of its white part. Define $f(m, n) = |S_b - S_w|$.
