Suppose that three players cut a cake using the scheme of random offers with the Dirichlet distribution, where the parameters are \( k_1 = 2 \), \( k_2 = 1 \), and \( k_3 = 1 \). Negotiations have a maximal duration of \( K = 3 \) rounds. Find the optimal strategies of the players when the final decision is made by (a) the majority of votes, or (b) the unanimous consent of all players.
