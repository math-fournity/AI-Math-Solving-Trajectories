We start a game with all the integers from $2$ to $2015$ written on a blackboard, in this order. A "change" consists of selecting two numbers, say $a$ and $b$, and replacing them with $\frac{4a+3b}{5}$ and $\frac{24a-7b}{25}$, respectively. After performing a series of such "changes," we aim to obtain all the integers from $1$ to $2014$, in this order. Calculate the sum of all possible values of the last number on the blackboard if it is possible to achieve this configuration. If it is not possible, enter $0$.
