Let $h$ be a positive odd integer and $n = 4h^2$. Consider the polynomial $R(t) = r_0 + r_1 t + \cdots + r_{n-1} t^{n-1}$ with integer coefficients $r_i \in \{-1, 1\}$. Suppose $R(1) = 2h$ and for every complex number $\omega$ such that $\omega^n = 1$ and $\omega \notin \{-1, 1\}$, $R(\omega)$ is a nonzero integer. Determine whether all these integers $R(\omega)$ have the same sign.
