Consider the set \( S = \{1, 2, \ldots, 1000\} \). Every second, Caleb removes an element from \( S \) and subtracts this element from all remaining elements in \( S \). If, after this process, some elements are left negative, Caleb adds \( 1000 \) to each element. Caleb repeats this process until \( S \) is empty. If the expected number of times Caleb removes \( 1 \) can be written as \( \frac{m}{n} \) for relatively prime positive integers \( m \) and \( n \), find \( m + n \).
