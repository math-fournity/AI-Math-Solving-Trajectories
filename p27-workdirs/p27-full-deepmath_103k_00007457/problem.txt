Given an element \( v = (v_1, \dots, v_d) \in \mathbb{Z}^d \) with \( \gcd(v_1, \dots, v_d) = 1 \), there exists a regular integer matrix \( A \in \mathop{GL}_d(\mathbb{Z}) \) such that \( A(v) = (1, 0, \dots, 0) \). Assume \( |v_1| + \dots + |v_d| = n \). Determine the smallest \( \ell \in \mathbb{N} \) such that the image \( A\left([-1, 1]^d\right) \) is contained in the cube \( [-\ell, \ell]^d \). Find the asymptotic behavior of \( \ell(n) \) up to a multiplicative constant.
