In triangle \(ABC\), let \(A'\), \(B'\), and \(C'\) be the respective excenters. Define \(A_{b}\) as the projection of \(A\) onto \(A'B'\), \(A_{c}\) as the projection of \(A\) onto \(A'C'\), and similarly define \(B_{c}\), \(B_{a}\), \(C_{a}\), and \(C_{b}\). Let \(P\), \(Q\), and \(R\) be the respective orthocenters of triangles \(A'A_{b}A_{c}\), \(B'B_{c}B_{a}\), and \(C'C_{a}C_{b}\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{[PQR]}{[ABC]}\), where \([XYZ]\) denotes the area of triangle \(XYZ\).
