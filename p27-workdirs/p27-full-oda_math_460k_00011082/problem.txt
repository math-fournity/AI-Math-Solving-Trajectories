Consider a triangle \(ABC\) with the first isodynamic point \(F\). Let \(A_1\) and \(A_2\) be the inverse points of \(B\) and \(C\) with respect to the circle centered at \(A\) with radius \(AF\). Similarly, define \(B_1\) and \(B_2\) with respect to \(C\) and \(A\), and \(C_1\) and \(C_2\) with respect to \(A\) and \(B\). Calculate the radius of the circle passing through the six points \(A_1, A_2, B_1, B_2, C_1, C_2\) if \(AF = 1\) and the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\) is \(R\). Express your answer in terms of \(R\).
