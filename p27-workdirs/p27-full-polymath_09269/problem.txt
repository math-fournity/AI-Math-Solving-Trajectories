In a specialized circular particle accelerator facility known as Omega, a complex web of laser beams is used to stabilize plasma. There are a finite number of laser beams, each acting as a straight-line chord spanning the circular chamber. The safety protocols of the facility require a high level of redundancy: every single anchor point on the perimeter of the circle where a beam originates or terminates must be the junction for at least $d=20$ different laser beams.
