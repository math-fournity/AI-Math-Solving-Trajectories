Consider triangle \(ABC\) with its first Fermat point \(F_1\) and second Fermat point \(F_2\). Let \(A'B'C'\) and \(A''B''C''\) be the reflections of \(ABC\) in \(F_1\) and \(F_2\) respectively. If \(O_1\) and \(O_2\) are the circumcenters of \((A'B'C')\) and \((A''B''C'')\), and \(O\) and \(H\) are the circumcenter and orthocenter of triangle \(ABC\), find the ratio of the radius of the circle passing through \(O_1\), \(O_2\), \(O\), and \(H\) to the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\).
