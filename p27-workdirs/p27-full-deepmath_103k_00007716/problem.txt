Consider a connected (undirected) graph $G$ with vertex set $V$ of size at least 2. Assign a real number $x_v$ to each vertex $v \in V$. The constraint is that for any subset $W \subseteq V$ such that the induced subgraphs on both $W$ and $V \setminus W$ are connected, it holds that \( \left| \sum_{v \in W} x_v \right| \le 1 \). Maximize \( \sum_{v \in V} |x_v| \). Is it true that for any maximizing solution, the sum of the $x_v$'s is 0?
