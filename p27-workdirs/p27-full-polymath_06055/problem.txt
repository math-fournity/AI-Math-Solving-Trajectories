A high-security data pipeline consists of 14 sequential processing nodes, indexed $1, 2, \ldots, 14$. Digital packets are transmitted between these nodes, where a packet entering the system at node $i$ and exiting at node $j$ must satisfy $1 \le i < j \le 14$. The pipeline has a strict bandwidth constraint: at any segment between two adjacent nodes, there can be at most $C$ active packets being transmitted simultaneously.
