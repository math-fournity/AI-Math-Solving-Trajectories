Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcircle \((O)\). \(AD\), \(BE\), and \(CF\) are the altitudes. \(AO\) intersects \(BC\) at point \(M\). Similarly, \(BO\) intersects \(CA\) at point \(N\) and \(CO\) intersects \(AB\) at point \(P\). A circle passes through \(M\) and \(D\) and touches \((O)\) at \(X\) (where \(X\) lies on the arc \(BC\) not containing \(A\)). Similarly, define \(Y\) and \(Z\) for the other sides. Calculate the value of \(\frac{AX \cdot BY \cdot CZ}{AO \cdot BO \cdot CO}\).
