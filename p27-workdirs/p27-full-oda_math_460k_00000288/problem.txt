Let \( P \) be a probability distribution defined on the Borel sets of the real line, symmetric with respect to the origin, absolutely continuous with respect to the Lebesgue measure, and its density function \( p \) is zero outside the interval \([-1,1]\) and inside this interval it is between the positive numbers \( c \) and \( d \) (\( c < d \)). Suppose \( Q \) is another probability distribution such that the convolution of \( Q \) with itself equals \( P \). If \( q \) is the density function of \( Q \), find the maximum possible value of the integral \(\int_{-1}^{1} q(x)^2 \, dx\).
