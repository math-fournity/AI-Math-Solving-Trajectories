Let \( \triangle ABC \) be a triangle with \( AB = 13 \), \( BC = 14 \), and \( CA = 15 \). Let \( G \) denote the centroid of \( \triangle ABC \), and let \( G_A \) denote the image of \( G \) under a reflection across \( BC \), with \( G_B \) the image of \( G \) under a reflection across \( AC \), and \( G_C \) the image of \( G \) under a reflection across \( AB \). Let \( O_G \) be the circumcenter of \( \triangle G_A G_B G_C \) and let \( X \) be the intersection of \( AO_G \) with \( BC \) and \( Y \) denote the intersection of \( AG \) with \( BC \). Compute \( XY \). If the answer is of the form of an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, compute the value of $a + b$.
