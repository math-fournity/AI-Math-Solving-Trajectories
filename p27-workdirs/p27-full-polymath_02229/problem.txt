Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{100}$ be $100$ distinct real numbers arranged in a circle. Let $S$ be the set of all indices $i \in \{1, 2, \dots, 100\}$ such that the sum of the two numbers on the outside of four consecutive points starting at $i$ is strictly greater than the sum of the two numbers on the inside. That is, $i \in S$ if $a_i + a_{i+3} > a_{i+1} + a_{i+2}$, where the indices are taken modulo 100. Find the minimum possible size of the set $S$.
