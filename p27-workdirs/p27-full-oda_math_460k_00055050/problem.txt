Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with altitudes \(BE\) and \(CF\) intersecting at \(H\). Points \(P\) and \(Q\) lie on \(AC\) and \(AB\) respectively such that \(APHQ\) is a parallelogram. \(PQ\) intersects \(BC\) at \(X\). Let \(T\) be the isogonal conjugate of \(Q\) with respect to \(\triangle HBC\) and \(U\) be the isogonal conjugate of \(P\) with respect to \(\triangle HBC\). Let \(d\) be the reflection of \(AH\) through the angle bisector of \(\angle THU\). Let \(O\) be the circumcenter of \(\triangle ABC\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{AO}{d}\) where \(d\) is the length of the segment from \(A\) to the intersection of \(d\) with the line \(AO\).
