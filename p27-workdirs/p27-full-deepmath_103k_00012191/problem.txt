Consider a length space $M$ and a finite set of geodesics $\Pi$ in this space, where each geodesic is the unique path between its endpoints. Suppose that any two distinct geodesics $\pi_1, \pi_2 \in \Pi$ intersect at most at a single point. Is it possible to assign a direction to each geodesic in $\Pi$ such that the resulting system of paths is acyclic, meaning there is no closed path in $\bigcup_{\pi \in \Pi} \pi$ that only travels along any given path $\pi$ in its chosen direction?
