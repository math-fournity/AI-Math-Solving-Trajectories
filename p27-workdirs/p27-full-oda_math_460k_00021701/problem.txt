(a) \[ \ddot{u}r - mr\dot{\theta}^2 - mr \sin^2 \theta \, \dot{\psi}^2 = 0 \]\n\n(b) \[ mr\ddot{\theta} + 2mr\dot{u} \, \dot{\theta} - mr \sin \theta \cos \theta \, \dot{\psi}^2 = 0 \]\n\n(c) \[ mr \sin \theta \, \dot{\psi}^2 + 2mr \sin \theta \cos \theta \, \dot{\theta}\dot{\psi} + 2mr \cos \theta \, \dot{\theta}\dot{\psi} = 0 \]\n\nThe second and third terms of (a) correspond to centrifugal force. The second and third terms of (c) may be interpreted as Coriolis forces (with \(\dot{u}\) the angular velocity of the rotating coordinate system).
