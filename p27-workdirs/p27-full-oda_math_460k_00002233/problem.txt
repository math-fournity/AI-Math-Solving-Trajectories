Consider a graph $G$ with $n$ vertices. Define a labeling of $G$ as "special" if each vertex is labeled with a distinct positive integer not greater than $\frac{n^2}{4}$ and there exists a set of non-negative integers $D$ such that two vertices are connected by an edge if and only if the difference between their labels is in $D$. Let $N$ be the smallest integer such that there exists a graph with $N$ vertices that does not have a "special" labeling. Find the smallest possible value of $N$.
