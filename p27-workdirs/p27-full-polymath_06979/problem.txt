In a remote digital archipelago, a surveyor is mapping out rectangular regions of land partitioned into $a \times b$ identical square plots, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. In each region, every plot either contains a hidden seismic sensor or is a "safe zone." Each safe zone is assigned a "detection value" equal to the total number of seismic sensors located in its immediate vicinity (this includes all plots that share at least one edge or a corner with the safe zone).
