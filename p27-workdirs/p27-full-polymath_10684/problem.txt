In the coastal province of Aethelgard, there are 2023 lighthouse beacons arranged in a single straight line along the shore. Each beacon has a rotating shield that can either be "Open" (letting light through) or "Closed" (blocking the light). Currently, the beacons are set in an alternating pattern: the 1st beacon on the far left is Closed, the 2nd is Open, the 3rd is Closed, and so on, continuing down the line to the 2023rd beacon.
