10,11 At the base of the pyramid $SABC$ lies an isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$, in which $AD=2, BC=1$, the height of the trapezoid is 3. The height of the pyramid passes through the point $O$ of intersection of the diagonals of the trapezoid, $SO=\frac{\sqrt[4]{\sqrt{5}}}{}$. Point $F$ lies on the segment $SO$, such that $SF: FO=1: 3$. A cylinder, whose axis is parallel to the height $SM$ of the face $SAD$, is positioned such that point $F$ is the center of its upper base, and point $O$ lies on the circumference of the lower base.
