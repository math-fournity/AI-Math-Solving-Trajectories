Let \(A'\) be the orthogonal projection of \(A\) on \(EF\), \(B'\) the orthogonal projection of \(B\) on \(FD\), and \(C'\) the orthogonal projection of \(C\) on \(DE\). Let \(K\) be the midpoint of \(DA'\), \(L\) the midpoint of \(EB'\), and \(M\) the midpoint of \(FC'\). If the lines \(AK\), \(BL\), and \(CM\) intersect at a point \(P\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{[APK]}{[ABC]}\).
