# IMO 2007 A3

Let $F$ be a totally ordered field, and let $n$ be a positive integer.
Let $x, y \in F$ be positive elements such that $x^n + y^n = 1$.
Prove that
$$ \left(\sum_{k = 1}^n \frac{1 + x^{2k}}{1 + x^{4k}}\right)
  \left(\sum_{k = 1}^n \frac{1 + y^{2k}}{1 + y^{4k}}\right)
  < \frac{1}{(1 - x)(1 - y)}. $$
