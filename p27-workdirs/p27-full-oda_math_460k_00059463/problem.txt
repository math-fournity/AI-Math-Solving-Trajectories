Let \(ABC\) be an acute, non-isosceles triangle with angle bisectors \(AL_1\) and \(BL_2\) intersecting at point \(I\). Points \(D\) and \(E\) are chosen on segments \(AL_1\) and \(BL_2\) respectively such that \(\angle DBC = \frac{1}{2} \angle A\) and \(\angle EAC = \frac{1}{2} \angle B\). Lines \(AE\) and \(BD\) intersect at point \(P\). Point \(K\) is the reflection of point \(I\) over line \(DE\). Let \(Q\) be the intersection of lines \(KP\) and \(DE\). If the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\) has a radius \(R\), find the value of \(R \cdot \sin(\angle BQC)\).
