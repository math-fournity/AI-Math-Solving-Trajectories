In a futuristic data-transmission facility, a central computer generates streams of integer signals from the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. For any positive real-valued "frequency" $\beta$, a filtered sequence is generated by the rule $\lfloor \beta m \rfloor$ for all positive integers $m$. We define $K_\beta$ as the set of all integers produced by this rule.
