Consider a space figure with \( n \) vertices and \( l \) lines connecting these vertices, where \( n = q^2 + q + 1 \), \( l \ge q^2(q+1)^2 + 1 \), \( q \ge 2 \), and \( q \in \mathbb{N} \). Suppose every four vertices are non-coplanar, every vertex is connected by at least one line, and there is a vertex connected by at least \( p + 2 \) lines. Find the minimum value of \( p \) such that there is guaranteed to exist a space quadrilateral in the figure, i.e., a quadrilateral with four vertices \( A, B, C, D \) and four lines \( AB, BC, CD, DA \) in the figure.
