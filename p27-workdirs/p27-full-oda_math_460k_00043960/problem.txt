Let \(ABCD\) be a cyclic quadrilateral, and \(T\) be its anticenter. Let \(E\), \(F\), \(G\), and \(H\) be the feet of the perpendiculars from \(T\) to the orthic axes of triangles \(ABC\), \(BCD\), \(CDA\), and \(DAB\) respectively. If the circumradius of the circle passing through \(E\), \(F\), \(G\), and \(H\) is \(R\), and the circumradius of the circle \(ABCD\) is \(R_0\), find the value of \(\frac{R}{R_0}\).
