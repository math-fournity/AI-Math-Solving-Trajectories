In a remote industrial federation, $n$ distinct logistics hubs are interconnected by dedicated one-way cargo routes. Between every pair of hubs, there is exactly one direct route where one hub acts as the "Supplier" and the other as the "Recipient." For every shipment completed as a Supplier, a hub earns $1$ credit; for every shipment completed as a Recipient, it earns $0$ credits. (In this high-efficiency system, there are no split shipments or ties).
