Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle and let \(A_1, B_1, C_1\) be the feet of its altitudes. The incircle of the triangle \(A_1B_1C_1\) touches its sides at the points \(A_2, B_2, C_2\). If the Euler lines of triangles \(ABC\) and \(A_2B_2C_2\) intersect at a point \(P\), and the distance from \(P\) to the centroid \(G\) of triangle \(ABC\) is \(d\), find the value of \(d\) given that the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\) is \(R\) and the inradius of triangle \(A_1B_1C_1\) is \(r\).
