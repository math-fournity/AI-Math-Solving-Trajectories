A specialized logistics company manages several pairs of storage facilities, $(a, b)$, where $a$ and $b$ represent the whole-number capacities (in units) of two different warehouses. These facilities must satisfy a strict efficiency regulation: the sum of the squares of their capacities plus 2 must be perfectly divisible by the product of their capacities. Additionally, to avoid redundancy, the second warehouse $b$ must have a capacity at least as large as the first warehouse $a$.
