There are  $10$  cups, each having  $10$  pebbles in them. Two players  $A$  and  $B$  play a game, repeating the following in order each move: $\bullet$   $B$  takes one pebble from each cup and redistributes them as  $A$  wishes.  $\bullet$  After  $B$  distributes the pebbles, he tells how many pebbles are in each cup to  $A$ . Then  $B$  destroys all the cups having no pebbles. $\bullet$   $B$  switches the places of two cups without telling  $A$ .
