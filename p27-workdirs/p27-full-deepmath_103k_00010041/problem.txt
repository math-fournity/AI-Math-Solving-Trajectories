Given a differentiable function \( f(x,y) = z \) representing a three-dimensional frictionless surface, with gravity acting in the \(-z\) direction, consider a sphere of arbitrary mass and small radius tangent to a point \( P_0(x_0,y_0,z_0) \) on the surface. The sphere has an initial velocity vector \( \langle \frac{dx_{sphere}}{dt}, \frac{dy_{sphere}}{dt} \rangle \). For any point \( P_1(x_1,y_1,z_1) \) on the surface, is it possible to choose \( \frac{dx_{sphere}}{dt}|_{t=0} \) and \( \frac{dy_{sphere}}{dt}|_{t=0} \) such that the sphere becomes tangent to \( P_1 \) after some time? Ignore air resistance.
