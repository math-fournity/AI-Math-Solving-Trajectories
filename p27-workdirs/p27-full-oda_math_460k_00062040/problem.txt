Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcenter \(O\) and incenter \(I\). Let the symmedian points of triangles \(IBC\), \(ICA\), and \(IAB\) be \(X_A\), \(X_B\), and \(X_C\) respectively. Let the midpoints of segments \(AI\), \(IB\), and \(IC\) be \(M_A\), \(M_B\), and \(M_C\) respectively. Let \(P\) be the point of concurrency of lines \(M_AX_A\), \(M_BX_B\), and \(M_CX_C\). If the ratio of the distance from \(P\) to \(O\) to the distance from \(P\) to \(I\) is expressed as \(\frac{m}{n}\) in simplest form, find \(m+n\).
