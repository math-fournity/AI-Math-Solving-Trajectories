Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with orthocenter \(H\). Let \(E\) and \(F\) be the intersections of lines \(BH\) and \(CH\) with \(AC\) and \(AB\) respectively, and let \(D\) be the intersection of lines \(EF\) and \(BC\). Let \(\Gamma_1\) be the circumcircle of \(\triangle AEF\), and \(\Gamma_2\) the circumcircle of \(\triangle BHC\). The line \(AD\) intersects \(\Gamma_1\) at point \(I \neq A\). Let \(J\) be the foot of the internal bisector of \(\angle BHC\) and \(M\) the midpoint of the arc \(\stackrel{\frown}{BC}\) of \(\Gamma_2\) that contains \(H\). The line \(MJ\) intersects \(\Gamma_2\) at point \(N \neq M\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{IE}{CN}\).
