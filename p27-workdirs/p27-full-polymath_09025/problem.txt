A specialized logistics hub manages a fleet of 108 distinct delivery drones, indexed from 1 to 108. A supervisor needs to select a subset of these drones, $S$, to form an elite rapid-response squad. The selection must adhere to two strict operational protocols regarding the "shared maintenance protocols" of the drones. Two drones are said to share a protocol if their index numbers have a greatest common divisor (GCD) greater than 1; otherwise, they are considered "independent" (GCD is 1).
