In the city of Permutopia, the High Council regulates the security of digital locks. Each lock is defined by a set of three distinct encryption protocols, $\{f, g, h\}$. These protocols are specific types of software that rearrange a sequence of $n$ unique identification chips, $\{1, 2, \dots, n\}$, such that every chip is mapped to exactly one position (forming a mathematical bijection).
