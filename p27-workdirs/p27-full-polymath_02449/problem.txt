On the plane, \( n \geq 2 \) points are marked, no three of which lie on the same line, and the distances between any two of them are different. We call the segment \( AB \) strange if point \( B \) is the closest to point \( A \) of all the other marked points, and point \( A \) is the farthest from point \( B \) of all the marked points. Depending on \( n \), determine the largest possible number of strange segments.
