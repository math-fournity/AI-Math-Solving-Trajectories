Consider an infinite matrix of complex functions, each analytic on a domain $A \subseteq \mathbb{C}$. Assume that each row converges pointwise to an analytic function on $A$, and each column converges uniformly to an analytic function on $A$. Let $f_n$ denote the analytic function obtained by taking the limit along the $n$-th column. If $f_n$ converges pointwise to a function $g$ on $A$, find a necessary and sufficient condition on the convergence of the rows and columns for $g$ to be analytic on $A$. Express your answer as a statement about the uniform convergence of the rows and columns.
