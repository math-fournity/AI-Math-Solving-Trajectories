In a specialized logistics network, three supply hubs are positioned at coordinates $A$, $B$, and $C$. A straight delivery pipeline connects hubs $B$ and $C$. An auxiliary monitoring station, $D$, is located further down the straight line passing through $C$ and $B$ (in the order $D, B, C$). Station $D$ was specifically placed at the intersection of this line and the path that perfectly bisects the exterior angle at hub $A$ (the angle supplementary to $\angle BAC$).
