On a carousel with $n$ seats, a boy rode for $n$ consecutive sessions. After each session, he stood up and, moving clockwise, transferred to another seat. The number of seats the boy passes while transferring, including the one he sits on, is called the length of the transition. We say that $n$ is a "carousel number" if the boy could visit every seat during the $n$ sessions, such that the lengths of all $n-1$ transitions are distinct and strictly less than $n$.
