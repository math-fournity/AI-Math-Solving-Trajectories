Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with orthocenter \(H\) and circumcenter \(O\). Let \(BE\) and \(CF\) be the altitudes from \(B\) and \(C\) respectively. Let \(N\) and \(P\) be the midpoints of \(AC\) and \(AB\) respectively, and let \(U\) and \(V\) be the reflections of \(E\) and \(F\) with respect to \(N\) and \(P\) respectively. Let \(UV \cap AO = I\), \(ON \cap EF = Y\), \(OP \cap EF = Z\), \(BZ \cap CY = T\), and \(TO \cap EF = S\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{IS}{AT}\).
