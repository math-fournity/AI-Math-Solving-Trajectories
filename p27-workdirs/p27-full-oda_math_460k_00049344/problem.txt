Given triangle \(ABC\) inscribed in a circle \((O)\) with orthocenter \(H\). Let \(A_1\) be the point symmetric to \(H\) through \(A\). Draw \(AA' \parallel BC\) where \(A' \in (O)\). The tangents at \(A\) and \(A'\) intersect at \(X\). Determine similarly \(B_1, Y\) and \(C_1, Z\). Let \(P\) be the point of concurrency of \(A_1X\), \(B_1Y\), and \(C_1Z\). If the coordinates of \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) are \((1,0)\), \((0,1)\), and \((-1,-1)\) respectively, find the coordinates of \(P\).
