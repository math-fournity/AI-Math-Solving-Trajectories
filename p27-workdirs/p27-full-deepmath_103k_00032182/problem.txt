Let \( n \) be a fixed integer, \( n \geq 2 \). Find the smallest constant \( c \) such that the inequality \(\sum_{1 \leq i < j \leq n} x_{i} x_{j}\left(x_{i}^{2} + x_{j}^{2}\right) \leq c \cdot \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} \right)^{4} \) holds for all non-negative real numbers \( x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{n} \). Determine the conditions under which equality holds.
