In triangle \(ABC\), \(AM\) is a median with \(M \in BC\), and \(BB_1\) and \(CC_1\) are altitudes with \(C_1 \in AB\) and \(B_1 \in AC\). The line through \(A\) perpendicular to \(AM\) intersects \(BB_1\) and \(CC_1\) at points \(E\) and \(F\), respectively. Let \(k\) be the circumcircle of \(\triangle EFM\). Suppose \(k_1\) and \(k_2\) are circles that touch both \(EF\) and the arc \(EF\) of \(k\) not containing \(M\). If \(P\) and \(Q\) are the points where \(k_1\) intersects \(k_2\), calculate the length of segment \(PQ\) given that \(AM = 10\).
