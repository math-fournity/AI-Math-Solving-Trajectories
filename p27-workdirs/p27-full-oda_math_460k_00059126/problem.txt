A labyrinth consists of 2024 caves and 2023 non-intersecting bidirectional corridors, each connecting exactly two caves, such that each pair of caves is connected through some sequence of corridors. Initially, Erik is standing in a corridor connecting two caves. In a move, Erik can walk through one of the caves to another corridor that connects that cave to a third cave, causing the original corridor to disappear and a new one to appear between the start and end caves of his path. Let $N$ be the number of distinct labyrinth layouts (up to isomorphism) that Erik can achieve by performing a sequence of such moves, starting from any initial layout. Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by $1000$.
