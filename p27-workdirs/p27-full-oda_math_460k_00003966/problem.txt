A sphere touches the base plane $ABCD$ of a regular quadrangular pyramid $SABCD$ at point $A$ and also touches the inscribed sphere of the pyramid. A secant plane is drawn through the center of the first sphere and the side $BC$ of the base. Find the angle of inclination of this plane to the base plane, given that the diagonals of the section are perpendicular to the edges $SA$ and $SD$.
