In a remote industrial facility, a pressure monitoring system utilizes a series of four synchronized rotary gauges, each indexed with the digits 0 through 9. These gauges operate identically to a standard four-dial gas meter, where each dial represents a power of ten: the rightmost dial (units) completes a full rotation as the value increases by 10, the second dial (tens) completes one rotation for every 100 units, the third (hundreds) for every 1,000, and the leftmost (thousands) for every 10,000.
