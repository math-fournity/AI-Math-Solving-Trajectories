Let $F$ be the free group on two elements $x$ and $y$. Define $x^{-1}$ as $X$ and $y^{-1}$ as $Y$. Consider the elements $g_1 = xyxY$, $g_2 = XYXyxYxyXyxYXYXyxYxyxyXYY$, $g_3 = XYXyx$, $g_4 = YxyXyxYXYXyyx$, $g_5 = YxyXYxyxyX$, and $g_6 = xxYXXy$. Let $G$ be the subgroup of $F$ generated by $g_1, g_2, g_3, g_4, g_5, g_6$. Prove that $G$ is a free group of rank 6.
