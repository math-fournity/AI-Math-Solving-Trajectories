Consider a tree where the set of vertices is the integer lattice $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and any two vertices connected by an edge have a Euclidean distance of at most 2014. Let $P$ be the set of all pairs of lattice points that are exactly 1 unit apart. For each pair $(A, B) \in P$, let $d(A, B)$ denote the length of the path in the tree connecting $A$ and $B$. Find the minimum possible value of $\max_{(A, B) \in P} d(A, B)$.
