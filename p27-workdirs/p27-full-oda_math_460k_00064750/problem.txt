Given a non-right triangle \(ABC\) with \(BC > AC > AB\), two points \(P_1 \neq P_2\) on the plane satisfy that for \(i=1,2\), if \(AP_i, BP_i\), and \(CP_i\) intersect the circumcircle of triangle \(ABC\) at \(D_i, E_i\), and \(F_i\) respectively, then \(D_iE_i \perp D_iF_i\) and \(D_iE_i = D_iF_i \neq 0\). Let the line \(P_1P_2\) intersect the circumcircle of \(ABC\) at \(Q_1\) and \(Q_2\). The Simson lines of \(Q_1\) and \(Q_2\) with respect to \(ABC\) intersect at \(W\). Calculate the ratio of the length of segment \(WQ_1\) to the circumradius \(R\) of triangle \(ABC\).
