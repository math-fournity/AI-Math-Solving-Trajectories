Given an undirected graph \( G = (V, E) \) with edge costs \( c_e \geq 0 \) (not necessarily obeying the triangle inequality) and a parameter \( k \) such that \( |V| \) is a multiple of \( k \), the problem is to partition the graph into a collection of trees such that each tree has exactly \( 0 \mod k \) vertices, and each vertex is in exactly one tree. The goal is to find such a partition with minimum total cost. Provide a 2-approximation algorithm for this problem using the primal-dual method.
