In a remote digital archives facility, a specialized data compression algorithm generates a sequence of file sizes, $\{a_n\}$, measured in kilobytes. The size of any new file, $a_{n+1}$, is strictly determined by the two previous file sizes, $a_n$ and $a_{n-1}$. Specifically, the algorithm calculates the Least Common Multiple of the two previous sizes and divides it by their Greatest Common Divisor to produce the next value.
