Given an inscribed quadrilateral \(ABCD\), let \((O_1)\) be a circle passing through \(D\) and touching \(AB\) at \(A\), \((O_2)\) be a circle passing through \(C\) and touching \(AB\) at \(B\), \((O_3)\) be a circle passing through \(B\) and touching \(CD\) at \(C\), and \((O_4)\) be a circle passing through \(A\) and touching \(CD\) at \(D\). Calculate the radius of the circle passing through the points \(O_1, O_2, O_3, O_4\) if \(AB = 10\), \(BC = 15\), \(CD = 12\), and \(DA = 13\).
