Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcircle \(\Omega\), circumcenter \(O\), and orthocenter \(H\). Let \(S\) lie on \(\Omega\) and \(P\) lie on \(BC\) such that \(\angle ASP = 90^\circ\). Line \(SH\) intersects the circumcircle of \(\triangle APS\) at \(X \neq S\). Suppose \(OP\) intersects \(CA\) and \(AB\) at \(Q\) and \(R\), respectively, and \(QY\) and \(RZ\) are the altitudes of \(\triangle AQR\). If \(X, Y, Z\) are collinear, find the value of \(\frac{AX}{XS}\) given that \(AS = 10\) and \(XS = 6\).
