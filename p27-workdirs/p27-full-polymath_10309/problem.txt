A specialized microgrid consists of $2n+1$ power distribution nodes located at various integer coordinates on a city’s Cartesian grid. This grid is considered "fully integrated" if, for any two nodes $R$ and $S$, a maintenance crew can travel between them by moving through a sequence of nodes $R=T_0, T_1, \dots, T_l=S$ within the grid, such that the distance between any two consecutive nodes $T_i$ and $T_{i+1}$ is exactly 1 kilometer.
