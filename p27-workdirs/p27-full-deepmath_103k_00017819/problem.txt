Let \( G \) be a finite group, and let \( A \) be a symmetric subset containing the identity (i.e., \( A^{-1} = A \) and \( 1 \in A \)). The powers of \( A \) form a chain \( A \subsetneq A^2 \subsetneq A^3 \subsetneq \cdots \subsetneq A^d = \langle A \rangle \). Determine the optimal upper bound on \( d \) in terms of \( k \) such that \((k + 1)|A| > |G|\). For example, when \( 2|A| > |G| \), the optimal bound is \( d \leq 2 \), and when \( 3|A| > |G| \), it is \( d \leq 4 \). How does this bound change with \( k \)?
