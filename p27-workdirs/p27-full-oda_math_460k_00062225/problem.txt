Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with \(AB < AC < BC\) inscribed in circle \(c(O, R)\). The excircle \((c_A)\) has center \(I\) and touches the sides \(BC\), \(AC\), and \(AB\) of the triangle \(ABC\) at \(D\), \(E\), and \(Z\) respectively. \(AI\) intersects \((c)\) at point \(M\), and the circumcircle \((c_1)\) of triangle \(AZE\) intersects \((c)\) at \(K\). The circumcircle \((c_2)\) of triangle \(OKM\) intersects \((c_1)\) at point \(N\). Calculate the length of the segment \(AN\) if \(R = 10\) and \(OI = 6\).
