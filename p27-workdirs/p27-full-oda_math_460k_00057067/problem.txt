For a domain \( D \) in \(\mathbb{C}\), define\n\n\[\n\rho(x, y) = \sup\{|f(x) - f(y)| : x, y \in D; f \text{ analytic in } D; |f'| \leq 1 \text{ in } D\}.\n\]\n\nIf \( D \) is convex, then \(\rho(x, y) = |x - y|\), but not otherwise. Clearly \(\rho(x, y) \leq L(x, y)\), where \(L\) is the infimum of the lengths of paths in \(G\) that join \(x\) to \(y\). What can be said about \(\rho\) for general \(D\) in terms of the geometry of \(D\)?\n\n*(L.A. Rubel)*
