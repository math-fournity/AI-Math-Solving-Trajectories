In the futuristic city of Aethelgard, three specialized power generators—Alpha, Beta, and Gamma—contribute to a unified energy grid. The relative energy outputs of these generators are represented by positive rational fractions $x$, $y$, and $z$ respectively, such that their combined output $x + y + z$ is exactly $1$ unit. To maintain grid stability, the outputs must remain distinct and ordered, with Alpha producing the least and Gamma the most ($x < y < z$).
