Let $1 \leq p < \infty$. Consider a von Neumann algebra $\mathcal{M}$ with a normal semifinite faithful trace $\tau$. Let $L_p(\mathcal{M}, \tau)$ be the associated noncommutative $L_p$-space. Suppose $(x_n)$ is a sequence in $L_p(\mathcal{M}, \tau)$ such that $\|x_n - x\|_p \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$ for some $x \in L_p(\mathcal{M}, \tau)$. Determine whether it is true that $\||x_n| - |x|\|_p \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$. Provide a justification for your answer.
