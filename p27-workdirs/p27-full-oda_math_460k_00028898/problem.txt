Let \( A_p = \{ n^k \mid 1 \le n \le p-1 \} \) over \(\mathbb{Z}/p \mathbb{Z}\), where \( p \) is a prime and \( k \) is an integer. Find the smallest integer \( n \) such that for any prime \( p > n \), the set \( A_p + A_p \) contains the element \( 1 \). (Here, \( A + B = \{ a + b \mid a \in A, b \in B \} \)).
