A specialized logistics firm manages a fleet of $n$ cargo ships. The firm categorizes its operational capacity by identifying every positive integer $d$ that divides $n$ without a remainder, representing different "fleet configurations." These configurations are ranked by size as $d_{1}, d_{2}, \dots, d_{k}$, where $1 = d_{1}$ is the smallest possible unit and $d_{k} = n$ is the full fleet capacity.
