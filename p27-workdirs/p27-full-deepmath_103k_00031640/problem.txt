In the country of Dodecaedria, there are 20 cities connected by 30 airline routes. Fantomas, a fugitive, is hiding in one of these cities. Each day, he travels to another city using one of the airline routes. Every evening, the police discover his location. During the night, the police, with the help of the airline, close one existing airline route and open a new route between two cities that are not directly connected. Fantomas will be caught if he cannot travel to another city the next morning. Can the police devise a strategy to catch Fantomas?
