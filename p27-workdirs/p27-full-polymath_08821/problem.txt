In a massive circular data center, there are 2012 server racks arranged as the vertices of a perfectly regular 2012-gon inscribed within the perimeter. A network engineer needs to select a subset of $k$ racks to form a secure, closed transmission loop. To ensure maximum signal integrity and avoid interference, the layout must satisfy a specific geometric constraint: when the $k$ chosen racks are connected in order to form a convex $k$-gon, no two communication cables (the sides of the $k$-gon) can be parallel to one another.
