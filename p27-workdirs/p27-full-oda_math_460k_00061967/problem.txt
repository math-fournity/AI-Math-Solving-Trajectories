A regular pyramid $Q$ with a square base has side lengths of $a, b,$ and height $c$, with $a\le b\le c.$ A plane parallel to the base of $Q$ cuts $Q$ into two pyramids, one of which is similar to $Q,$ and both of which have nonzero volume. Given that $b=2000,$ for how many ordered triples $(a, b, c)$ does such a plane exist, assuming that the original pyramid $Q$ can be inscribed in a sphere with a radius of 2000?
