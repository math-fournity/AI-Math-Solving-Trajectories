A specialized logistics firm is tasked with auditing a high-security shipment of 52 distinct chemical canisters stored in a single-file pressurized tunnel. The audit team needs to determine the exact linear sequence of the canisters. However, because the tunnel is symmetrical and lacks orientation markers, the team only needs to identify the sequence up to a total reversal (i.e., they don't need to know which end is the "front" or "back").
