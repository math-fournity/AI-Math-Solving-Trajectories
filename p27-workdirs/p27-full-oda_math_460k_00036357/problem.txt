Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcenter \(O\). Let \(P\) and \(Q\) be two isogonal conjugates in the interior of \(ABC\), and let \(R\) be the reflection of \(O\) with respect to the midpoint of \(PQ\). Let \(S\) be the reflection of \(R\) with respect to the center of the nine-point circle. Let \(\omega\) be the circle passing through \(P\) and \(Q\) such that \(\omega\) is orthogonal to the circumcircle of \(ABC\). Let \((OP)\) and \((OQ)\) intersect \(\omega\) at \(P'\) and \(Q'\) respectively. Let \(P'Q\) and \(PQ'\) intersect at \(T\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{OS}{OT}\).
