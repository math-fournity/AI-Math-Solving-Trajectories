Consider triangle \(ABC\) with its circumcircle \((O)\). Let \(L\) be an arbitrary point on arc \(BC\) which does not contain \(A\). Let \(I_A\), \(I_L\), and \(I'_L\) be the centers of the A-mixtilinear incircle of triangle \(ABC\), the L-mixtilinear incircle of triangle \(LAB\), and the L-mixtilinear incircle of triangle \(LAC\), respectively. If the common tangent to these circles intersects \(BC\) at point \(P\), find the ratio \(\frac{BP}{PC}\) in terms of the side lengths \(AB = c\), \(AC = b\), and \(BC = a\).
