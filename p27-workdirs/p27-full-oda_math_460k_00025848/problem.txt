Let \(ABC\) be a triangle, \(P\) a point on the Euler line, and \(A'B'C'\) the pedal triangle of \(P\). Denote \(Na\), \(Nb\), and \(Nc\) as the Nine Point Circle (NPC) centers of triangles \(PB'C'\), \(PC'A'\), and \(PA'B'\), respectively. Calculate the ratio of the distance from the orthocenter \(H'\) of triangle \(NaNbNc\) to the orthocenter \(H\) of triangle \(ABC\) to the distance from \(H\) to the circumcenter \(O\) of triangle \(ABC\).
