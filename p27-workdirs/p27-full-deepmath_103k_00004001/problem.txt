Determine the asymptotic behavior of the function \( CL(n) \), where \( CL(n) = \max \{len_c(G) \mid |G| \leq n \} \) and \( len_c(G) \) is defined as the minimum sum of the sizes of supports of permutations in a generating set of a finite group \( G \) isomorphic to a subgroup of \( S_\infty \). Given the bounds \( CL(n) = O(n \log(n)) \) and \( CL(n) = \Omega(n) \), is either bound tight?
