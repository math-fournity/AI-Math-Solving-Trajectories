The line \(BC\) intersects the tangent line at \(A\) of \((O)\), the line \(EF\), and the line \(AO'\) at points \(T\), \(S\), and \(L\), respectively. The circle \((O)\) intersects \(AS\) again at \(K\). If the circumcenter of triangle \(AKL\) is \(M\), find the ratio \(\frac{AM}{AD}\).
