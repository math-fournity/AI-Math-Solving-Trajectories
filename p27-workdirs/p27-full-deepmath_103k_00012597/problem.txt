Let $X$ be a locally compact Hausdorff space, and let $K \subset U$ where $K$ is compact and $U$ is open. Find a function $f: X \to \mathbb{R}$ with compact support such that $1_K \leq f \leq 1_U$ on $X$. Given an open set $V$ with $K \subset V \subset \overline{V} \subset U$ where $\overline{V}$ is compact, construct a function $f: \overline{V} \to [0,1]$ that is $1$ on $K$ and $0$ on $\overline{V} \setminus V$. Extend $f$ to $X$ by setting $f = 0$ on $X \setminus \overline{V}$. Is this extended function $f$ continuous on $X$?
