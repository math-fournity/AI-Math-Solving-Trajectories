Let $X$ and $Y$ be locally convex spaces. Consider the biduals $X^{\prime \prime}$ and $Y^{\prime \prime}$ with the strong topologies $\beta(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime})$ and $\beta(Y^{\prime \prime}, Y^{\prime})$, respectively. Suppose $A: (X^{\prime \prime}, \beta(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime})) \rightarrow (Y^{\prime \prime}, \beta(Y^{\prime \prime}, Y^{\prime}))$ is a linear continuous map. Does this imply that $A : (X^{\prime \prime}, \tau_N(X^{\prime \prime}, X^{\prime})) \rightarrow (Y^{\prime \prime}, \tau_N(Y^{\prime \prime}, Y^{\prime}))$ is also continuous?
