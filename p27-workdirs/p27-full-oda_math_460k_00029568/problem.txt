Given a right triangle \(ABC\) with the right angle at \(C\), let \(D\) be the foot of the perpendicular from \(C\) to \(AB\). Let \(E\) be a point on segment \(CD\), and define lines \(L\) and \(L'\) through \(E\) parallel to \(BC\) and \(AC\) respectively. Suppose \(L\) intersects \(AC\) at \(A'\) and \(L'\) intersects \(BC\) at \(B'\). The circle \((B'CD)\) intersects \(L\) at \(M\) such that points \(B\) and \(M\) are on the same half-plane with respect to line \(AC\). Similarly, the circle \((A'DC)\) intersects \(L'\) at \(N\) such that points \(A\) and \(N\) are on the same half-plane with respect to line \(BC\). Let \(O'\) be the center of the circle through \(N\), \(C\), and tangent to \(L'\), and let \(O\) be the center of the circle through \(M\), \(C\), and tangent to \(L\). If \(AB = 13\), \(AC = 12\), and \(BC = 5\), find the length of segment \(OO'\).
