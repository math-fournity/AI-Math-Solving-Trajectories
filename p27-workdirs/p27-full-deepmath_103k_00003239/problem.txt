Let $X$ and $Y$ be two Banach spaces with respective norms $\|\cdot\|_X$ and $\|\cdot\|_Y$. Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are subsets of a vector space $Z$. Define $K(t,x)$ for $t\in (0,\infty)$ and $x\in X+Y$ by \[K(t,x)=\inf_{x=a+b,\ a\in X,\ b\in Y}\{\|a\|_X+t\|b\|_Y\}\] Assume that for a fixed $x$ and $p\in [1,\infty)$, \[\int_0^\infty \frac{|K(t,x)|^p}{t}dt<\infty.\] Can we conclude that $x=0$?
