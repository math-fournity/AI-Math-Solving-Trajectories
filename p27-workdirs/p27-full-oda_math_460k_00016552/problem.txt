Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with \(AB \neq AC\). Let \(D, E, F\) be the midpoints of the sides \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) respectively, and \(M, N\) be the midpoints of the minor arc \(BC\) not containing \(A\) and the major arc \(BAC\) respectively. Suppose \(W, X, Y, Z\) are the incenter, \(D\)-excenter, \(E\)-excenter, and \(F\)-excenter of triangle \(DEF\) respectively. Let \(P\) be the common point of intersection of the circumcircles of triangles \(ABC\), \(WNX\), and \(YMZ\). If the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\) is \(R\), find the value of \(\frac{OP}{R}\), where \(O\) is the circumcenter of triangle \(ABC\).
