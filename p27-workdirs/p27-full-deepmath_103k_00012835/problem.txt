Let $(\mathbb{X} \times \mathbb{Y}, \mathscr{X} \otimes \mathscr{Y})$ be a measurable product space obtained from measurable spaces $(\mathbb{X}, \mathscr{X})$ and $(\mathbb{Y}, \mathscr{Y})$. Define $\mathscr{A}$ as the $\sigma$-algebra formed by the cylinders of $\mathscr{X} \otimes \mathscr{Y}$ based on $\mathscr{X}$, i.e., $\mathscr{A} = \{X \times \mathbb{Y} : X \in \mathscr{X}\}$, with the agreement that $\emptyset \times \mathbb{Y} = \emptyset$. If $f: \mathbb{X} \times \mathbb{Y} \rightarrow [0, +\infty]$ is $\mathscr{A}$-measurable, is it true that $f(x, y_1) = f(x, y_2)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{X}$ and for all $y_1, y_2 \in \mathbb{Y}$?
