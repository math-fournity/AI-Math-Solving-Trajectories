Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle and let \(T\) be the reflection of \(A\) in the perpendicular bisector of \(BC\). Let \(A'\) be the \(A\)-antipode on the circumcircle \((ABC)\) and let \(P\) be the second intersection of \(AA'\) with the parabola through \(ABCT\). Let \(D\) be the point on \((ABC)\) such that \(AD \perp BC\) and let \(Q = PD \cap BC\). Let \(R\) be the intersection of \(AD\) with the tangent to \((ADC)\) at \(A'\). If \(M\) is the midpoint of \(BC\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{QR}{AM}\).
