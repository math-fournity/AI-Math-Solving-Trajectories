Determine if there is a known bound for the VC-dimension of the set of binary classifiers \(\mathcal{H} = \{\boldsymbol{x}\mapsto\boldsymbol{1}\hspace{-0.9ex}\mathrm{1}(\boldsymbol{h}^\mathsf{T}\boldsymbol{x}\ge 0)\mid  \boldsymbol{h} \in \mathcal{C} \}\), where \(\mathcal{C}\) is a pointed, salient, full-dimensional cone in \(\mathbb{R}^d\), that is significantly better than the VC-dimension of the set of all linear classifiers in \(\mathbb{R}^d\) (i.e., \(d\)).
