Given an acute triangle \(ABC\) with circumcircle \((O)\), let \(T\) be the antipode of \(A\) with respect to \((O)\). Let \(H\) and \(I\) be the orthocenter and incenter of \(ABC\), respectively. Let \(K\) and \(L\) be the points where the line perpendicular to \(AI\) through \(H\) intersects \((O)\). Let \(A'\) be the midpoint of the arc \(BC\) not containing \(A\). Let \(U\) and \(Y\) be the points where \(OA'\) intersects \(KL\) and \(TL\) intersects \(BC\), respectively. Calculate the ratio \(\frac{KA'}{UY}\).
