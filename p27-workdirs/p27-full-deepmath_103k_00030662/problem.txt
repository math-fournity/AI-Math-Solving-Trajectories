Find the optimal value of \( k_n \) in terms of \( n \) such that for any positive integer \( n \), the inequality \( \left(\sum_{i=1}^{3n} a_{i}\right)^{3} \geqslant k_{n}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{i} a_{n+i} a_{2n+i}\right) \) holds for all sequences \( 0 \leqslant a_{1} \leqslant a_{2} \leqslant \cdots \leqslant a_{3n} \).
