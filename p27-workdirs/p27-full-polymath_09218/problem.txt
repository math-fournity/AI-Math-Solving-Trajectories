A massive solar power grid is constructed in a perfect $100 \times 100$ kilometer square layout, divided into $10,000$ individual $1 \times 1$ kilometer square plots. To optimize energy collection, every single plot is split into two triangular sectors by a diagonal maintenance path. Crucially, all these maintenance paths are oriented in the same direction—specifically, they are all parallel to the main diagonal connecting the southeast corner to the northwest corner of the entire grid.
