Let \( R \rightarrow S \) be a local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings. Denote \( \mathfrak{m} \) the maximal ideal of \( R \). Let \( M \) be a flat \( R \)-module and \( N \) a finite \( S \)-module. Let \( u: N \rightarrow M \) be a map of \( R \)-modules. If \( \bar{u}: N / \mathfrak{m} N \rightarrow M / \mathfrak{m} M \) is injective, then \( u \) is injective. In this case, \( M / u(N) \) is flat over \( R \).
