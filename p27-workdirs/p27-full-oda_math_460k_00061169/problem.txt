Let \(ABC\) be a triangle and let \(P\) be a point in its plane not the same as \(A\), \(B\), or \(C\). Let the perpendicular to \(AP\) passing through \(P\) intersect \(AB\) and \(AC\) at \(A_1\) and \(A_2\). Define \(B_1\), \(B_2\), \(C_1\), and \(C_2\) similarly. Let \(f(P)\) be the eccentricity of the conic section passing through \(A_1\), \(A_2\), \(B_1\), \(B_2\), \(C_1\), and \(C_2\). If \(P\) is the orthocenter of triangle \(ABC\), find \(f(P)\).
