A specialized logistics software calculates a "Network Efficiency Rating," denoted as $\psi(n)$, for a server cluster containing $n$ nodes. The rating is determined by summing the number of shared protocols between a master controller and each node $k$ from 1 to $n$, where the number of shared protocols is defined as the greatest common divisor of $k$ and $n$. It is established that if two clusters have sizes that are coprime, the efficiency rating of their combined system is the product of their individual ratings.
