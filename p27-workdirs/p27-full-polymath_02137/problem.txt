Let $k$ be a natural number such that $1 < k < 100$. For an arbitrary permutation $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{100}$ of the numbers from $1$ to $100$, let $a_{101} = 0$. We choose the minimum index $m > k$ for which $a_m$ is less than at least $k-1$ of the numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_k$. Let $N_k$ be the number of permutations for which $a_m = 1$. Find the sum of all values of $k$ such that $N_k = \frac{100!}{4}$.
