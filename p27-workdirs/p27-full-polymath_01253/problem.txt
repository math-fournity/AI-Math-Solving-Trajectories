The sizes of the angles \( \angle B A J, \angle H G F, \angle F K J, \) and \( \angle D E C \) are denoted in this order as \( \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \) and \( \delta \). Determine the size \( \epsilon \) of the angle \( \angle D C E \).
