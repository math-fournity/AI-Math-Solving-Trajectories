A remote expedition team is setting up a communication network across a sharp, mountainous valley defined by three base camps: $A$, $B$, and $C$, which form an acute triangle. To power the equipment at camp $A$, they utilize a large circular solar grid, $\Gamma$, positioned as the $A$-excircle of the camp triangle (the circle tangent to the supply path $BC$ and the extensions of paths $AB$ and $AC$).
