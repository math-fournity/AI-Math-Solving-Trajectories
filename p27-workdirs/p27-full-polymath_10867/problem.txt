A high-security data center consists of a square server farm arranged in a grid of $110 \times 110$ individual server racks. A maintenance robot moves between these racks to perform repairs. From its current rack, the robot can move to any of the eight adjacent racks (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) in a single step. The distance between any two racks, $A$ and $B$, is defined as the minimum number of steps the robot requires to travel from $A$ to $B$.
