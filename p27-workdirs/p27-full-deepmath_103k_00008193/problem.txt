For a connected n-manifold M, the Lie algebra of all smooth vector fields is denoted by \( \chi^{\infty}(M) \). For a point \( p \in M \), we define \( L_{p} = \{ X \in \chi^{\infty}(M) \mid X(p) = 0 \} \). \( L_{p} \) is a Lie subalgebra of \( \chi^{\infty}(M) \) with codimension equal to \( n \). Is it true that every codimension-\( n \) Lie subalgebra of \( \chi^{\infty}(M) \) is necessarily in the form of (or isomorphic to) \( L_{p} \) for some \( p \in M \)?
