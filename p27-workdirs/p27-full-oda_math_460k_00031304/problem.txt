Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with circumcenter \(O\) and orthocenter \(H\). Point \(X\) lies on the circumcircle such that the Simson Line of \(X\) is perpendicular to \(OH\). The line \(OA\) intersects the circle \((BOC)\) at points \(O\) and \(D\). Point \(P\) lies on ray \(OA\) such that \(PO^2 = PA \cdot PD\). Denote by \(O'\) the reflection of \(O\) in \(X\). Calculate the value of \(\frac{PO}{PD}\).
