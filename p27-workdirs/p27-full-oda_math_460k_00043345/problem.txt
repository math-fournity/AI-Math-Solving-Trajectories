Determine the values of $r \in \mathbb{R}$ for which the series \[ S(r) = \sum_{x \in L^*_r} E_1(2 (x_1^2+x_2^2)) \exp((x_1^2+x_2^2) - (x_3^2+x_4^2)) \] is finite, where \( L^*_r = L_r \setminus W \), \( L_r = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a+cr \\ b+dr \\ c \\ d \end{pmatrix} : a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{Z} \right\} \), \( W = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ x_3 \\ x_4 \end{pmatrix} : x_3,x_4 \in \mathbb{R} \right\} \), and \( E_1(s) = \int_s^\infty \exp(-t) \frac{dt}{t} \).
