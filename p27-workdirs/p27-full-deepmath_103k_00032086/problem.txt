Consider the game where Arnaldo and Bernaldo alternate saying natural numbers, and the winner is the one who says 0. In each turn except the first, the possible moves are determined from the previous number \( n \) by writing \( n = \sum_{m \in O_n} 2^m \); the valid numbers are the elements \( m \) of \( O_n \). For example, after Arnaldo says \( 42 = 2^5 + 2^3 + 2^1 \), Bernaldo must respond with \( 5 \), \( 3 \), or \( 1 \).
