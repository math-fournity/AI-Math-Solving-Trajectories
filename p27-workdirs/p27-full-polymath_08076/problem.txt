A specialized microchip manufacturer is designing a triangular cooling heat-sink that must be placed entirely within a 1 mm by 1 mm square processing zone. The heat-sink is defined by three contact points, $A$, $B$, and $C$, which can be positioned anywhere on the boundary or in the interior of this square area. To optimize the flow of coolant, a circular induction pipe must be fitted inside the triangular heat-sink such that it is tangent to all three sides of the triangle.
