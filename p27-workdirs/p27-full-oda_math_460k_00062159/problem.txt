Let \(ABCD\) be a quadrilateral with non-parallel opposite sides, and let \(O\) be the point of intersection of its diagonals \(AC\) and \(BD\). Denote by \((XYZ)\) the circumcircle of any triangle \(XYZ\). Let \(A'\) be the point of intersection of \((OAB)\) and \((OCD)\) apart from \(O\). Let \(B'\) be the point of intersection of \((OBC)\) and \((ODA)\) apart from \(O\). Let the circles with diameters \(AC\) and \(BD\) intersect at \(C'\) and \(D'\). If the points \(A'\), \(B'\), \(C'\), \(D'\) lie on a circle, find the radius of this circle given that \(AC = 10\), \(BD = 14\), and the distance between the midpoints of \(AC\) and \(BD\) is \(8\).
