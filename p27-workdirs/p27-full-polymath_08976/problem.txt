In a bustling coastal city, a master architect is designing a $5 \times 5$ grid of luxury solar panels. Each panel has an efficiency rating of $1, 2, 3, 4,$ or $5$. To ensure the power grid remains balanced and resilient, the architect follows a strict zoning law: every single row, every single column, and both of the two main long diagonals must contain each of the five efficiency ratings exactly once.
