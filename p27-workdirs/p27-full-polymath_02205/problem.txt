\( \triangle ABC \) is a right triangle with \(\angle A = 30^\circ\) and circumcircle \( O \). Circles \(\omega_1, \omega_2, \omega_3\) lie outside \( \triangle ABC \) and are tangent to \( O \) at \( T_1, T_2, T_3 \) respectively and to \( AB, BC, \) and \( CA \) at \( S_1, S_2, S_3 \), respectively. Lines \( T_1S_1, T_2S_2, \) and \( T_3S_3 \) intersect \( O \) again at \( A', B', \) and \( C' \), respectively. What is the ratio of the area of \( \triangle A'B'C' \) to the area of \( \triangle ABC \)?
