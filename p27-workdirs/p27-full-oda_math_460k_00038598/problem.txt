Let \( f \) be a primitive polynomial with integer coefficients of degree \( n \). Suppose there exist distinct integers \( x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \) such that \( 0 < |f(x_i)| < \frac{k!}{2^k} \) where \( k = \left\lfloor \frac{n+1}{2} \right\rfloor \). If \( f \) is reducible in \( \mathbb{Z}[X] \), what is the minimum possible value of \( n \) for which such a polynomial \( f \) can exist?
