An architect is designing a series of observation decks for a futuristic city. Each deck is shaped like a perfectly regular $n$-sided polygon, where $n$ ranges from 3 up to 20. To support the structure, each deck must be partitioned into triangular sections using exactly $n-3$ straight steel beams (diagonals). These beams are installed such that they only meet at the corners of the polygon and never cross each other in the interior.
