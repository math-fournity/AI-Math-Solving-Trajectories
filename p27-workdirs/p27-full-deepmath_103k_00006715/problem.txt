Consider a full-dimensional convex polytope $P \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ with $n \ge 3$, having a bipartite edge-graph $G = (V_1 \cup V_2, E)$. Suppose there are concentric spheres $S_1$ and $S_2$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ such that all vertices of $V_i$ lie on $S_i$. If $|V_1| = |V_2|$, does it necessarily follow that $S_1 = S_2$, meaning that $P$ is inscribed?
