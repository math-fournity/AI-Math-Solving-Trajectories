In a specialized logistics center, a robotic conveyor belt operates based on a constant speed setting $x$ (a real number). For every second $k$ that passes, where $1 \leq k \leq 1000$, the system records a cumulative sensor reading $a_k$. This reading is determined by the floor function of the product of the time elapsed and the speed setting, such that $a_k = \lfloor kx \rfloor$. Any sequence of integers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{1000}$ that can be generated by some speed $x$ is categorized as a "stable transmission."
