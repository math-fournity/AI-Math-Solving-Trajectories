Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcircle \((O)\). Suppose that \(D\) is a point on segment \(BC\) such that \(AD \perp BC\) and suppose that \(AO \cap BC = D'\). Let a circle \(\omega_a\) pass through \(D\) and \(D'\) and touch \((O)\) at \(A'\), which is on the opposite side of \(BC\) from \(A\). Points \(B'\) and \(C'\) are defined similarly. Calculate the value of \(\frac{[AA' \cap BB' \cap CC']}{[ABC]}\), where \([XYZ]\) denotes the area of triangle \(XYZ\).
