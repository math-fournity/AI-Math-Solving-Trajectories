In a futuristic data-storage facility, engineers construct high-density server racks modeled as rectangular frames. For any positive integer $n$, a specific rack $K_n$ is built with dimensions $1 \times 1 \times n$ units. Along the four long edges of length $n$, the engineers have installed sensor nodes at every unit interval, including the corners. Additionally, a single central hub is located at each of the remaining four corners of the rack. In total, there is a set $R_n$ of $4n + 4$ potential node locations; however, because the corners are shared, the set $R_n$ consists of exactly $4n + 1$ distinct physical points.
