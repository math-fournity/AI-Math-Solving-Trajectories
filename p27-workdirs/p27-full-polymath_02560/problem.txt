Find the set of all natural numbers $d$ for which there exists an infinite arithmetic progression $a_{1}, a_{2}, \ldots$ of natural numbers with difference $d$ such that there exists a natural number $k$ for which for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the numbers $a_{S_{n+1}}, (n+k) a_{k}, -a_{S_{n}}$ form (in this order) an arithmetic progression, where $S_{n} = \sum_{i=1}^n a_i$. Let $D$ be the set of all such $d$. Find the sum of all $d \in D$ such that $d \le 100$.
