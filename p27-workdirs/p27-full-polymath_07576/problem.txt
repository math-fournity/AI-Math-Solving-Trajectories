In a vast, flat desert, a central observation hub $O$ monitors four remote solar stations, $A_1, A_2, A_3$, and $A_4$. To ensure signal clarity and prevent interference, the surveillance team maintains a strict protocol: for any pair of solar stations $A_i$ and $A_j$ (where $1 \leq i < j \leq 4$), the triangular region formed by the hub $O$ and those two stations, denoted as $\text{Region}(OA_iA_j)$, must have an area of at least 1 square kilometer.
