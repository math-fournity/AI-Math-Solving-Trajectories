Define the degree of an interesting equation \( P(x) = Q(y) \) by \( \max\{\deg(P), \deg(Q)\} \). An interesting equation is called primary if there is no other interesting equation with a lower degree that yields in it. Let \( P(x) \) and \( Q(y) \) be monic polynomials such that \( P(x) = Q(y) \) is a primary interesting equation. If \( \deg(P) = 12 \) and \( \deg(Q) = 16 \), find the smallest positive integer \( k \) such that \( \deg(P^k) \) and \( \deg(Q^k) \) are coprime.
