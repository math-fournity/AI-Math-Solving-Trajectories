A kangaroo is jumping in the quadrant $x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0$ of the coordinate plane $Oxy$ in the following manner: from the point $(x, y)$, the kangaroo can jump to the point $(x-5, y+7)$ or to the point $(x+1, y-1)$. However, jumping to points where one of the coordinates is negative is not allowed. From which initial points $(x, y)$ can the kangaroo not reach a point that is more than 1000 units away from the origin? Draw the set of all such points $(x, y)$ and determine its area.
