Consider a function $u$ in $C^{0,1}(\overline{B(0,1)})$ such that $u(0) = 0$ and $[u]_{C^{0,1}(B(0,1))} \leq M$ for some $M > 0$. Define $u^r(x) = r^{-1}u(rx)$ for $0 < r \leq 1$. It is known that $[u^r]_{C^{0,1}(B(0,1))} \leq M$ and $u^r(0) = 0$. Using the Ascoli-Arzelà lemma, a subsequence $\{r_j\}$ can be chosen such that $r_j \to 0$ as $j \to \infty$ and $u^{r_j} \to u^0$ uniformly in $C^0(\overline{B(0,1)})$. Is the limit $u^0$ unique?
