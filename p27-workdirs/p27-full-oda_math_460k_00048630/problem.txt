March 14, 1958; Boris Gnedenko (Kiev) (in Russian)\nLet \( \xi_1, \xi_2, \ldots, \xi_n, \ldots \) be mutually independent random variables such that for some positive constants \( b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n, \ldots \), the law of the random variable \( b_n \xi_n \) is independent of \( n \). Find the class of limit laws of the sums\n\[\ns_n = \frac{\xi_1 + \cdots + \xi_n}{B_n} - A_n,\n\]\nwith some constants \( A_n \) and \( B_n \to \infty \) as \( n \to \infty \).
