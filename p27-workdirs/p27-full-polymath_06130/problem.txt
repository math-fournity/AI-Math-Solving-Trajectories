A specialized thermal regulation system utilizes three distinct non-zero temperature sensors—Sensor $X$, Sensor $Y$, and Sensor $Z$—each recording a real number value ($x, y,$ and $z$). To ensure system stability, the safety protocol requires that for any possible set of readings, there must exist at least one pair of sensors (let’s call their readings $a$ and $b$) that satisfies a "proximity threshold."
