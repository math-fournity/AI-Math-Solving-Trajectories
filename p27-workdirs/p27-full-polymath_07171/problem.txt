In the high-tech logistics center of the "Millennium Network," a signal transmitter generates a complex wave pattern over a specific operational timeframe. The integrity of the signal is determined by a master function, $f(x)$, which is calculated as the product of 2,000 individual oscillating sub-signals. Each sub-signal $i$ (where $i$ ranges from 1 to 2,000) is defined by the frequency component $\cos(x/i)$.
