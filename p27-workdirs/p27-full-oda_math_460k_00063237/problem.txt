For a pair \( a, b \) of integers with \( 0 < a < b < 1000 \), the subset \( S \) of \( \{1, 2, \ldots, 2003\} \) is called a skipping set for \( (a, b) \) if \( |s_1 - s_2| \notin \{a, b\} \) for any \( (s_1, s_2) \in S^2 \). Let \( f(a, b) \) be the maximum size of a skipping set for \( (a, b) \). Determine the maximum and minimum values of \( f \).\n\nZuming Feng, USA TST 2003
