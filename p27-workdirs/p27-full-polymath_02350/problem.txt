Find all pairs $(m, n)$ of positive integers with $1 \le m, n \le 10$ such that each unit square of an $m \times n$ grid can be colored with black and white in a way that for each unit square, the number of unit squares that have the same color and share at least one common vertex (including itself) is even. Let $S$ be the set of all such pairs $(m, n)$. Calculate the number of elements in $S$.
