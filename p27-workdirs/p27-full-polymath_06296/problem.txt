A specialized architecture firm is designing a series of modular artistic pavilions. Each pavilion is a "triangulated shell"—a convex 3D structure where every single exterior panel is a triangle. The series consists of 8 different pavilions, categorized by the total number of triangular panels used: the first pavilion has $2n$ panels where $n=3$, the second where $n=4$, and so on, up to the eighth pavilion where $n=10$.
