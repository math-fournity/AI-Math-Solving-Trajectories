Let \( A \) be a symmetric matrix with entries being polynomials \( f_{ij}(X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{m}) \) with real coefficients in \( m \) variables. Suppose that for any real numbers \( x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{m} \), the matrix with coefficients \( f_{ij}(x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{m}) \) is positive semidefinite. Express \( A \) as a sum of squares of symmetric matrices with entries in \( \mathbb{R}[X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{m}] \) and find the minimum number of such matrices needed for \( m = 3 \).
