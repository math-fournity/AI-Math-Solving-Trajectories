Let \( K \) be a number field and \( O_K \) be the ring of algebraic integers in \( K \). Suppose \( R \subseteq O_K \) is a subring whose quotient field is equal to \( K \). Define \( f(n) \) as the maximum order of any element in the group \( O_K^*/R^* \) under multiplication, where \( O_K^* \) and \( R^* \) are the multiplicative groups of units of \( O_K \) and \( R \), respectively. Calculate \( f(2) \).
