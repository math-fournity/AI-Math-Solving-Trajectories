Consider the (strong) Bruhat order, \(\leq_B\), on the symmetric group \(S_n\). Suppose there are permutations \(\pi,\sigma \in S_n\) such that \(\pi \geq_B \sigma\). Additionally, if \(i\) precedes \(j\) in \(\sigma\) written in single line notation, then \(i\) precedes \(j\) in \(\pi\) written in single line notation. Does there exist a maximal chain in the Hasse diagram of the Bruhat order from \(\sigma\) to \(\pi\), say \(\sigma \leq_B \sigma_2 \leq_B \cdots \leq_B \sigma_k = \pi\), such that each \(\sigma_i\) satisfies the same property with respect to \(\sigma\) for all \(1 \leq i \leq k\)?
