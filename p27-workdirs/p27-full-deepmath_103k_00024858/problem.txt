Determine a condition on the integer $N$ such that an $N \times N$ matrix, constructed as follows, has full row and column rank. For each entry in the matrix, compute $v$ by multiplying the row number with the column number. Convert $v$ to binary, and for each bit $v_b$, raise the $b$th prime number to the power of $v_b$. For example, the entry at row 5 and column 7 would be $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 13 = 78$, and at row 4 and column 2 would be $7$. What is the condition on $N$ for the matrix to have full rank?
