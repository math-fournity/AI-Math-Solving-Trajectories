Alice and Bob play a game using two thousand and eleven $2011 \times 2011$ grids. Bob has 1 grid, while Alice has 2010 grids. They fill their grids with the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 2011^{2}$, such that the numbers across rows and down columns are strictly increasing. No two of Alice's grids are identical. After filling the grids, Bob looks at all of Alice's grids and makes swaps on his own grid two numbers at a time, ensuring the grid remains strictly increasing across rows and down columns after each swap. Once Bob finishes swapping, one of Alice's grids is selected at random. If any two integers in the same column of the selected grid also appear in the same row of Bob's grid, Bob wins. Otherwise, Alice wins. Given Bob selects his initial grid optimally, what is the maximum number of swaps Bob may need to guarantee victory?
