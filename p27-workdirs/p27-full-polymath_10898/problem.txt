In a remote sector of the ocean, a circular exclusion zone named Omega is monitored by a central radar station at point $O$. The zone has a detection radius of exactly 8 miles. Two long, straight underwater fiber-optic cables, $AC$ and $BD$, are laid such that they intersect at a right angle within the zone. Sensor readings confirm that the cable $AC$ spans a length of $2\sqrt{61}$ miles, while the cable $BD$ spans $6\sqrt{7}$ miles.
