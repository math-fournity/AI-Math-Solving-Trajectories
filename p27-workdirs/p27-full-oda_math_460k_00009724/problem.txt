In triangle \(ABC\) with \(AB > AC\), the incircle \(\odot I\) is tangent to sides \(AB\), \(BC\), and \(CA\) at points \(F\), \(D\), and \(E\) respectively. Let \(M\) and \(N\) be the midpoints of \(DE\) and \(DF\) respectively, and let \(FE\) intersect \(BC\) at \(P\). A moving line through \(P\) intersects \(\odot I\) at points \(G\) and \(H\) such that \(IMG\) and \(INH\) are not collinear. Let \(Q\) be the intersection of \(\odot(IMG)\) and \(\odot(INH)\). Calculate the radius of the circle on which all possible points \(Q\) lie.
