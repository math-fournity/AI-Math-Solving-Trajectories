Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with incentre \(I\) and circumcircle \(\Omega\) such that \(|AC| \neq |BC|\). The internal angle bisector of \(\angle CAB\) intersects side \(BC\) at \(D\), and the external angle bisectors of \(\angle ABC\) and \(\angle BCA\) intersect \(\Omega\) at \(E\) and \(F\) respectively. Let \(G\) be the intersection of lines \(AE\) and \(FI\), and let \(\Gamma\) be the circumcircle of triangle \(BDI\). If \(E\) lies on \(\Gamma\), find the value of \(\frac{EG}{GI}\).
