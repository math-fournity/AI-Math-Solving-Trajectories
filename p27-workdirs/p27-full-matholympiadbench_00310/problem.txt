# IMO 2016 N5

Fix some $k, a ∈ ℤ$ with $k ≥ 0$ and $a > 0$.
A pair $(x, y) ∈ ℤ^2$ is called *nice* if $(k + 1) y^2 - k x^2 = a$.
Prove that the following two statements are equivalent:
* There exists a nice pair $(x, y)$ with $x ≥ 0$ and $x^2 > a$;
* There exists a nice pair $(x, y)$ with $x ≥ 0$ and $x^2 ≤ a$.
