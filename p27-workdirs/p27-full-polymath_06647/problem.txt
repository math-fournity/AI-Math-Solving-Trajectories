In the competitive world of logistics, a company manages several regional hubs. For a specific shipping network, let $n$ represent the total storage capacity of a main warehouse, where $n$ is a positive integer. This capacity $n$ is unique because it has exactly twelve distinct integer scaling factors (divisors) which represent the possible sizes of standardized shipping containers that can perfectly divide the warehouse space. These factors are ordered by size as $1=d_1 < d_2 < \dots < d_{12}=n$.
