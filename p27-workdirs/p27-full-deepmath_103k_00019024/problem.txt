Consider a time-homogeneous Markov chain $X=(X_n)_{n\ge 1}$ on a countable state space $S$. If $X$ is irreducible, recurrent, and aperiodic, does it necessarily follow that $P(X=s)=P(X_1=s_1,X_2=s_2,\ldots)=0$ for any nonrandom sequence $s=(s_1,s_2,s_3,\ldots)\in S^\infty$? Provide a justification for your answer.
