A high-security data vault is organized into a square grid of $n \times n$ storage lockers, where $n$ and $k$ are positive integers such that $n > k > 1$. The facility director wants to place thermal cooling units inside certain lockers. To prevent the cooling units from interfering with one another's sensors, a placement is considered "stable" only if no two lockers containing a cooling unit share a common wall or even a single corner.
