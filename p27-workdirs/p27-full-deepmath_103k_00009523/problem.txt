Let $x$ be an infinite set and let $\text{Part}(x)$ be the collection of all partitions of $x$. For a partition $P \in \text{Part}(x)$ and a subset $t \subseteq x$, define $P_{[t]} = \{p \in P : p \cap t \neq \emptyset\}$. The tiling relation on $\text{Part}(x)$ is defined by $P \triangleleft Q$ if and only if for all $S \subseteq P$, we have $\mathsf{card}(S) \leq \mathsf{card}(Q_{[\bigcup S]})$. Let $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \text{Part}(x)$ be a non-empty collection of partitions such that there is a set $M$ of subsets of $x$ where every member of $\mathcal{A}$ refines $M$. Is there a partition $Z \in \text{Part}(x)$ such that $Z$ refines $M$ and $Z \triangleleft A$ for all $A \in \mathcal{A}$?
