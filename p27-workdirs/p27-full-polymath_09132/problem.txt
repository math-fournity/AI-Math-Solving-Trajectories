An architect is designing a commemorative plaza in the shape of a five-sided polygon named $JAMES$. To ensure the aesthetic symmetry of the monument, the city council has mandated that four of its boundary walls must be of equal length: specifically, the sections $JA$, $AM$, $ME$, and $ES$ must each measure exactly 2 decameters. There are no restrictions on the length of the final boundary segment, $SJ$.
