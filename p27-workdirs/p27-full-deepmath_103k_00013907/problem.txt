Suppose \(\{a_n\}\) is a sequence of non-negative real numbers such that \(a_n = O(n^M)\) for a positive number \(M\), and its Dirichlet series \(L(s)=\sum \frac{a_n}{n^s}\) has an analytic continuation to a meromorphic function on \(\mathbb{C}\) with only a simple pole at a positive real number \(a\) with residue \(A\). Using a standard Tauberian theorem, determine the maximum value of \(\delta\) for which the asymptotic formula \(\sum_{n\leq x} a_n = Ax^a + O(x^{a-\delta})\) holds.
