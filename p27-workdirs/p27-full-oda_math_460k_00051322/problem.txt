Find the largest value of the real number \( \lambda \) such that for any point \( P \) inside an acute triangle \( ABC \) satisfying \( \angle PAB = \angle PBC = \angle PCA \), and where rays \( AP \), \( BP \), \( CP \) intersect the circumcircle of triangles \( PBC \), \( PCA \), \( PAB \) at points \( A_1 \), \( B_1 \), \( C_1 \) respectively, the inequality \( S_{A_1BC} + S_{B_1CA} + S_{C_1AB} \geq \lambda S_{ABC} \) holds.
