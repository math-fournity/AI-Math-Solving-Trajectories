Mudkip constructs an array of right triangles where each horizontal triangle is congruent to the vertical triangle directly above it, and all triangles in the array are similar. The initial triangle has leg lengths \(0 < a < b < 100\), and the arrangement stops with the last triangle that has all integer leg lengths. Let \(m\) be the maximum possible number of triangles in his array, and let \(A_m\) be the maximum total area of an array with \(m\) triangles. Find \(m + A_m\).
