Three wooden equilateral triangles of side length 18 inches are placed on axles. Each axle is 30 inches from the other two axles. A 144-inch leather band is wrapped around the wooden triangles, and a dot at the top corner is painted. The three triangles are then rotated at the same speed, and the band rotates without slipping or stretching. Compute the length of the path that the dot travels before it returns to its initial position at the top corner.
