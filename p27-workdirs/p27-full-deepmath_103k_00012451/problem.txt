Consider a lattice \(\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{Z}v_1 \oplus \ldots \oplus \mathbb{Z}v_l\) in \(\mathbb{R}^n\) and let \(S_0\) be the set of edges of the fundamental unit of \(\mathcal{L}\). A region \(X\) is lattice path connected if for every two lattice points \(P\) and \(Q\), there exists a path of lattice points \(P = M_1, M_2, \ldots, M_{t - 1}, M_t = Q\) with \(M_i = M_{i-1} + u_i\) and \(u_i \in S_0\) and \(M_i \in X\). Let \(P \subset \langle v_1, \ldots, v_l \rangle\) be a convex \(k\)-dimensional polytope. Suppose the supporting linear space of \(P\) has a spanning set \(\mathbf{S}\) which is a subset of \(S_0\), and every codimension 1 face has a spanning set which is a subset of \(\mathbf{S}\). Is it true that \(P\) is lattice path connected?
