Example 5 Let $A=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}, B=\{7,8,9, \cdots, n\}$. Take 3 numbers from $A$ and 2 numbers from $B$ to form a set $A_{i}(i=1,2, \cdots, 20)$ with 5 elements, such that $\left|A_{i} \cap A_{j}\right| \leqslant 2,1 \leqslant i<j \leqslant 20$, find the minimum value of $n$.
