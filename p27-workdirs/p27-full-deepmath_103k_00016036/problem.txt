Evaluate the integral \( I_{k} = \int_{t = 0}^{1}\int_{y = y_{k}}^{y_{k+1}}\int_{x = x_{k}}^{x_{k+1}} \left\{\dfrac{y}{t}\right\}\left\{\dfrac{t}{x}\right\} dt dy dx \) where \( x_{k} = y_{k} = \frac{k}{n} \), \( k = 0,\dots,n-1 \) and \( n \geqslant 2 \). Express the result in terms of \( h = \frac{1}{n} \) and \( k \). Here, \( \{z\} \) denotes the fractional part of \( z \) (\( z > 0 \)), i.e., \( \{z\} = z - \lfloor z\rfloor \) where \( \lfloor z \rfloor \) is the greatest integer less than or equal to \( z \).
