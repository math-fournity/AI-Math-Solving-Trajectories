Given an acute triangle \(ABC\) with orthocenter \(H\), orthic triangle \(DEF\), and circumcircle \((O)\). Let \(P\) be the intersection of \(EF\) and \(AH\). Let \(Y\) and \(Z\) be the second points of intersection of \((O)\) with lines \(BP\) and \(CP\) respectively. Let \(X\) be the intersection of \(YE\) and \(ZF\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{AX}{AO}\) where \(O\) is the circumcenter of triangle \(ABC\).
