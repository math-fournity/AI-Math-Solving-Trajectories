For a fixed real number \(0 \leq p \leq 1\), the series \(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^i}{i^p}\) converges. Find the maximum real number \(q\) such that for all permutations \(k_n\) of \(\mathbb{N}\) satisfying \(\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{k_n - n}{n^q} = 0\), the rearranged series \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{k_n}}{k_n^p}\) converges.
