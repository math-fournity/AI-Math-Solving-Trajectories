Let $V$ be a real $d$-dimensional vector space, and let $1 < k < d$ be fixed. Consider two bases $v_i$ and $w_i$ for $V$. The basis $v_i$ induces a basis for the $k$-th exterior power $\bigwedge^k V$, given by $v_{i_1} \wedge \dots \wedge v_{i_k}$. Suppose that for each strictly increasing multi-index $I = (i_1, \ldots, i_k)$, there exists a unique multi-index $J = (j_1, \ldots, j_k)$ such that $v^I = \lambda_I w^J$ for some non-zero scalar $\lambda_I$. Is it true that there exists a permutation $\sigma \in S_d$ such that $\text{span}(v_i) = \text{span}(w_{\sigma(i)})$ for all $i$?
