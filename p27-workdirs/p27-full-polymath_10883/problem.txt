In a remote logistics hub, there are 68 specialized shipping containers, each containing a set of two calibrated weights (non-zero integers). Because of specific balancing protocols, for any positive integer $k$, it is impossible for the hub to house both a container with weights $(k, k)$ and a container with weights $(-k, -k)$ simultaneously; at most, only one such type of container exists in the inventory. Note that among the 68 containers, some may contain identical pairs of weights.
