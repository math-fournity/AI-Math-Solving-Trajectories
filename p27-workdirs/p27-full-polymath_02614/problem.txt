For any positive integer $n$, let $d(n)$ denote the number of positive divisors of $n$. Determine the condition that a positive integer $k$ must satisfy such that there exists a positive integer $n$ satisfying $\frac{d(n^2)}{d(n)} = k$. Specifically, find the set of all such integers $k$. Provide your answer in terms of a property that $k$ must satisfy (e.g., if $k$ is even, your answer would be $2m$; if $k$ is odd, your answer would be $2m-1$ for $m \in \mathbb{N}$).
