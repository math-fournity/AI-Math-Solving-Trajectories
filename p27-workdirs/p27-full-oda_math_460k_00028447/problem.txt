Consider triangle \(ABC\) and a point \(P\) such that the cevian triangle \(A'B'C'\) of \(P\) with respect to \(ABC\) is orthologic to \(ABC\). Let \(\mathcal{E}\) be a conic which is tangent to the sides of \(ABC\) at \(A', B', C'\). Let \(P, Q, R, T\) be the intersection points of \(\mathcal{E}\) with the nine-point circle of \(ABC\). If the perpendiculars from \(P, Q, R, T\) to \(\mathcal{E}\) meet at a point \(Q\), calculate the sum of the coordinates of \(Q\) assuming \(A = (0,0)\), \(B = (1,0)\), and \(C = (0,1)\).
