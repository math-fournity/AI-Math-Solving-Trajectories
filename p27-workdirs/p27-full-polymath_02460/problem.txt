Points \( A \) and \( L \) lie outside circle \(\omega\), whose center is \( O \), and \(\overline{A L}\) contains diameter \(\overline{R M}\). Circle \(\omega\) is tangent to \(\overline{L K}\) at \( K \). Also, \(\overline{A K}\) intersects \(\omega\) at \( Y \), which is between \( A \) and \( K \). If \( K L=3, M L=2\), and \(\angle A K L - \angle Y M K = 90^{\circ}\), compute \([A K M]\) (i.e., the area of \(\triangle A K M\)). If the answer is of the form of an irreducible fraction $\frac{a}{b}$, compute the value of $a + b$.
