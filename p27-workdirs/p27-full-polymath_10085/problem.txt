In a futuristic metropolis, an advanced central computer monitors a grid of 2011 power substations and 2011 data hubs, forming a massive $2011 \times 2011$ network. Currently, every one of the 2011 horizontal power lines and every one of the 2011 vertical data channels exhibits exactly the same total energy throughput. This is because a technician named Cain has configured the integer power units at each intersection point such that the 4022 total sums (representing the 2011 rows and 2011 columns) are all identical.
