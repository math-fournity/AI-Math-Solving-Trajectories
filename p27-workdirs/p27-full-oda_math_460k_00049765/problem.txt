$\pm$ Find all positive real numbers $a$ such that there exists a positive integer $n$ and $n$ pairwise disjoint infinite sets $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n}$ satisfying $A_{1} \cup A_{2} \cup \cdots \cup A_{n}=\mathbf{Z}$, and for any two numbers $b>c$ in each $A_{i}$, we have $b-c \geqslant a^{i}$. (Yuan Hanhui)
