A fly is being chased by three spiders on the edges of a regular octahedron. The fly moves at a speed of 50 meters per second, while each spider moves at a speed of $r$ meters per second. The spiders can choose their starting positions and the fly's starting position, with the condition that the fly must start at a vertex. All bugs are aware of each other's positions at all times. Determine the maximum value of $c$ such that for any $r < c$, the fly can always avoid being caught by the spiders.
