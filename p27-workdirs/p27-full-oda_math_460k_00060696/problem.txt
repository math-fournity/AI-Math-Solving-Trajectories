Let \(ABC\) be a triangle and \(P\) be an arbitrary point on the plane. Let \(P^{*}\) be the isogonal conjugate of \(P\) with respect to \(ABC\). Let \(O\) be the circumcenter of \(\triangle ABC\). Given that the orthopole of \(OP\) with respect to \(\triangle ABC\) is the center of the rectangular hyperbola passing through \(A\), \(B\), \(C\), and \(P\), find the coordinates of this center in terms of the coordinates of \(A\), \(B\), \(C\), and \(P\). Assume \(A = (x_1, y_1)\), \(B = (x_2, y_2)\), \(C = (x_3, y_3)\), and \(P = (x_0, y_0)\).
