Let \(ABC\) be a triangle inscribed in a circle \((O)\) with incircle \((I)\) tangent to \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) at \(D\), \(E\), and \(F\) respectively. Let \(B_1 = BI \cap (O) \neq B\) and \(C_1 = CI \cap (O) \neq C\). Let \(EF \cap (O) = X_1, X_2\). Calculate the ratio of the length of the segment joining the circumcenter of \(\triangle DX_1X_2\) to the midpoint of \(C_1B_1\) to the radius of the circumcircle \((O)\).
