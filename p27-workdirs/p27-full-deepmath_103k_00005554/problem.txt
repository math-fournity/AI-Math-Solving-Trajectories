Let $X$ be a projective variety with two morphisms $f:X\rightarrow Y$ and $g:X\rightarrow Z$ with irreducible fibers of positive dimension. Assume that $Pic(X) = f^{*}Pic(Y)\oplus g^{*}Pic(Z)$. If $D$ is a divisor on $X$, we can write $D = f^{*}D_Y + g^{*}D_Z$, where $D_Y$ and $D_Z$ are divisors on $Y$ and $Z$ respectively. If $D$ is effective, are $D_Y$ and $D_Z$ effective as well?
