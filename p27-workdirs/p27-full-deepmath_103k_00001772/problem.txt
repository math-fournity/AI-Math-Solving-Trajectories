Determine whether there exists a hereditary $\sigma$-space $X$ that is not a $Q$-space. A topological space $X$ is a $\sigma$-space if every $F_{\sigma}$-subset of $X$ is $G_{\delta}$. A $Q$-space is a topological space where any subset is $F_{\sigma}$. A hereditary $\sigma$-space is one where every subset is a $\sigma$-space.
