Four, (50 points) Given a convex 20-gon $P$. Use 17 non-intersecting diagonals inside $P$ to divide $P$ into 18 triangles, and the resulting figure is called a triangulation of $P$. For any triangulation $T$ of $P$, the 20 sides of $P$ and the added 17 diagonals are all called the edges of $T$. Any set of 10 edges of $T$ that have no common endpoints is called a perfect matching of $T$. When $T$ ranges over all triangulations of $P$, find the maximum number of perfect matchings of $T$.
