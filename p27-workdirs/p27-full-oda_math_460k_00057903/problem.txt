Given a triangle \(ABC\) with circumcenter \(O\), a line \(\ell\) through \(O\) intersects \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) at \(A_0\), \(B_0\), and \(C_0\) respectively. Construct the paralogic triangle \(A'B'C'\) of triangle \(ABC\) with respect to \(\ell\), and let \(O'\) be the circumcenter of \(A'B'C'\). Let \(A_b\) and \(A_c\) be the orthogonal projections of \(A_0\) on \(A'B'\) and \(A'C'\) respectively, and let \(A_a\) be the midpoint of \(A_bA_c\). Define \(B_b\) and \(C_c\) similarly. Calculate the length of the segment \(A_aB_b\) if \(OO' = 10\) and the distance from \(O\) to \(\ell\) is 3.
