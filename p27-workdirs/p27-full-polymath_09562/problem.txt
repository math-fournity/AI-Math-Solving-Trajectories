In a specialized logistics simulation, a technician is analyzing the efficiency of a futuristic transport drone. The drone’s total operational energy threshold is defined by a constant $T$, which is exactly equal to the numerical value of the constant $\text{TNFTPP}$. Due to system calibration, the effective resistance $R$ is calculated by subtracting 914 units from the threshold $T$ (so $R = T - 914$).
