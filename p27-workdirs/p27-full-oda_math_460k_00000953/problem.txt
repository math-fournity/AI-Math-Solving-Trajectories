Find the shape of a mirror that will reflect all incident rays from a point source parallel to a given direction.\n\n*Hint.* Considering the plane section of the mirror, take a given source as the origin, and a given direction for the y-axis. The tangent to the required curve at the point M forms equal angles with OM and the y-axis, i.e., cuts off on the y-axis an intercept ON = OM.
