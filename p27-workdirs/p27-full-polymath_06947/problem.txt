In a specialized laboratory, a team of cryptographers is testing a security protocol based on a cyclic keypad with 37 distinct keys, labeled $\{0, 1, 2, \ldots, 36\}$. Due to a mechanical constraint, the system considers two keys $x$ and $y$ to be a "valid pair" for a target transmission $k$ if they satisfy the modular equation $ax + y \equiv k \pmod{37}$, where $a$ is a fixed "coupling coefficient" and all calculations are performed using modulo 37 arithmetic.
