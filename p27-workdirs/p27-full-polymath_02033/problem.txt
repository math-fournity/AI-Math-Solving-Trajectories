Example 1 A deck of tri-color cards, consisting of 32 cards in total, where there are 10 cards of each color: red, yellow, and blue, numbered $1,2, \cdots, 10$; in addition, there is a big joker and a small joker, each numbered 0. From this deck, several cards are drawn, and the score is calculated according to the following rules: each card numbered $k$ is worth $2^{k}$ points. If the total score of these cards is 2004, they are called a "good card group." Try to find the number of good card groups.
