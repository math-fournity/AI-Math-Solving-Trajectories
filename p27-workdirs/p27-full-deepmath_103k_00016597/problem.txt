Given two matrices $M, M' \in M_{2 \times 2}(\mathbb{Z})$ with non-zero determinants, such that for all $\lambda, \mu \in \mathbb{Z}$ there exists $U_{\lambda, \mu} \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ satisfying the equation $M' \begin{pmatrix} \lambda \\ \mu \end{pmatrix} = U_{\lambda, \mu} M \begin{pmatrix} \lambda \\ \mu \end{pmatrix}$. Determine if there exists a matrix $U \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ such that $M' = UM$. Provide a proof or counterexample.
