Given a triangle \(ABC\) with points \(D\) and \(E\) on \(AB\) and \(AC\) respectively such that \(D, E, C, B\) are concyclic. Let \(S\) be a point on the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\), \(T = BE \cap CD\), \(X = SD \cap (ABC)\), \(Y = BE \cap (ABC)\), and \(Z = ST \cap (ABC)\). Let \(P\) be the intersection of \(BC\) and \(XY\). If \(AP\) intersects the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\) again at \(Q\), find the value of \(\frac{AQ}{AP}\).
