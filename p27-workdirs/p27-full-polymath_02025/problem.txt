Let $n$ be a positive integer. We have $n$ boxes where each box contains a nonnegative number of pebbles. In each move, we are allowed to take two pebbles from a box we choose, throw away one of the pebbles, and put the other pebble in another box we choose. An initial configuration of pebbles is called solvable if it is possible to reach a configuration with no empty box in a finite number of moves.
