6. (2002 Vietnam Mathematical Olympiad) Given positive integers $m, n, m<2001, n<2002$, there are $2001 \times 2002$ different real numbers. Place these numbers into a $2001 \times 2002$ rectangular chessboard (the chessboard has 2001 rows and 2002 columns), so that each cell contains one number, and each number is placed in one cell. A cell is called a "bad cell" if the number in it is less than at least $m$ numbers in its column and less than at least $n$ numbers in its row. For each placement, let $S$ be the number of "bad cells". Find the minimum value of $S$.
