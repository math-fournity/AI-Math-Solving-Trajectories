In an acute-angled triangle \( ABC \), let \( H \) be the orthocenter. Point \( X_A \) lies on the tangent at \( H \) to the circumcircle of triangle \( BHC \) such that \( AH = AX_A \) and \( X_A \neq H \). Points \( X_B \) and \( X_C \) are defined similarly. If the ratio of similarity between triangle \( X_A X_B X_C \) and the orthotriangle of \( ABC \) is \( k \), find \( k^2 \).
