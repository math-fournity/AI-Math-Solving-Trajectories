In an isosceles triangle \(ABC\) with \(AB = AC\), let \(P\) be a point inside the triangle such that \(\angle BPC = 180^\circ - \angle BAC\). Let \(BP \cap CA = E\) and \(CP \cap AB = F\). Let \(I\) and \(J\) be the \(B\)-excenter of triangle \(ABE\) and the \(C\)-excenter of triangle \(ACF\), respectively. Let \(O\) be the circumcenter of triangle \(AEF\). If \(OI = OJ = k\), find the value of \(k\) given that \(AB = AC = 10\) and \(BC = 12\).
