Suppose that Romeo and Juliet each have a regular tetrahedron with positive real numbers assigned to its vertices. They associate each edge of their tetrahedra with the product of the two numbers assigned to its endpoints. Then, they write on each face of their tetrahedra the sum of the three numbers associated with its three edges. If the four numbers written on the faces of Romeo's tetrahedron are the same as those on Juliet's tetrahedron, do the numbers assigned to the vertices of Romeo's tetrahedron necessarily match those assigned to the vertices of Juliet's tetrahedron?
