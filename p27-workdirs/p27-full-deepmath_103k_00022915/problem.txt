Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space characterized by an upward filtered family $\mathcal{K}=\{K_{\alpha} : \alpha \in I \}$ of compact subsets, where $X=\cup_{\alpha \in I} K_{\alpha}$. A subset $F$ is open (closed) in $X$ if $F\cap K_{\alpha}$ is open (closed) in $K_{\alpha}$ for every $\alpha \in I$. Consider $C(X)$, the space of all continuous complex functions on $X$. The topology of uniform convergence on $\{K_{\alpha} : \alpha \in I \}$ induces the family of seminorms $\{||\cdot||_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha \in I}$, with $||f||_{\alpha}:= \sup \{|f(x)| : x \in K_{\alpha} \}$. Does this topology equal the topology of uniform convergence on all compact subsets in $X$? Provide a justification for your answer.
