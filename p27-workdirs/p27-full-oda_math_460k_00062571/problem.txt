Given a natural number \( N \geq 3 \), a set of \( N \) points on the coordinate plane is called permissible if their x-coordinates are distinct and each point is colored either red or blue. A polynomial \( P(x) \) separates a permissible set of points if either no red points are above the graph of \( P(x) \) and no blue points are below it, or vice versa. Determine the smallest integer \( k \) such that any permissible set of \( N \) points can be separated by a polynomial of degree at most \( k \).
