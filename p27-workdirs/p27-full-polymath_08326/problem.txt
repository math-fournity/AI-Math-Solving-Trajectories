A specialized acoustics engineering firm is testing "Harmonic Chambers," which are rooms built in the shape of non-degenerate triangles. A chamber is classified as "Resonant" if its interior corner angles $\theta_{1}, \theta_{2}, \theta_{3}$ satisfy a specific linear stability condition: there must exist a set of integers $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$ (where at least one integer is non-zero) such that the weighted sum of the angles $r_{1} \theta_{1}+r_{2} \theta_{2}+r_{3} \theta_{3}$ equals exactly zero.
