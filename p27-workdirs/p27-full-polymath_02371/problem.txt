Let $S = \{1, 2, \dots, n\}$. For any two subsets $A$ and $B$ of $S$, we say $A$ extends $B$ if $A \cap B \neq \emptyset$ and $A \setminus B \neq \emptyset$. We have a collection $\mathcal{F}$ of subsets of $S$ such that for any three subsets $A, B, C \in \mathcal{F}$ where $A$ extends $B$ and $B$ extends $C$, it holds that $A \cap C = \emptyset$. Let $M(n)$ be the maximal number of sets in such a collection $\mathcal{F}$. Compute $\sum_{n=1}^{10} M(n)$.
