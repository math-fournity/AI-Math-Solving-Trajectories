In an acute triangle \(ABC\), \(M\) is the midpoint of \(BC\) and \(P\) lies on \(BC\) such that \(\angle BAP = \angle CAM\). The tangent to the circumcircle of \(ABC\) at \(A\) meets \(BC\) at \(N\). \(AP\) meets the circumcircle of \(AMN\) again at \(Q\). Point \(R\) lies on \(QM\) such that \(NR \perp AQ\). \(PR\) meets \(AB\) at \(D\) and \(AC\) at \(E\). If \(AN\), \(BE\), and \(CD\) are concurrent at point \(X\), find the value of \(\frac{AX}{XN}\) given that \(AB = 13\), \(AC = 14\), and \(BC = 15\).
