Consider the hyperbola $C_{1}: x^{2}-y^{2}=2 m^{2}$ ($m \neq 0$) and the parabola $C_{2}$ which has its vertex at $N(n, 0)$ and its focus at the left focus $F$ of the hyperbola $C_{1}$. A line $l$ with a slope of 1 passes through $F$ and intersects the parabola $C_{2}$ at points $P$ and $Q$. Suppose there exist real numbers $m$ and $n$ such that the area of triangle $PNQ$ is $S_{\triangle P N Q}=4 \sqrt{2} m^{2}$. Let $S$ be the set of all possible values of the ratio $k = \frac{n}{m}$. Find the sum of the squares of all elements in $S$.
