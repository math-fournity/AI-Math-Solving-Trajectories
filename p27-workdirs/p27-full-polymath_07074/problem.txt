In a remote archipelago, a group of $n$ distinct islands is arranged in a perfect circle. Each island is assigned a unique rank from $1$ up to $n$, such that every rank from the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ is used exactly once. An island chain configuration is considered "stable" if, for every island $i$ in the circle, the alternating sum of ranks starting from that island and proceeding clockwise is strictly greater than zero.
