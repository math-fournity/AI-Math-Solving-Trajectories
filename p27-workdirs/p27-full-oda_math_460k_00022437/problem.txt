Consider triangle \(ABC\) with circles \((O_a)\), \((O_b)\), and \((O_c)\) having diameters \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) respectively. Let \(d_{AB}\) and \(d_{AC}\) be the tangents to \((O_a)\) parallel to \(AB\) and \(AC\) respectively, and let \(X\) be their intersection. Define \(Y\) and \(Z\) similarly with respect to \(B\) and \(C\). Let \(P\) be the point of concurrency of \(AX\), \(BY\), and \(CZ\). If the area of triangle \(ABC\) is 120 and the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\) is 13, find the value of \(AP^2 + BP^2 + CP^2\).
