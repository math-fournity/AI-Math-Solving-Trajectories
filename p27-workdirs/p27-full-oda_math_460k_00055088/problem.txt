Let \( a_1 < a_2 < a_3 < \dots \) be positive integers such that \( a_{k+1} \) divides \( 2(a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_k) \) for every \( k \geq 1 \). Suppose that for infinitely many primes \( p \), there exists \( k \) such that \( p \) divides \( a_k \). Find the smallest positive integer \( n \) such that for every positive integer \( m \leq n \), there exists \( k \) such that \( m \) divides \( a_k \).
