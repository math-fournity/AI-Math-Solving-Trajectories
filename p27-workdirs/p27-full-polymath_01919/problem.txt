Task 5. At a fish market, there are 10 stalls all selling the same 10 types of fish. All the fish are caught in the North Sea or the Mediterranean Sea, and each stall has only one sea of origin per fish species. A number, $k$, of customers buy one fish from each stall so that they have one fish of each species. Furthermore, we know that each pair of customers has a fish species with different origins. We consider all possible ways to stock the stalls according to the above rules.
