Let $E, F$ be finite dimensional normed spaces, $A\subset E$ be a connected open set, $f:A\times F\to F$ be a continuously differentiable mapping. Assume that the set $\Gamma=f^{-1}(0)$ is non-empty and for each $(x,y)\in\Gamma$ the derivative $\partial_y f(x,y)$ is invertible. Let $G$ be a connected component of $\Gamma$ such that $pr_1(G)=A$ ($pr_1$ being a local homeomorphism of $G$ onto $A$). Determine whether any continuous mapping $\gamma:[0,1]\to A$ can be lifted to a continuous mapping $\tilde{\gamma}:[0,1]\to G$ (i.e. $pr_1\circ\tilde{\gamma}=\gamma$).
