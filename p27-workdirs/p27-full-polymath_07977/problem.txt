A specialized microchip architecture consists of an $n \times n$ grid of logic gates, where $n \geq 3$ is a natural number. During the initialization phase, energy packets are loaded onto the gates along the main diagonal, which stretches from the upper-left gate to the lower-right gate. Specifically, every gate on this diagonal contains exactly one energy packet, except for the first gate (top-left) and the last gate (bottom-right), which remain empty.
