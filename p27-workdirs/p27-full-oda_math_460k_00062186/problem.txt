In a two-player game, a fraction with $1010$ squares in the numerator and $1011$ squares in the denominator serves as the game board. Players take turns choosing a number from $1$ to $2021$ and inserting it into any empty square, with each number used only once. The starting player wins if the value of the fraction after all squares are filled differs from $1$ by less than $10^{-6}$. Otherwise, the other player wins. Determine which player has a winning strategy.
