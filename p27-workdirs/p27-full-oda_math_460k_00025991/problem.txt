Let \(ABCD\) be a tetrahedron such that its altitudes are concurrent. Let \(R\) and \(r\) be respectively the circumradius and the inradius of the tetrahedron \(ABCD\). Let \(R_A, R_B, R_C, R_D\) be respectively the circumradii of the tetrahedra \(OBCD, OACD, OABD, OABC\) where \(O\) is the circumcenter of the tetrahedron \(ABCD\). Calculate the minimum value of \( \sum_{\text{cyclic}} \frac{1}{R_A^2} \) given that \(R = 1\).
