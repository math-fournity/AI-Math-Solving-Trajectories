Consider the sequence of prime numbers. Define the first difference as the absolute difference between consecutive primes, the second difference as the absolute difference between consecutive first differences, and so on. Let \( a_{m,n} \) represent the \( n \)-th term in the \( m \)-th row of differences. Prove or disprove the conjecture that \( a_{m,1} = 1 \) for all \( m \geq 2 \).
