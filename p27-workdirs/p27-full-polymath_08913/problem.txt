In a specialized logistics hub, a sequence of storage bins is indexed from $1$ up to a maximum capacity $h(r)$. For every integer number of shipping companies $r$ (where $r > 1$), $h(r)$ represents the minimum number of bins required to guarantee a specific outcome: no matter how the bins $\{1, 2, \dots, h(r)\}$ are assigned among the $r$ companies, there will always be at least one company that possesses three bins labeled with values that can be expressed as $a + x$, $a + y$, and $a + x + y$, for some non-negative integer $a \geq 0$ and some positive integers $x$ and $y$ such that $1 \leq x \leq y$.
