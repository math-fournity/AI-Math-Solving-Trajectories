The set \( S = \{0, 1, 2, \ldots, 99\} \) is given. A subset \( A \) of \( S \) is called "almost additive" if it has more than one element, and for any two distinct \( a \) and \( b \) in \( A \), the remainder of \( a + b + 1 \) modulo 100 is also in \( A \). Find the number of "almost additive" subsets of \( S \).
