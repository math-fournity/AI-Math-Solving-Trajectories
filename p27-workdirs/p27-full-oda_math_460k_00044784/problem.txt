Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with circumcircle \(\Gamma\), circumcenter \(O\), and \(AB < AC\). Let \(D\) be the midpoint of \(AB\), and \(E\) a point on \(AC\) such that \(BE = CE\). The circumcircle \(\omega\) of \(BDE\) intersects \(\Gamma\) at \(F \ne B\). Let \(K\) be the orthogonal projection of \(B\) onto \(AO\), and suppose that \(A\) and \(K\) lie on different sides of \(BE\). Let \(P\) be the intersection of \(DF\) and \(CK\). If \(P\) lies on \(\Gamma\), find the value of \(\angle BPC\) in degrees.
