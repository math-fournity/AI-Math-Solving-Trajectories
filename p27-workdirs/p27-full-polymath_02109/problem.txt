Let $n \ge 2$ be an integer. There are $n$ positive integers written on a blackboard. In each step we choose two of the numbers on the blackboard and replace each of them by their sum. Determine the sum of all integers $n$ in the set $\{2, 3, \dots, 100\}$ for which it is always possible to get $n$ identical integers in a finite number of steps, regardless of the initial $n$ positive integers.
