The 23rd question: For a finite graph, the following operation can be performed: choose any cycle of length 4, select any edge in this cycle, and remove it from the graph. For a fixed positive number $\mathrm{n}(\mathrm{n} \geq 4)$, if the complete graph with $\mathrm{n}$ vertices is repeatedly subjected to the above operation, find the minimum possible number of edges in the resulting graph.
