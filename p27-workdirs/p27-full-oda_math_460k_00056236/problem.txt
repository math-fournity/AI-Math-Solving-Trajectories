Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with \(O\) as the center of the circumscribed circle and \(I\) as the center of the inscribed circle. Lines parallel to the angle bisectors through \(O\) intersect the sides \(BC\), \(AC\), and \(AB\) at points \(A_3\), \(B_3\), and \(C_3\) respectively. Let \(A_1\), \(B_1\), and \(C_1\) be the intersection points of the angle bisectors with the circumscribed circle. If the lines \(A_1A_3\), \(B_1B_3\), and \(C_1C_3\) intersect at a point \(P\), find the ratio \(\frac{OP}{OI}\).
