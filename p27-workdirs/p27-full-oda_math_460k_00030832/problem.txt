Given a triangle \(ABC\) with the circumcircle \((O)\), incircle \((I)\), and \(A\)-excircle \((I_a)\). Let \(E\) and \(F\) be the points of intersection of \(BI\) with \(AC\) and \(CI\) with \(AB\), respectively. Let \(M\) be the point of intersection of the ray \(FE\) with the circumcircle \((O)\). Let \(U\) be the second point of intersection of \(MI_a\) with the circumcircle \((O)\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{MU}{MI_a}\).
