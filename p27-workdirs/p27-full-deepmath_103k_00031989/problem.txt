In a circle \(\omega\), quadrilateral \(ABCD\) is inscribed such that the center of \(\omega\) lies on side \(AB\). Another circle \(\omega_{1}\) is externally tangent to \(\omega\) at point \(C\). A third circle \(\omega_{2}\) is tangent to both \(\omega\) and \(\omega_{1}\) at points \(D\) and \(E\) respectively. Line \(BC\) intersects circle \(\omega_{1}\) again at point \(P\), and line \(AD\) intersects circle \(\omega_{2}\) again at point \(Q\). Given that points \(P\), \(Q\), and \(E\) are distinct, determine the measure of angle \(PEQ\).
