Let $M$ be a smooth, connected manifold, and let $K \subset M$ be a compact subset. Consider the set $M \setminus K$, which consists of multiple connected components. Let $(U_j)_{j \in \mathcal{J}}$ denote the collection of components in $M \setminus K$ that are bounded, i.e., have compact closures within $M$. Does the union $U = \bigcup_{j \in \mathcal{J}} U_j$ form a bounded subset of $M$?
