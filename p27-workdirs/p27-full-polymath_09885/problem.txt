A specialized logistics company, "Aegis Solutions," has designed a foolproof security protocol for their high-priority courier crates. Each crate is secured by a keypad featuring digits 0 through 9. To open the crate, a three-digit sequence must be entered. Due to a unique safety feature, the locking mechanism will disengage if the entered sequence matches the actual secret code in at least two of the three positions (for example, if the code is 1-2-3, entering 1-2-0, 1-9-3, or 8-2-3 will all successfully open the crate).
