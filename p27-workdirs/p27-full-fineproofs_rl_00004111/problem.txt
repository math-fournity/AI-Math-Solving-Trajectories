Let \( L \) be a positive integer. There are  $2024$  balls with distinct weights placed on a table. Initially, player  $A$  knows the weight of each ball, but player  $B$  knows nothing. To determine the weight of every ball, player  $B$  asks player  $A$  a series of questions. In each question, player  $B$  may specify any set of at most \( L \) balls, and player  $A$  will only respond with the set of weights of the specified balls (i.e., without revealing which weight corresponds to which ball).
