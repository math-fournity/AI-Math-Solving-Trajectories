Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with incenter \(I\) and let its incircle touch \(BC\) at \(D\). Let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(BC\) and \(M_A\) be the intersection of \(AI\) and the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\) other than \(A\). Let \(G\) be the intersection of \(DI\) and \(AM\), and \(K\) be the midpoint of \(DG\). Let circles \((M_AKI)\) and \((ABC)\) intersect again at \(L \neq M_A\) and let \(N\) be the intersection of \(KL\) and the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\) other than \(L\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{KN}{KL}\).
