In the futuristic city of Neoterra, an architect named Manda is commissioned to build a structural model of a skyscraper with a floor plan shaped like a regular $n$-sided polygon, where $n$ is a whole number greater than 3. To represent the framework, she uses thin titanium rods to create every possible straight-line connection between the $n$ vertices of the polygon, including all outer perimeter walls and all internal cross-bracing diagonals.
