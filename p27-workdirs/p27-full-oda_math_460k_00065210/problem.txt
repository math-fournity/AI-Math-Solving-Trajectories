Consider the group $\mathrm{SL}_{n}(\mathbb{Z})$. Let $\phi$ be an injective endomorphism of $\mathrm{SL}_{n}(\mathbb{Z})$. Define $f(n)$ to be the smallest positive integer such that $\phi$ restricted to the subgroup of matrices in $\mathrm{SL}_{n}(\mathbb{Z})$ with entries in $f(n)\mathbb{Z}$ is surjective onto the subgroup of matrices in $\mathrm{SL}_{n}(\mathbb{Z})$ with entries in $\mathbb{Z}$. Find $f(2)$.
