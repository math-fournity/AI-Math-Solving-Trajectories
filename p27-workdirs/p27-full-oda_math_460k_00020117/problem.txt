In triangle \(ABC\), let \(M_A\) be the midpoint of side \(BC\), and \(P_A\) be the orthogonal projection of \(A\) on \(BC\). Similarly, define \(M_B, M_C, P_B, P_C\). The line \(M_BM_C\) intersects \(P_BP_C\) at \(S_A\), and the tangent to the circumcircle of \(ABC\) at \(A\) meets \(BC\) at \(T_A\). Similarly, define \(S_B, S_C, T_B, T_C\). Let \(D\) be the point where the perpendiculars through \(A, B, C\) to \(S_AT_A, S_BT_B, S_CT_C\) respectively, meet. If \(D\) lies on the Euler line of triangle \(ABC\), find the ratio \(\frac{OD}{OH}\), where \(O\) is the circumcenter and \(H\) is the orthocenter of triangle \(ABC\).
