Verify Stokes' Theorem by computing the line integral \( \oint_C -y^3 \, dx + x^3 \, dy - z^3 \, dz \), where \(C\) is the intersection of the cylinder \(x^2 + y^2 = 1\) and the plane \(x + y + z = 1\). Find a parametrization of \(C\) and compute the line integral directly to show that it equals \(\frac{3\pi}{2}\).
