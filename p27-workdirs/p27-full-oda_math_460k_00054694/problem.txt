Let \(ABC\) be an isosceles triangle with \(AB = AC\) and \(D\) the foot of the height from \(A\) to \(BC\). Let \(P\) be a point inside triangle \(ADC\) such that \(\angle APB > 90^\circ\) and \(\angle PAD + \angle PBD = \angle PCD\). The lines \(CP\) and \(AD\) intersect at \(Q\), and the lines \(BP\) and \(AD\) intersect at \(R\). Let \(T\) be a point on segment \(AB\) such that \(\angle TRB = \angle DQC\), and let \(S\) be a point on the extension of segment \(AP\) (on the \(P\) side) such that \(\angle PSR = 2 \angle PAR\). If \(AB = 10\) and \(AD = 8\), find the length of \(RS\).
