In the sprawling digital archives of a galactic library, there are $3,000,000$ unique data-storage cassettes. Each cassette contains exactly $100$ distinct data slots arranged in a single row. To ensure data diversity, every cassette is programmed with a sequence of ID codes ranging from $1$ to $100$, such that each code appears exactly once per cassette. Crucially, no two cassettes in the entire library share the exact same sequence of ID codes across their $100$ slots.
