The cells of a table with \(2009\) rows and \(2011\) columns are colored in a checkerboard pattern. Find the largest natural number \(k\) with the following property: When deleting any \(k\) cells from the table, such that among the unremoved cells there is an equal number of white and black cells, the remaining part of the table (i.e., the unremoved cells) can be covered with dominoes. (A domino consists of two cells with a common side).
