In a specialized network of data relays, an infinite stream of integer-valued signals $A = (a_1, a_2, \ldots)$ is classified as "Synchronized" if it adheres to a specific recursive protocol. For every transmission index $n > 100$, the signal value $a_n$ is automatically generated by summing the products of all pairs of previous signals $a_k$ and $a_\ell$ that satisfy two conditions: the indices must be distinct and strictly less than the current index ($1 \leq k < \ell \leq n-1$), and their combined indices must reach or exceed the current index ($k + \ell \geq n$).
