Given a triangle \(ABC\) with circumcircle \((O)\), let \(A_0\), \(B_0\), and \(C_0\) be the midpoints of \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) respectively. Let \(A_1\), \(B_1\), and \(C_1\) be the perpendicular projections of \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) on \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) respectively. Define circles \((O_a)\), \((O_b)\), and \((O_c)\) passing through \(B_0C_0\), \(C_0A_0\), and \(A_0B_0\) respectively, and internally tangent to \((O)\) at points \(A_2\), \(B_2\), and \(C_2\) (different from \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\)). Let \(A_3\) be the intersection of \(B_1C_1\) and \(B_2C_2\), and similarly define \(B_3\) and \(C_3\). If \(H\) is the orthocenter of triangle \(ABC\), find the length of the perpendicular from \(H\) to the line passing through \(A_3\), \(B_3\), and \(C_3\) in terms of the circumradius \(R\) of triangle \(ABC\).
