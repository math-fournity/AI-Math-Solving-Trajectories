Given a convex polygon with 2019 sides, where no three diagonals intersect at a single point, the intersection of two diagonals inside the polygon is called a "node". If two nodes are on the same diagonal, they are referred to as "adjacent". If a closed path formed by adjacent nodes satisfies that any three consecutive nodes are not on the same diagonal, then this closed path is called a "loop". Find the maximum number of colored nodes such that there is no loop formed solely by the colored nodes.
