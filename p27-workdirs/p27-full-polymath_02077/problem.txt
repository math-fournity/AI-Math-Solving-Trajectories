Suppose $\triangle ABC$ is a scalene triangle with circumcenter $O$ and circumradius $R$. Let $A'$ be a point on the extension of segment $AO$ such that $\angle BA'A = \angle CA'A$. Let $A_1$ and $A_2$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $A'$ onto lines $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let $H_A$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $A$ onto $BC$. Denote $R_A$ as the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle H_A A_1 A_2$. Similarly, define $R_B$ and $R_C$ by cyclically permuting $A, B, C$.
