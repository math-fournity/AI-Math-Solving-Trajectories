Consider a measurable space $(\Omega,\Sigma)$ with two probability measures $\nu_1$ and $\nu_2$. For $i=1,2$, define the linear map $L_{\nu_i}:L^{1}(\nu_i)\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ by $L_{\nu_i}(g)=\int_\Omega g\,d\nu_i$. For the equivalence class $[1_E]_{\nu_i}$ in $L^1(\nu_i)$ of the characteristic function $1_E$, does there always exist a linear map $M:L^1(\nu_1)\rightarrow L^1(\nu_2)$ such that $$L_{\nu_2}\circ M([1_E]_{\nu_1})=L_{\nu_1}([1_E]_{\nu_1})$$ for all $E\in\Sigma$? Assume $\nu_1$ and $\nu_2$ are singular measures.
