In the complex vector space \(\mathbb{C}^d\), consider two orthonormal bases \(\mathbf{B_1}\) and \(\mathbf{B_2}\) that are mutually unbiased. For any non-zero vector \(|\alpha\rangle \in \mathbb{C}^d\), determine the maximum number of vectors from the combined set \(\mathbf{B_1} \cup \mathbf{B_2}\) that can be orthogonal to \(|\alpha\rangle\). What is the maximum cardinality of this set?
