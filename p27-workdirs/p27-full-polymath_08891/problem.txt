A specialized logistics company manages a fleet of transport drones. For any fleet size $n$ (where $n$ is a positive integer), the company categorizes its operational sub-units based on every possible divisor of $n$. These sub-unit sizes are ranked in strictly increasing order as $d_{1}, d_{2}, \dots, d_{k}$, where $d_{1}=1$ represents an individual drone and $d_{k}=n$ represents the entire fleet.
