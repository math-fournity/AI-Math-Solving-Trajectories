Let \(ABC\) be an acute-angled, nonisosceles triangle. Points \(A_1\) and \(A_2\) are symmetric to the feet of the internal and external bisectors of angle \(A\) with respect to the midpoint of \(BC\). Segment \(A_1A_2\) is a diameter of a circle \(\alpha\). Circles \(\beta\) and \(\gamma\) are defined similarly for angles \(B\) and \(C\). If the three circles \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), and \(\gamma\) intersect at two points \(P\) and \(Q\), calculate the sum of the squares of the distances from \(P\) to each of the vertices \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\).
