Each side of a unit square is colored with one of four colors: red, yellow, blue, and green, such that all four sides of each square have distinct colors. Squares can be assembled to form a rectangle by joining sides of the same color. A rectangle can be built if its four boundary sides are monochromatic and have distinct colors. Let $m$ and $n$ be the dimensions of such a rectangle. Determine the condition on $m$ and $n$ for such a rectangle to be constructible. Using this condition, how many pairs $(m, n)$ with $1 \le m, n \le 100$ represent the dimensions of a constructible rectangle?
