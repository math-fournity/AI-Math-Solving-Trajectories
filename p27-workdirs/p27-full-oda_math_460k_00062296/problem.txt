In a non-isosceles triangle \(ABC\) with incenter \(I\), the incircle touches the sides \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) at points \(A_1\), \(B_1\), and \(C_1\) respectively. The lines \(AI\), \(BI\), and \(CI\) intersect \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) at points \(A_2\), \(B_2\), and \(C_2\) respectively. Define point \(A'\) on \(AI\) such that \(A_1A' \perp B_2C_2\), and define \(B'\) and \(C'\) similarly. If the area of triangle \(A'B'C'\) is equal to the area of triangle \(A_1B_1C_1\), calculate the ratio \(\frac{[A'B'C']}{[ABC]}\).
