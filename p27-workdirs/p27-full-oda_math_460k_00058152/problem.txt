Consider an infinite grid $G$ of unit square cells. A chessboard polygon is a simple polygon whose sides lie along the gridlines of $G$. Nikolai chooses a chessboard polygon $F$ with $n$ vertices and challenges you to paint some cells of $G$ green such that any chessboard polygon congruent to $F$ has at least $1$ green cell but at most $2020$ green cells. Find the smallest value of $n$ for which Nikolai can choose $F$ to make your job impossible.
