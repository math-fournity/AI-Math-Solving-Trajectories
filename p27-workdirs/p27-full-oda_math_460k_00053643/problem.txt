Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $2019$ with real coefficients and a non-zero leading coefficient. Find the smallest positive integer $N$ such that $P(x)$ can be expressed as a sum of $N$ terms, each of which is a linear polynomial raised to the power of $2019$. That is, $P(x) = p_1(x)^{2019} + p_2(x)^{2019} + \ldots + p_N(x)^{2019}$, where each $p_i(x)$ is a linear polynomial with real coefficients. Calculate $N$.
