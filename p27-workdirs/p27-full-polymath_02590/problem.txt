Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with \(\angle BAC = 60^\circ\). Let \(P\) be the intersection of the angle bisector of \(\angle ABC\) with side \(AC\), and let \(Q\) be the intersection of the angle bisector of \(\angle ACB\) with side \(AB\). Let \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) be the in-radii of triangles \(ABC\) and \(APQ\), respectively. Determine the circum-radius of triangle \(APQ\) in terms of \(r_1\) and \(r_2\).
