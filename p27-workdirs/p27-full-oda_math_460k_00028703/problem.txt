Let \( P \in \mathbb{R}[X] \) be a nonconstant polynomial in one variable with no multiple roots (i.e., \( \gcd(P, P') = 1 \)). Let \( Q_1, \ldots, Q_\ell \in \mathbb{R}[X] \) be polynomials. For each \( \varepsilon = (\varepsilon_1, \ldots, \varepsilon_\ell) \in \{0,1\}^\ell \), define \( Q^\varepsilon = Q_1^{\varepsilon_1} \cdots Q_\ell^{\varepsilon_\ell} \), and let \( s_\varepsilon = v_{P,Q^\varepsilon}(-\infty) - v_{P,Q^\varepsilon}(+\infty) \), where \( v_{P,Q^\varepsilon} \) is the number of sign changes in the Sturm sequence of \( P \) and \( P'Q^\varepsilon \). What is the number of distinct real roots \( c \) of \( P \) such that \( Q_i(c) \neq 0 \) for all \( i = 1, \ldots, \ell \)?
