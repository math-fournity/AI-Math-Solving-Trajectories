Given distinct points \( \lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_m \) in the open right-half plane \( \mathbf{C}_+ \), vectors \( u_1, \ldots, u_m \in \mathbf{C}^q \), and vectors \( v_1, \ldots, v_m \in \mathbf{C}^p \), find a function \( H: \mathbf{C} \to \mathbf{C}^{p \times q} \) that is analytic in \( \mathbf{C}_+ \), satisfies the interpolation conditions \( H(\lambda_i) u_i = v_i \) for \( i = 1, \ldots, m \), and has \( \mathbf{H}_\infty \) norm \( \|H\|_\infty \leq 1 \).
