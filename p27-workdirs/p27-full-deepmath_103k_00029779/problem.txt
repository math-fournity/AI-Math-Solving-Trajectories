Let $X$ be a Hausdorff topological space and $G$ be a group acting on $X$ by homeomorphisms. Suppose that for every point $x \in X$, there exists a neighborhood $U$ of $x$ such that $g \cdot U \cap U = \emptyset$ for all but finitely many $g \in G$. Prove or disprove: For every pair of points $x$ and $y$ in $X$, there exist neighborhoods $U$ of $x$ and $V$ of $y$ such that $g \cdot U \cap V = \emptyset$ for all but finitely many $g \in G$. \(\text{True or False?}\)
