Given two infinite non-constant sequences of rational numbers \((A_i)\) and \((B_i)\) such that the product \((A_i - A_j)(B_i - B_j)\) is an integer for all natural numbers \(i\) and \(j\), find the smallest positive integer \(k\) such that there exists a rational number \(C\) with denominator at most \(k\) (in lowest terms) satisfying \(C(A_i - A_j)\) and \((B_i - B_j)/C\) are integers for all natural numbers \(i\) and \(j\).
