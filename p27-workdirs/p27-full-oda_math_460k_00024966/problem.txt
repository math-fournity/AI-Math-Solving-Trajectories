Given a non-degenerate triangle \(ABC\), let \(A_1, B_1, C_1\) be the points of tangency of the incircle with the sides \(BC, AC, AB\) respectively. Let \(Q\) and \(L\) be the intersections of the segment \(AA_1\) with the incircle and the segment \(B_1C_1\) respectively. Let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(B_1C_1\). Let \(T\) be the point of intersection of \(BC\) and \(B_1C_1\). Let \(P\) be the foot of the perpendicular from the point \(L\) on the line \(AT\). Calculate the value of \(\frac{A_1M \cdot A_1Q}{A_1P \cdot A_1T}\).
