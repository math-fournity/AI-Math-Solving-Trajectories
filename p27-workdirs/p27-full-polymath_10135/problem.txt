A specialized microchip architecture consists of a primary rectangular substrate divided into $H$ distinct rectangular processing zones by a network of straight conductive traces. Within this grid, $V$ represents the number of four-way "X-junctions" where two traces cross, $T$ represents the number of three-way "T-junctions" where one trace ends perpendicularly at another, and $S$ represents the total number of maximal straight traces used in the design.
