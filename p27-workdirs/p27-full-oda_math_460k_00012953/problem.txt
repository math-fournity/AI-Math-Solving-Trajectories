Let \(D, E, F\) be collinear points on the side lines \(BC, CA, AB\) of a triangle \(\triangle ABC\). Let \(K, L, M\) be the midpoints of the segments \(AD, BE, CF\) and \((K), (L), (M)\) be circles on the diameters \(AD, BE, CF\). If the circumcenter of the triangle formed by the lines \(AD, BE, CF\) is denoted by \(O\), calculate the product of the distances from \(O\) to the centers of the circles \((K), (L), (M)\).
