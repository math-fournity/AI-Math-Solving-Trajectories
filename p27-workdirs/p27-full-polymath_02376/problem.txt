In a $3 \times 3$ grid of unit cells, a snake of length $k$ occupies an ordered $k$-tuple of cells such that each cell is distinct and adjacent cells in the tuple share a side. The snake can move by shifting its head into an adjacent unoccupied cell. Determine the largest integer $k$ such that a snake of length $k$ can be placed in the grid and can turn around, meaning it can move to occupy the cells in the reverse order.
