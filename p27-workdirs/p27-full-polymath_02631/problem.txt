A $2N \times 2N$ board is covered by non-overlapping dominoes of $1 \times 2$ size. A lame rook (which moves exactly one cell at a time, horizontally or vertically) visits each cell of the board exactly once in a path. A move is called longitudinal if it is a move between the two cells of the same domino. Let $M_N$ be the maximum possible number of longitudinal moves and $m_N$ be the minimum possible number of longitudinal moves for a given $N$.
