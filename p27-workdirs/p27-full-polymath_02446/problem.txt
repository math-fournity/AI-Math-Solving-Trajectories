Let $\overline{a_{1} a_{2} a_{3}}$ and $\overline{a_{3} a_{2} a_{1}}$ be two three-digit decimal numbers, where $a_{1}$ and $a_{3}$ are different non-zero digits. Suppose the squares of these numbers are five-digit numbers $\overline{b_{1} b_{2} b_{3} b_{4} b_{5}}$ and $\overline{b_{5} b_{4} b_{3} b_{2} b_{1}}$ respectively. Find the sum of all such three-digit numbers $\overline{a_{1} a_{2} a_{3}}$.
