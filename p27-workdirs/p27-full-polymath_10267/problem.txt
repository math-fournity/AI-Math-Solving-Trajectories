In the remote digital ecosystem of Neo-Aether, a self-sustaining data stream $a_n$ is generated for every natural time cycle $n$. The network protocol dictates a strict recursive evolution for the stream's intensity: the intensity at any cycle $n+2$ is calculated by taking the sum of the intensity from two cycles prior ($a_n$) and a constant stability coefficient of $2022$, then dividing that sum by the quantity of $1$ plus the intensity of the immediate previous cycle ($a_{n+1}$).
