Denote by \( X \) the midpoint of major arc \( \widehat{BAC} \) of the circumcircle of \( \triangle ABC \). Suppose \( P \) is a point on line \( XI \) such that \( \overline{DP} \perp \overline{EF} \). Given that \( AB=14, AC=15, \) and \( BC=13 \), compute \( DP \).
