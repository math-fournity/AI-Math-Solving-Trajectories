Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a finite-dimensional Lie algebra, and let $\mathfrak{g}^{(i)}$ denote the derived series of $\mathfrak{g}$, with $\mathfrak{g}^{(0)} = \mathfrak{g}$. Let $\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak{g})$ be the solvable radical of $\mathfrak{g}$. There exist minimal natural numbers $n$ and $m$ such that $\mathfrak{g}^{(n)} = \mathfrak{g}^{(n+1)}$ and $\operatorname{rad}(\mathfrak{g})^{(m)} = 0$. Determine the relationship between the numbers $n$ and $m$. \(\text{Hint: Consider the properties of solvable and nilpotent Lie algebras.}\)
