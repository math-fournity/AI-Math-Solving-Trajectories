Inside each barrel is a herring either with its head up or its head down. In a move, Ali Baba chooses from  $1$  to  $n$  of the barrels and turns them upside down. Then the table spins around. When it stops, it is impossible to tell which barrels have been turned over. The cave will open if the heads of the herrings in all  $n$  barrels are up or are all down.
