Find all positive integers \( n > 2 \) such that the only functions \( f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z} \) satisfying the following property are linear functions of the form \( f(x) = bx + c \), where \( b \) and \( c \) are integers: If \( a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n \) form an arithmetic progression, then the numbers \( f(a_1), f(a_2), \dots, f(a_n) \) also form an arithmetic progression, possibly in a different order.
