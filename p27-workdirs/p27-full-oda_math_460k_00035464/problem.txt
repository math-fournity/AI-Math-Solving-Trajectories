Let \( A \) be the set of all permutations \( a = (a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{2003}) \) of the first 2003 positive integers such that there is no proper subset \( S \) of the set \( \{1, 2, \ldots, 2003\} \) for which \( \{a_k \mid k \in S\} = S \). For each \( a = (a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{2003}) \in A \), define \( d(a) = \sum_{k=1}^{2003} (a_k - k)^2 \). Find the least value of \( d(a) \). Denote this least value by \( d_0 \).
