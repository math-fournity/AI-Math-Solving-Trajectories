In $\triangle ABC$, the points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ lie on the sides $BC, CA, AB$ respectively, such that $AB_{1}-AC_{1} = CA_{1}-CB_{1} = BC_{1}-BA_{1}$. Let $I_A$ be the incenter of $\triangle AB_1C_1$, $I_B$ be the incenter of $\triangle A_1BC_1$, and $I_C$ be the incenter of $\triangle A_1B_1C$. If the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle I_A I_B I_C$ is $R$ and the inradius of $\triangle ABC$ is $r$, let $d$ be the distance between the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ and the circumcenter of $\triangle I_A I_B I_C$. If $r = 7$ and the distance from the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ to $A_1$ is $11$, find the value of $R^2 - d^2$.
