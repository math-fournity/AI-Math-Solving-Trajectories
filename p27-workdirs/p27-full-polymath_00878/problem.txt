Greetings $M$.  Around a round table, $N$ knights are seated. Every morning, the wizard Merlin seats them in a different order. Starting from the second day, Merlin allows the knights to make as many swaps as they like during the day: two adjacent knights can swap places if and only if they were not neighbors on the first day. The knights try to sit in the same order as on one of the previous days: then the meetings will end. What is the maximum number of days Merlin can guarantee to hold meetings?  (Seatings that can be obtained from each other by rotation are considered the same. Merlin does not sit at the table.)
