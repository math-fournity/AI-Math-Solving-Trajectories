Given triangle \(ABC\) with \(AB < AC\) and its circumcircle \(\Omega\). Let \(I\) be the incenter of \(ABC\), and the feet from \(I\) to \(BC\) is \(D\). The circle with center \(A\) and radius \(AI\) intersects \(\Omega\) at \(E\) and \(F\). \(P\) is a point on \(EF\) such that \(DP\) is parallel to \(AI\). Let \(M\) be the midpoint of arc \(BAC\). If \(AP\) and \(MI\) intersect at point \(X\) on \(\Omega\), find the value of \(\frac{AX}{XI}\).
