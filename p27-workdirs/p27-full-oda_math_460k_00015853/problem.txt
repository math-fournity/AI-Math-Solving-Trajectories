Let \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\) be variables, and let \(y_1, y_2, \ldots, y_{2^n-1}\) be the sums of nonempty subsets of \(x_i\).  \nLet \(p_k(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\) be the \(k\)th elementary symmetric polynomial in the \(y_i\)s (the sum of every product of \(k\) distinct \(y_i\)'s).  \nFor which \(k\) and \(n\) is every coefficient of \(p_k (x_1, \ldots, x_n)\) (as a polynomial in \(x_1,\ldots,x_n\)) even?\n\nFor example, if \(n = 2\), then \(y_1, y_2, y_3\) are \(x_1, x_1 + x_2\) and\n\n\[\n\begin{align*}\np_1 &= y_1 + y_2 + y_3 = 2x_1 + 2x_2, \\\np_2 &= y_1 y_2 + y_2 y_3 + y_3 y_1 = x_1^2 + 2x_1 x_2 + 3x_1 x_2, \\\np_3 &= y_1 y_2 y_3 = x_1^2 x_2 + x_1 x_2^2.\n\end{align*}\n\]
