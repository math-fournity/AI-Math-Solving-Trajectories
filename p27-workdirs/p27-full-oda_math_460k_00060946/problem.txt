**A Lalescu limit.** Let \( m \geq 0 \) be an integer and let \( f \) be a polynomial of degree \( m \) whose coefficient in the leading term is positive. Then \n\n\[\n\lim_{x \to \infty} \left( (f(x+2)\Gamma(x+2))^{\frac{1}{x+2}} - (f(x+1)\Gamma(x+1))^{\frac{1}{x}} \right) = \frac{1}{e}\n\]\n\nand \n\n\[\n\lim_{x \to \infty} x \left( (f(x+2)\Gamma(x+2))^{\frac{1}{x+2}} - (f(x+1)\Gamma(x+1))^{\frac{1}{x}} - \frac{1}{e} \right) = \frac{1}{e} \left( m + \frac{1}{2} \right),\n\]\n\nwhere \(\Gamma\) denotes the Gamma function.
