Let $f$ be a function from positive integers to positive integers satisfying the following properties: (i) $f$ is strictly increasing, meaning $f(n + 1) > f(n)$ for all positive integers $n$; (ii) $f(mn) = f(m) \cdot f(n)$ for all positive integers $m$ and $n$; (iii) if $m \neq n$ and $m^n = n^m$, then $f(m) = n$ or $f(n) = m$. Determine the sum of all possible values of $f(30)$. \(\)
