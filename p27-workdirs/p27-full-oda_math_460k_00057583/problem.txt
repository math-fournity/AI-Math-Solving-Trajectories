Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with \(D, E, F\) as the tangency points of the incircle with \(BC, CA, AB\) respectively. Let \(EF\) intersect the circumcircle \((O)\) of \(ABC\) at \(X\) and \(Y\). Let \(T\) be the second intersection of the circumcircle of \(DXY\) with the incircle. If \(AT\) intersects the circumcircle \((O)\) again at \(P\), find the ratio \(\frac{AP}{PT_a}\) where \(T_a\) is the tangency point of the \(A\)-mixtilinear incircle with \((O)\).
