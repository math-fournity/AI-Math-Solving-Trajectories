A specialized logistics company manages an infinite sequence of warehouses labeled $1, 2, 3, \dots$. Every warehouse $n$ contains a specific inventory of power cells. To manage these, the company employs two automated protocols, $f$ and $g$, both of which take a warehouse label as input and output a positive integer. Protocol $g$ is designed to be highly diverse, outputting an infinite variety of different values across the entire set of warehouses.
