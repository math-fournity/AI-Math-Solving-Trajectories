Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $n = 10$ such that $P(x) = Q(x) P''(x)$, where $Q(x)$ is a quadratic polynomial and $P''(x)$ is the second derivative of $P(x)$. Let $k$ be the number of distinct roots of $P(x)$. Find the set of all possible values for $k$ given that $P(x)$ has at least two distinct roots. Return the sum of all such possible values.
