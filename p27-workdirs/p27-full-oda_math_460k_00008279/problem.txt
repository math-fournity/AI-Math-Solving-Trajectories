Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle inscribed in circle \((O)\). Let \(M\) and \(N\) be the midpoints of \(AB\) and \(AC\), respectively. Let \(E\) and \(F\) be the midpoints of the minor arcs \(CA\) and \(AB\), respectively. \(EF\) meets \(CA\) and \(AB\) at \(Q\) and \(R\), respectively. Let \(L\) and \(K\) be the projections of \(E\) and \(F\) onto \(AO\), respectively. Let \(S\) and \(T\) be the centers of \((RMK)\) and \((QNL)\), respectively. On line \(ST\), take \(X\) such that \(XO \perp OA\). Suppose the line through \(X\) parallel to \(EF\) intersects \((O)\) at \(Y\) and \(Z\). Calculate the length of \(YZ\).
