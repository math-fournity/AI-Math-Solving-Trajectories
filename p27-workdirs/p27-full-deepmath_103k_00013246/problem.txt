Consider a continuous-time, homogeneous diffusion process $X$ and two points $x$ and $y$. A "bump" is defined as the time it takes for $X_t$ to go from $y$ to $x$ and then back to $y$ again. Define the stopping times $t_1, t_2, \ldots$ iteratively as follows: $t_1$ is the first time a bump finishes, $t_2$ is the time it takes from the first bump finished till the second bump finishes, and so on. Are the random variables $t_1, t_2, \ldots$ independent and identically distributed?
