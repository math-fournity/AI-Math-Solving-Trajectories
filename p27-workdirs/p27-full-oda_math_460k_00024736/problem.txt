Let \( G(V,E) \) be a connected simple graph on \( n \) vertices. Initially, each vertex \( v \) has a weight \( a_v \in \mathbb{R} \) such that the sum of initial weights is positive. The game is played as follows: the player picks a vertex \( v \) with a negative weight \( w_v \) and replaces it by the opposite number, then adds \( \frac{2w_v}{\deg(v)} \) to the weight of each neighbor of \( v \). What are necessary and sufficient conditions on \( G \) for the game to always end in a finite time?
