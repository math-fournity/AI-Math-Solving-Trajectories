Consider a locally compact Hausdorff topological group $\langle G, \cdot, \mathcal{T} \rangle$ with a left Haar measure $\mu$ that is not right-invariant. Does there exist a Borel subset $B$ of $G$ and an element $g \in G$ such that $\operatorname{closure}(B)$ is compact, and for the set $C = \{ h \in G : g^{-1}hg \in B \}$, the conditions $C \subset B$ and $\operatorname{interior}(B-C) \neq \{\}$ hold?
