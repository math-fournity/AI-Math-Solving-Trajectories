A \(90 \, \text{m}\) long train travels at a speed of \(72 \, \text{km/h}\). It is \(150 \, \text{m}\) from the level crossing when a cyclist notices it. The cyclist, \(100 \, \text{m}\) from the level crossing, is moving towards the crossing at a speed of \(6 \, \text{m/s}\). After how many seconds does the cyclist have the least distance from the end of the train? If x is the answer you obtain, report $\lfloor 10^2x \rfloor$
