A and B play a game on a $2^{100} \times 100$ grid. They take turns filling in symbols in the first row, with A going first. In each round, A chooses an empty cell in the first row and fills it with an "$\times$"; then, B chooses an empty cell in the first row and fills it with an "○". Once all the cells in the first row are filled, they start filling in the symbols in the second row, and so on, until all the cells in the grid are filled. A's goal is to have as many distinct rows as possible in the grid, while B's goal is to have as few distinct rows as possible. If both use their optimal strategies, how many distinct rows will there be in the grid in the end?
