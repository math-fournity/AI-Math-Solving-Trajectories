Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle inscribed in circle \((O)\). Let \(H\) be the orthocenter and \(D\) the midpoint of arc \(BAC\); \(BH\) intersects \(AC, AD\) at \(E, M\); \(CH\) intersects \(AB, AD\) at \(F, N\). The circle \((HMN)\) intersects circle \((AEF)\) at \(G\) (\(H \ne G\)). \(AG\) intersects \(BH, CH\) at \(P, Q\). Let \(K\) be the intersection of the median through \(G\) in triangle \(GAH\) with \(EF\), and \(L\) be the intersection of the median through \(H\) in triangle \(HPQ\) with circle \((AEF)\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{AK}{KL}\).
