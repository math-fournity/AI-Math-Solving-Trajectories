The players Alfred and Bertrand together determine a polynomial \( x^{n} + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{0} \) of the given degree \( n \geq 2 \). To do this, they alternately choose the value of each coefficient in \( n \) moves, with all coefficients having to be integers and \( a_{0} \neq 0 \). Alfred goes first. Alfred wins if the polynomial ultimately has an integer root.
