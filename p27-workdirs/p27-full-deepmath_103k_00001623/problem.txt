Let $I$ be an ideal in $A[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$, where $A$ is a Noetherian commutative ring, and suppose $I$ has a Groebner basis $G = \{g_1, \ldots, g_t\}$ with respect to some monomial order, where all the leading coefficients of each $g_i$ are equal to $1$. Given $S \subseteq \{x_1, \ldots, x_n\}$ and $f \in A[S] \cap I$, can we always find $f' \in A[S] \cap I$ such that the leading coefficient of $f'$ is $1$?
