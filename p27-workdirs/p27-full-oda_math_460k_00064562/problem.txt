Let \(ABC\) be a fixed triangle with circumcircle \((O)\) and ex-centers \(I_b\) and \(I_c\) corresponding to angles \(B\) and \(C\), respectively. The line \(I_bI_c\) meets \(BC\) at \(D\). Let \(E\) be an arbitrary point on \(BC\), and let the circumcircle of \(\triangle ADE\) intersect \((O)\) at \(K\). The circumcircles \((I_bAK)\) and \((I_cAK)\) intersect \(AC\) and \(AB\) at \(M\) and \(N\), respectively. Calculate the fixed center of the circumcircle \((MNE)\) in terms of the coordinates of \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\).
