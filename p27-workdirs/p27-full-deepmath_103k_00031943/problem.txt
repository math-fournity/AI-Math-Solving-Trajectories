In a game between David and Linus, David selects a subset \( Q \) from the set \( \{1, \ldots, 2018\} \). Linus then picks a natural number \( a_{1} \) and computes the sequence \( a_{2}, \ldots, a_{2018} \) recursively, where each \( a_{n+1} \) is the product of the positive divisors of \( a_{n} \). Define \( P \) as the set of indices \( k \in \{1, \ldots, 2018\} \) for which \( a_{k} \) is a square number. Linus wins if \( P = Q \); otherwise, David wins. Determine who has a winning strategy.
