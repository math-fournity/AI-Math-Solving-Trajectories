# IMO 2017 A7

Let $(b_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a sequence of positive integers.
Let $(a_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a sequence of integers defined by $a_0 = 0$, $a_1 = 1$, and
for $n \ge 0$:
- $a_{n + 2} = a_{n + 1} b_{n + 1} + a_n$ if $b_n = 1$;
- $a_{n + 2} = a_{n + 1} b_{n + 1} - a_n$ if $b_n > 1$.

Prove that $\max\{a_n, a_{n + 1}\} \ge n$ for any $n \ge 0$.
