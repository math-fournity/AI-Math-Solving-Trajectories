Two players each have a blank die with \(26^{5^{2019}}\) faces. Player A writes positive integers on all faces of their die such that the sum of all numbers is \(27^{5^{2019}}\). After observing Player A's die, Player B writes positive integers on their die such that the sum is also \(27^{5^{2019}}\). Both players roll their dice simultaneously, and the player with the higher number wins; if tied, they roll again. Can Player B always create a die that is more optimal than Player A's die, meaning it has a higher probability of winning regardless of Player A's configuration?
