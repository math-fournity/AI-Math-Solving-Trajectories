Let \((M^n,g)\) be a compact Riemannian manifold with boundary \(\partial M=N\). Suppose \(|Rm_g| \le C_1\) on \(M\) and the second fundamental form of \(N\) is bounded by \(C_2\). Moreover, there exists a constant \(\epsilon\) such that \(\exp_x(t n_x) \in M\backslash N\) for any \(t \in (0,\epsilon]\) and \(x \in N\), where \(n_x\) is the inner unit normal vector. Determine if there exists a constant \(L=L(C_1,C_2,\epsilon)>0\) such that the map \(F(x,t)=\exp_x(t n_x)\) on \(N \times [0,L)\) is an embedding.
