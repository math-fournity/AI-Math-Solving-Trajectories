In a remote archipelago, there are 2022 isolated research outposts. The regional council is planning to build a network of bridges to connect all outposts. However, due to strict environmental regulations, a bridge between two outposts can only be constructed if those two outposts already share at least two common "intermediate" neighbors (meaning there must already exist an outpost $A$ and an outpost $B$ such that both target outposts are already connected to $A$ and both are already connected to $B$).
