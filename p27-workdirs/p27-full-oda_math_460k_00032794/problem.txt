Let \( n>2 \) be an integer. Anna, Edda, and Magni play a game on a hexagonal board tiled with regular hexagons, with \( n \) tiles on each side. The game begins with a stone on a tile in one corner of the board. Edda and Magni are on the same team, playing against Anna, and they win if the stone is on the central tile at the end of any player's turn. Anna, Edda, and Magni take turns moving the stone: Anna begins, then Edda, then Magni, and so on.
