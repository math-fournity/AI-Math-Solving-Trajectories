In triangle \(ABC\) with circumcenter \(O\), let \(I\) be the incenter and let \(R\) be the radius of the circle centered at \(I\) with radius \(\sqrt{\frac{AB \cdot AC}{2}}\). Let \(f\) be the composition of inversion with respect to the circle centered at \(I\) with radius \(R\) and the reflection through the \(A\)-internal angle bisector. If \(C_1\) is the circumcircle of triangle \(OBC\) and \(C_2\) is the nine-point circle of triangle \(ABC\), find the ratio of the radii of the images of \(C_1\) and \(C_2\) under \(f\).
