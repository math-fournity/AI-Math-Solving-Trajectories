6. Given that there are 15 chocolates arranged in a row on the table, some of which are milk chocolates and the rest are dark chocolates. Players A and B play the following game: The player eats a certain number of consecutive chocolates starting from the left, with the requirement that the number of chocolates of the same type as the first chocolate eaten must be odd. For example, if the remaining chocolates on the table are $\operatorname{MMDMD}(\mathrm{M}$ representing milk chocolate and D representing dark chocolate), the player can eat 1, 4, or 5 consecutive chocolates from the left. The player who eats the last chocolate wins. Question: If A goes first, out of all $2^{15}$ different arrangements of chocolates, how many situations will result in B winning?
