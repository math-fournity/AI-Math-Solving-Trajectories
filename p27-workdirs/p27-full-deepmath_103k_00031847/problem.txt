A positive integer \( N \) is called *apt* if for each integer \( 0 < k < 1009 \), there exists exactly one divisor of \( N \) with a remainder of \( k \) when divided by \( 1009 \). For a prime \( p \), suppose there exists an *apt* positive integer \( N \) such that \( \frac{N}{p} \) is an integer but \( \frac{N}{p^2} \) is not. Determine the number of possible remainders when \( p \) is divided by \( 1009 \).
