Consider a measure space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mu)$ and a sub-$\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{G} \subset \mathcal{F}$. Let $\mu|_\mathcal{G}$ be the restricted measure on $\mathcal{G}$, so that $(\Omega, \mathcal{G}, \mu|_\mathcal{G})$ is also a measure space. Suppose $f: \Omega \rightarrow [0,+\infty[$ is a $\mathcal{G}$-measurable function. For any $B \in \mathcal{G}$, consider the integrals $\int_B f \, d\mu$ in the measure space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mu)$ and $\int_B f \, d\mu|_\mathcal{G}$ in the measure space $(\Omega, \mathcal{G}, \mu|_\mathcal{G})$. Are these integrals equal for all $B \in \mathcal{G}$?
