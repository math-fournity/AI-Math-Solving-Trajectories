Suppose $n=10$ rectangles are drawn in the plane such that each rectangle has parallel sides and the sides of distinct rectangles lie on distinct lines. The rectangles divide the plane into a number of regions. For each region $R$, let $v(R)$ be the number of its vertices. Let $S$ be the sum $\sum v(R)$ over all regions $R$ which have one or more vertices of the rectangles on their boundary. Find the maximum possible value of $S$.
