Given a sequence of 2000 distinct positive integers \( n_1 < n_2 < \ldots < n_{2000} < 10^{100} \), find the smallest possible value of \( k \) such that there exist two nonempty disjoint subsets \( A \) and \( B \) of the sequence with \( |A| = |B| = k \), the sum of all elements in \( A \) equals the sum of all elements in \( B \), and the sum of the squares of all elements in \( A \) equals the sum of the squares of all elements in \( B \).
