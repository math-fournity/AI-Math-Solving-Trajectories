An architect is designing a custom floor for a high-end gallery. The floor was originally planned as a grid of $8 \times 8$ large granite slabs, each measuring $1 \text{ meter} \times 1 \text{ meter}$. However, the client requested a modification: one single $1 \text{ meter} \times 1 \text{ meter}$ corner slab must be removed to accommodate a structural pillar, leaving the rest of the floor area intact.
