In a vast futuristic city designed as a perfect grid of $n \times n$ sectors ($n \geq 4$), a telecommunications company is installing high-powered signal towers. The city planners have imposed two strict regulations: first, to avoid signal interference, no two towers can be placed in the same row of sectors or in the same column of sectors. Second, to ensure maintenance connectivity, it must be possible for a specialized technician drone—which moves only in the L-shaped pattern of a chess knight—to start at one tower and visit every other tower exactly once, traversing a single path that touches every marked location.
