For $0 < n \le \sqrt{a} - 1$, find all $(n, k, \lambda) \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $S = \dfrac{(2019)^n - k}{\lambda^2}$ is a semiprime, where $k$ is the number of valid prime sequences generated by permuting the first $n$ natural numbers such that the sum of adjacent elements is always a prime number, and reversed ordered sequences are not acceptable.
