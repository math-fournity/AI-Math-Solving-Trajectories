In a specialized digital archive, there are 100 memory sectors indexed by their identification codes in the set $S = \{0, 1, 2, \ldots, 99\}$. A group of these sectors is designated as a "Stable Cluster" if it contains at least two distinct sectors, and it satisfies a specific data-linking protocol: for any two different sectors in the cluster with codes $a$ and $b$, the sector whose code is equal to the value of $(a + b + 1)$ remainder 100 must also be a member of that same cluster.
