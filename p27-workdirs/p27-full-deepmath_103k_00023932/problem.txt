Consider an ergodic Markov chain \( \{Y_t\} \) with state space \( \{0, \dots, m\} \). Given that \( E(Y_{t+1}-Y_t)=\alpha \) for \( Y_t\in [1,m-2] \), \( E(Y_{t+1}-Y_t)>\alpha \) for \( Y_t=m-1 \), and \( E(Y_{t+1}-Y_t)=\gamma \) for \( Y_t=0 \), where \( \alpha \) is a small negative number, \( \gamma>\alpha \), and \( m \) is a large positive constant, determine the behavior of \( \lim_{t\to\infty}E(Y_t)/m \) as \( m\rightarrow\infty \).
