An encoder maps source codewords $x \in \{0,1\}^K$ to channel codewords $y \in \{0,1\}^{N'}$ with $K/N' < 1$. The corresponding decoder maps a codeword $r \in \{0,1\}^{N'}$ to a source codeword $\hat{x} \in \{0,1\}^K$. Assume this coding scheme can correctly decode codewords at the output of a BSC with transition probability $q$, satisfying $K/N' \leq 1-H(q)$. Now, consider the reverse operation: an arbitrary codeword $r \in \{0,1\}^{N'}$ is input to the decoder, outputting $\hat{x} \in \{0,1\}^K$. The codeword $\hat{x}$ is then input to the encoder, outputting $y \in \{0,1\}^{N'}$. What is the probability that a certain bit of $y$ differs from the corresponding bit of $r$?
