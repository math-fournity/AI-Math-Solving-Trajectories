Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with all angles \(\leq 120^\circ\), and let \(F\) be the Fermat point of triangle \(ABC\), such that \(\angle AFB = \angle BFC = \angle CFA = 120^\circ\). For each of the triangles \(BFC\), \(CFA\), and \(AFB\), draw its Euler line (the line connecting its circumcenter and centroid). Let \(P\) be the common point where these three Euler lines intersect. If \(O_A\), \(O_B\), and \(O_C\) are the circumcenters of triangles \(AFB\), \(BFC\), and \(CFA\) respectively, and \(G_A\), \(G_B\), and \(G_C\) are their respective centroids, find the value of \(\frac{FP}{FO_A} + \frac{FP}{FO_B} + \frac{FP}{FO_C}\).
