Evaluate the limit \( \lim_{\epsilon\rightarrow0}\frac{1}{\epsilon}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\rho\left(\frac{x}{\epsilon}\right)f(x)dx \) for any continuous function \( f:\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R} \), given that \( \rho:\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R} \) is a continuous function such that \( \rho(x)\geq0 \) for all \( x\in\mathbb{R} \), \( \rho(x)=0 \) for \( |x|\geq1 \), and \( \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\rho(t)dt=1 \).
