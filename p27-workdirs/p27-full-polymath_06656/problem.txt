In a futuristic energy grid, a central processor manages power distribution across a complex network. The system’s efficiency is governed by a specific regulatory constant $c$, defined as the largest possible real number that allows for the existence of a non-constant stabilization function $P(x)$. For the grid to remain stable, the function must satisfy the balancing equation $P(z^2) = P(z-c)P(z+c)$ for every possible input load $z$.
