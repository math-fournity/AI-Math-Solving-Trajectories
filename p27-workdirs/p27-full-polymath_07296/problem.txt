In a specialized logistics hub, two specific types of shipping containers are used to store high-tech components. The total inventory is governed by a precise structural equilibrium: the number of components in a standard shipment is calculated as $x^y + 1$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive integers representing the base capacity and the scaling power, respectively. To ensure stable stacking, this total inventory must always equal $z^2$, where $z$ is a positive integer representing the side length of a square storage grid.
