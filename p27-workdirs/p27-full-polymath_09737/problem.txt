In the city of Gridhaven, a developer named Diego is planning a housing complex on a square plot of land measuring 60 meters by 60 meters. He intends to fill the entire plot with 18 identical rectangular prefabricated housing units, each measuring 10 meters by 20 meters. These units can be oriented either north-south or east-west, and they must be placed so that they do not overlap and their edges align perfectly with the boundaries of the 60x60 grid.
