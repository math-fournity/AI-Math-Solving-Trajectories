A new currency, the markka, is introduced in a country, divided into one hundred pennies. Only three different coin denominations are to be used, one of which must be the 1-penny coin. Let the three denominations be $1, a, b$ where $1 < a < b < 100$. The coins are chosen such that any purchase less than one markka (from 1 to 99 pennies) can be paid exactly using a purse containing at most $N$ coins, where $N$ is the smallest possible maximum number of coins required for any choice of $a$ and $b$.
