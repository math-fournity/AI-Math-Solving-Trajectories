Consider a sequence of conformal injective mappings \(f_n\) of the unit disk \(D\) onto the nested smooth Jordan domains \(D_n\subset D\), such that \(\cup_{n=1}^\infty D_n=D\) and \(D_n\) are images of \(n/(n+1) D\) under a fixed diffeomorphic quasi-conformal mapping of the unit disk onto itself. Assume that \(f_n\to id\) uniformly on \(D\). Determine whether the sequence \[ I_n=\int_D\frac{|f_n'(z)|^2}{|1-z|} \, dx \, dy \] is bounded.
