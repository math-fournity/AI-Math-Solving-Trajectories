Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with circumcircle \((O)\) and \(AB < AC\). Let \(I\) be the midpoint of arc \(BC\) (not containing \(A\)). Point \(K\) lies on \(AC\) such that \(IK = IC\). \(BK\) intersects \((O)\) at the second point \(D\) and intersects \(AI\) at \(E\). \(DI\) intersects \(AC\) at \(F\). Point \(M\) lies on \(DI\) such that \(CM\) is parallel to \(AD\). \(KM\) intersects \(BC\) at \(N\). The circumcircle of triangle \(BKN\) intersects \((O)\) at the second point \(P\). Let \(G\) be the midpoint of segment \(AD\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{PG}{PK}\).
