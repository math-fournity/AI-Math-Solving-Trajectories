Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with \(AC \neq BC\). Let \(I\) and \(U\) be the incenter and circumcenter of triangle \(ABC\), respectively. The incircle touches \(BC\) and \(AC\) at points \(D\) and \(E\), respectively. The circumcircles of triangles \(ABC\) and \(CDE\) intersect at points \(C\) and \(P\). Let \(S\) be the common point of the lines \(CU\) and \(PI\). If the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\) is \(R\) and the inradius is \(r\), find the value of \(\frac{CU \cdot SI}{R \cdot r}\) given that \(S\) lies on the circumcircle of triangle \(ABC\).
