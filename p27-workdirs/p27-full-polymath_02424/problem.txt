Let \(Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \ldots, Z_{n}\) be independent vectors with a standard normal distribution in \(d\) dimensions, where \(n-1>d\). Let \(\bar{Z}=\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} Z_{i}\) and \(S_{n}=\frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(Z_{i}-\bar{Z}\right)\left(Z_{i}-\bar{Z}\right)^{\top}\) denote the sample mean and the corrected empirical covariance matrix, respectively. Define the standardized sample vectors \(Y_{i}=S_{n}^{-1 / 2}\left(Z_{i}-\bar{Z}\right)\) for \(i=1,2, \ldots, n\). Let \(R(n, d) = \frac{E|Y_1 - Y_2|}{E|Z_1 - Z_2|}\). Given that \(n=11\), compute the value of the ratio \(R(11, d)\).
