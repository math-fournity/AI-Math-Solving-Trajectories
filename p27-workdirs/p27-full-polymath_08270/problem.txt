A specialized irrigation hub is designed as a circular water reservoir $\omega$. Three straight service roads, $BC$, $CA$, and $AB$, are paved such that they are perfectly tangent to the reservoir at three distribution valves: Valve $D$ (on $BC$), Valve $E$ (on $CA$), and Valve $F$ (on $AB$). To monitor the system, a straight main pipeline $AD$ is laid from the junction of the two side roads directly to the valve on the far road.
