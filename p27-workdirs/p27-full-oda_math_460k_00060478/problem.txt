Assume that we have an isolating interval representation for \( \alpha \) (relative to \( P(X) \)) and the coefficients of \( Q(Y) \) are represented by rational polynomials in \( \alpha \). Show how to carry out a Sturm sequence computation to isolate the real roots of \( Q(Y) \).
