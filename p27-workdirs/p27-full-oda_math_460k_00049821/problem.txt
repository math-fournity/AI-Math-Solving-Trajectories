In the cells of an $11 \times 11 \times 11$ cube, the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 1331$ are arranged, with one number per cell. From one corner cube to the opposite corner cube, two worms are sent. Each worm can move to an adjacent cell by face. The first worm can move if the number in the adjacent cell differs by 8, and the second worm can move if it differs by 9. Is it possible to arrange the numbers so that both worms can reach the opposite corner cell?
