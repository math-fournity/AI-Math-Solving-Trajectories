Find a Möbius transformation that fixes the origin and maps the interior of the circle $|z - 1| = 1$ to the exterior of the circle $|z + 1| = 1$. This transformation should also map the intersection points of the lines $\operatorname{Im}(z) = \pm 1$ with the circle $|z - 1| = 1$ to their corresponding intersections with the circle $|z + 1| = 1$. Determine the image of the line $\operatorname{Re}(z) = \frac{1}{2}$ under this transformation.
