Let \(\mathcal{R}\) be the set of all convex bodies in \(E^n\) that are centrally symmetric with respect to the origin. For \(K \in \mathcal{R}\), let \(PK\) denote its projection body, defined as the convex body whose support function in direction \(u\) is equal to the brightness of \(K\) in direction \(u\). Which \(K \in \mathcal{R}\) satisfy \(PK = \lambda K\) for some \(\lambda \geq 0\)?
