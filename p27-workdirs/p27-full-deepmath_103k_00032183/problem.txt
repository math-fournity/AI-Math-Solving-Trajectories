Given a positive integer $n > 1$ and distinct positive integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{n+1}$, determine if there exists a polynomial $P(x)$ with integer coefficients and degree at most $n$ such that for any $1 \leq i < j \leq n + 1$, $\gcd(P(a_i), P(a_j)) > 1$, and for any $1 \leq i < j < k \leq n + 1$, $\gcd(P(a_i), P(a_j), P(a_k)) = 1$. Provide a justification for your answer.
