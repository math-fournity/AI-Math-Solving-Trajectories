A person places 2013 balls in sequence on a circle according to the following rules: First, place the first ball, then move counterclockwise around the circle, and whenever passing a previously placed ball, place a new ball in the gap; continue this process. The ball placed on the $k$-th time is numbered $k$. After all 2013 balls are placed, find the numbers of the two balls adjacent to the 2013rd ball. Let these numbers be $x$ and $y$. Report the value of $x + y$.
