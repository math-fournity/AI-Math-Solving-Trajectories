A sequence of numbers \(a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}, \ldots, a_{8}\) is written. Initially, \(a_{k}=(-1)^{k}\) for each \(k=1, 2, \ldots, 8\). In one move, some \(k\) is chosen, and those of the numbers \(a_{k-1}\) and \(a_{k+1}\), which exist, are replaced respectively with \(a_{k-1} a_{k}\) and \(a_{k} a_{k+1}\). We say that a sequence is achievable if it can be obtained from the initial one in a finite number of moves. How many are all achievable sequences?
