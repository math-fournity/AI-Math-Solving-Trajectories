In a remote digital library, a curator manages a collection of rare manuscripts. The curator assigns an initial identification number, $a_1$, to the collection, where $a_1$ is an integer greater than 2. Following a specific archival protocol, every subsequent cataloging cycle $n$ (where $n \geq 1$) generates a new index number $a_{n+1}$ based on the previous index $a_n$ using the formula $a_{n+1} = a_n^n - 1$.
