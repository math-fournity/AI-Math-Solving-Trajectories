In an acute-angled triangle \(ABC\), altitudes \(AD\), \(BE\), and \(CF\) are drawn, intersecting at a point \(H\). Let \(O\) be the center of the circumscribed circle \(\omega\). The tangents to the circle \(\omega\), drawn at points \(B\) and \(C\), intersect at the point \(T\). The points \(K\) and \(L\) are symmetrical to the point \(O\) with respect to \(AB\) and \(AC\), respectively. The circles \((DFK)\) and \((DEL)\) intersect at a point \(P\) other than \(D\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{PT}{PD}\).
