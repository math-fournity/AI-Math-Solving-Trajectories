Given triangle \(ABC\) with \(I\) as the incenter, \(D\), \(E\), and \(F\) as the points of tangency of the incircle with sides \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) respectively. Let \(A'\) be the reflection of \(A\) over line \(EF\), \(B'\) be the reflection of \(B\) over line \(DF\), and \(C'\) be the reflection of \(C\) over line \(DE\). Let \(X\), \(Y\), and \(Z\) be the intersections of \(A'D\) and \(B'E\), \(B'E\) and \(C'F\), and \(C'F\) and \(A'D\) respectively. Let \(K\) be the orthocenter of triangle \(DEF\). Calculate the ratio of the distance between the orthocenter of triangle \(XYZ\) and \(K\) to the distance between \(K\) and \(I\).
