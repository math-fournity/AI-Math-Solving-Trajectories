Consider the set of positive integers divided into an infinite number of infinite subsets such that if \( x, y, z, w \) all belong to the same subset, then \( x - y \) and \( z - w \) belong to the same subset if and only if \( \frac{x}{y} = \frac{z}{w} \). Let \( S_n \) be the subset containing the integer \( n \). Find the sum of all integers in the subset \( S_{12} \) that are less than 1000.
