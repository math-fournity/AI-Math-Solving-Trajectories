The scorpion tries to catch the flea on a $10 \times 10$ chessboard. The length of the side of each small square of the chessboard is $1$. In this game, the flea and the scorpion move alternately. The flea is always on one of the $121$ vertices of the chessboard and, in each turn, can jump from the vertex where it is to one of the adjacent vertices. The scorpion moves on the boundary line of the chessboard, and, in each turn, it can walk along any path of length less than $d$, where $d$ is a constant of the game. At the beginning, the flea is at the center of the chessboard and the scorpion is at a point that he chooses on the boundary line. The flea is the first one to play. The flea is said to escape if it reaches a point of the boundary line, which the scorpion can't reach in the next turn. Find the smallest value of $d$ for which the flea can guarantee an escape.
