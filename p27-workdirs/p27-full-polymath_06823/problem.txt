A specialized logistics network is designed as an infinite grid of storage containers, indexed by a tier level $i \geq 0$ and a horizontal position $j \in \mathbb{Z}$. The inventory in each container, denoted by $A(i, j)$, is an integer determined by a specific flow rule: the stock in any container is exactly equal to the sum of the stock in the container directly above it ($A(i-1, j)$) and the container directly to its left ($A(i, j-1)$).
