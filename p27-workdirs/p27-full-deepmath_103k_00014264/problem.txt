Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space over the field with $p$ elements, where $p$ is a prime number, and let $d$ be the dimension of $V$. Two subsets $A$ and $A'$ of $V$ are equivalent if there exists an automorphism $f$ such that $f(A) = A'$. This defines an equivalence relation on the set of subsets of $V$. Determine the number of equivalence classes containing generating subsets of $V$ with $d+1$ elements.
