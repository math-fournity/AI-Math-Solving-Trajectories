Consider a collection of strong triples $(a, b, c)$ such that for each integer $m > 1$, the number $a + b + c$ does not divide $a^m + b^m + c^m$, and the sums of these triples are all pairwise coprime. Let $S$ be the set of all such sums. Find the smallest possible value of the sum $s \in S$ such that $s > 100$.
