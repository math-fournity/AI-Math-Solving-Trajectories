Determine whether there exists a real analytic diffeomorphism $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $f(x_i) = a_i$ for all $i = 1, \dots, k$, given a finite set $F = \{x_1, \dots, x_k\} \subset \mathbb{R}$ with $x_i < x_{i+1}$ for $i = 1, \dots, k-1$, and real numbers $a_1, \dots, a_k \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $a_i < a_{i+1}$ for $i=1,\dots,k-1$. Provide a justification for your answer.
