A circular power grid consists of $n$ relay stations. Each station is in one of two states: "Active" (represented by $1$) or "Inactive" (represented by $0$). Due to a specific safety override protocol, a technician is permitted to change the state of two adjacent stations simultaneously—toggling them from $(0,0)$ to $(1,1)$ or from $(1,1)$ to $(0,0)$—but only if their current states are identical. If two adjacent stations have different states, the override cannot be applied to that pair.
