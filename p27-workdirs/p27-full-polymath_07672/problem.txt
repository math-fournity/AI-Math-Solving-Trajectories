In a specialized data-storage warehouse, a square grid of 64 server racks is arranged in an $8 \times 8$ formation. A technician is tasked with installing 64 hardware modules, uniquely labeled with serial numbers from 1 to 64, into these racks. To ensure optimal heat distribution, the modules must be installed such that any two modules with consecutive serial numbers (differing by exactly 1) are housed in racks that share a common side.
