Given a square with side length \( x \). Construct points \( A, B, C, D \) inside the square and draw a circle around each of them with radius 1, denoted as \( \Gamma_A, \Gamma_B, \Gamma_C, \Gamma_D \). The circles do not intersect each other and do not intersect the sides of the square (but can touch them). Find the minimal value of \( x \) for which there do not exist points \( E \in \Gamma_A, F \in \Gamma_B, G \in \Gamma_C, H \in \Gamma_D \) such that \( EFGH \) forms a cyclic quadrilateral.
