Consider an acute angled triangle \(\triangle ABC\) with side lengths \(7, 8,\) and \(9\). Let \(H\) be the orthocenter of \(\triangle ABC\). Let \(\Gamma_A, \Gamma_B,\) and \(\Gamma_C\) be the circumcircles of \(\triangle BCH, \triangle CAH,\) and \(\triangle ABH\) respectively. Find the area of the region \(\Gamma_A \cup \Gamma_B \cup \Gamma_C\) (the set of all points contained in at least one of \(\Gamma_A, \Gamma_B,\) and \(\Gamma_C\)).
