At the vertices of a regular hexagon are written six nonnegative integers whose sum is \(2003^{2003}\). Bert is allowed to make moves of the following form: he may pick a vertex and replace the number written there by the absolute value of the difference between the numbers written at the two neighboring vertices. Let \(S\) be the sum of the numbers at the vertices after Bert makes a sequence of moves. Find the smallest possible value of \(S\) that can be achieved.
