In a specialized cryptographic network, a "secure signal" is defined as a polynomial $f$ with integer coefficients that satisfies a specific vulnerability test: for every prime number $p$, the signal must be decomposable into two smaller signals, $g_p$ and $h_p$, such that the degree of both $g_p$ and $h_p$ is strictly less than the degree of $f$. This decomposition is valid if all coefficients of the difference $f - g_p h_p$ are multiples of $p$.
