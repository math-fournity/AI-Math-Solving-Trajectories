Find all real numbers \(\lambda\) such that the polynomial equation \[ x^{n} + \lambda\left(x^{n-1} + \cdots + x^{3} + W x^{2} + A B x + 1\right) = 0 \] has only real roots, where \( n \geq 4 \), and \(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n} ; \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{n} \) are two sets of real numbers satisfying \(\sum_{j=1}^{n} \alpha_{j}^{2}<1\) and \(\sum_{j=1}^{n} \beta_{j}^{2}<1\). Here, \( A^{2}=1-\sum_{j=1}^{n} \alpha_{j}^{2} \), \( B^{2}=1-\sum_{j=1}^{n} \beta_{j}^{2} \), and \( W=\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\sum_{j=1}^{n} \alpha_{j} \beta_{j}\right)^{2} \).
