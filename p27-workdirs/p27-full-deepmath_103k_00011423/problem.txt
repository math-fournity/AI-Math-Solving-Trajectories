Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $\phi$ and $\psi$ be two automorphisms of $G$ of order $p$, where $p$ is a prime number. Suppose that for every $g \in G$, the sets $\{\phi(g), \phi^2(g), \ldots, \phi^p(g)\}$ and $\{\psi(g), \psi^2(g), \ldots, \psi^p(g)\}$ are equal. Is the subgroup generated by $\phi$ equal to the subgroup generated by $\psi$?
