In an acute-angled triangle \(ABC\), the medians \(AA_0\), \(BB_0\), and \(CC_0\) intersect at the centroid \(M\), and the altitudes \(AA_1\), \(BB_1\), and \(CC_1\) intersect at the orthocenter \(H\). The tangent to the circumcircle of triangle \(A_1B_1C_1\) at \(C_1\) intersects the line \(A_0B_0\) at point \(C'\). Points \(A'\) and \(B'\) are defined similarly. Let \(d\) be the distance from the centroid \(M\) to the line \(A'B'C'\). If the circumradius of triangle \(ABC\) is \(R\) and the length of the segment \(MH\) is \(d_{MH}\), find the value of \(\frac{d}{d_{MH}}\).
