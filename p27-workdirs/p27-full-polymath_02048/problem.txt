Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers such that $k\leq 2^n$. Banana and Corona are playing the following variant of a guessing game. First, Banana secretly picks an integer $x$ such that $1\leq x\leq n$. Corona will attempt to determine $x$ by asking some questions. In each turn, Corona chooses $k$ distinct subsets of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ and, for each chosen set $S$, asks the question "Is $x$ in the set $S$?".
