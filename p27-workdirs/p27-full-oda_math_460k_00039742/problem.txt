Given a polynomial \( f(X) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} A_k X^{n-k} \) with only positive roots, and non-negative integers \( p, q, r, s, t \) where \( q \ge 1 \) and \( n \ge 2 \), define \( B_k = B_k(p, q, r, s, t) := \frac{{n \choose k}^p A_k^q A_{n-k}^r (k+1)^s}{(k!)^t} \). Let \( Tf(X) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} B_k X^{n-k} \). Determine the value of \( p + q + r + s + t \) for which \( Tf(X) \) has only real roots, given that \( A_k = k+1 \) for all \( k \).
