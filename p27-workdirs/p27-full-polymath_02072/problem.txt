Stanford has a new admissions process that it would like to test out on the Stanford Class of 2027. An admissions officer starts by ordering applicants \(1, 2, \ldots, 2022\) in a circle with applicant \(1\) being after applicant \(2022\). Then, starting with applicant \(1\), the admissions officer removes every \(2023\)rd applicant. What is the number of the applicant removed in the \(49\)th iteration?
