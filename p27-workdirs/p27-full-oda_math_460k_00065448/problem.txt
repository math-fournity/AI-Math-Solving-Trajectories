Given a positive integer \( n \geq 3 \), if there exists a positive real number \( k > 1 \) such that for any positive real numbers \( x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots x_{n} \) satisfying \( \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i} = k \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{x_{i}} \), the inequality \( \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{x_{i}^{2}}{x_{i}^{2}+1} \leq k \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{x_{i}^{2}+1} \) must hold, then \( k \) is called a "good number". Find the smallest good number \( k \).
