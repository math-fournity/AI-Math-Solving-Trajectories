Problem:
Let $b_0$, $b_1$, $b_2$, $\dots$ be an infinite sequence of complex numbers such that $b_0 = e^{i\pi/7}$ and
\[
b_{n} = b_{n-1}^3 - 3b_{n-1}^2 + 1
\]for every positive integer $n$.  Let $d$ be the smallest number such that for every positive integer $n$, the sum of the first $n$ terms satisfies the inequality
\[|b_0 + b_1 + \dots + b_{n - 1}| \le d\sqrt{n}.\]What is the value of $\lfloor 1000d \rfloor$?
