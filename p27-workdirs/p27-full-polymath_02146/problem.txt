The country of Claredena has 5 cities and is planning to build a road system so that each of its cities has exactly one outgoing (unidirectional) road to another city. Two road systems are considered equivalent if we can get from one road system to the other by just changing the names of the cities. That is, two road systems are considered the same if given a relabeling of the cities, if in the first configuration a road went from city \(C\) to city \(D\), then in the second configuration there is a road that goes from the city now labeled \(C\) to the city now labeled \(D\).
