Consider a graph \( G \) with list chromatic number \( \chi_{\ell}(G) = t+1 \). Let \( S \) be the set of all possible lists \( L(v_i) \) of colors for each vertex \( v_i \) in \( G \) such that \( |L(v_i)| = t \) and \( |\cup_{i=1}^{n} L(v_i)| \leq |V(G)| \), and \( G \) cannot be colored using these lists \( L(v_i) \). Calculate the minimum value of \( |S| \) for a graph \( G \) with \( |V(G)| = 10 \) and \( t = 3 \).
