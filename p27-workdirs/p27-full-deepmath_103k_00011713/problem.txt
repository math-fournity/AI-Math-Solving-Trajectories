In a Banach space $E$, consider biorthogonal basic sequences $e_{n} \in E$ and $g_{n} \in E^{*}$, where $\langle e_n, g_m \rangle = \delta_{mn}$, and both sequences are weakly null. Can we always find $\alpha > 0$, an infinite-dimensional subspace $H \subset E$, and a weakly compact set $D \subset E^{*}$ such that $\sup_{d \in D} |\langle h, d \rangle| \ge \alpha \|h\|$ for every $h \in H$?
