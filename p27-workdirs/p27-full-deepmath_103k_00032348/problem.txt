Consider a square grid of size \(2011 \times 2011\) where each cell is labeled with a unique integer from 1 to \(2011^2\). The grid is wrapped to form a torus by identifying the left and right edges, as well as the top and bottom edges. Determine the largest positive integer \(M\) such that, for any labeling of the grid, there exist two neighboring cells with a difference in their labels of at least \(M\). Cells \((x,y)\) and \((x',y')\) are neighbors if \(x = x'\) and \(y - y' \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{2011}\), or if \(y = y'\) and \(x - x' \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{2011}\).
