In the city of Metropolia, a civil engineer is designing a high-speed transit rail represented by a specialized path function $P(x)$. This path is classified as "Dual-Stable" if it satisfies a specific structural requirement: for every whole-numbered station coordinate $k$, both the elevation of the track $P(k)$ and the physical grade (slope) of the track $P'(k)$ must result in perfect whole numbers.
