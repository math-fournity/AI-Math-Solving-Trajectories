Consider a path $\sigma$ in the complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ starting at $-1$ and ending at $+1$, and a closed curve $\gamma$ that does not intersect $\sigma$. Let $\sqrt{1-z^2}$ be the analytic branch defined on $\mathbb{C}\setminus\sigma$. Determine the possible values of the integral $\displaystyle\int_{\gamma} \frac{dz}{\sqrt{1-z^2}}$. Express your answer in terms of $2\pi k$ for integer $k$.
