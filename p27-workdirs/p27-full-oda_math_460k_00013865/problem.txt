Let \(ABC\) be a triangle with circumcircle \(\Gamma\). Let \(D\) be a point on \(\Gamma\) and points \(E\) and \(F\) lie on the lines \(AB\) and \(AC\) respectively such that \(AEDF\) is a parallelogram. Let \(O_1\) and \(O_2\) be the circumcenters of triangles \(AEF\) and \(DEF\), respectively. As \(D\) moves on \(\Gamma\), \(O_1\) and \(O_2\) move on two fixed ellipses with perpendicular major axes. Find the ratio of the lengths of the major axes of these ellipses.
