A specialized deep-sea research probe is programmed to record a "Data Peak" whenever its internal sensor value \( P(x) \) is exactly 1 unit, and a "Data Trough" whenever the sensor value is exactly -1 unit, where \( x \) represents the time in seconds over a mission duration. The sensor's behavior is governed by a continuous modeling function \( P(x) \) defined as a polynomial of degree 100 with real coefficients.
