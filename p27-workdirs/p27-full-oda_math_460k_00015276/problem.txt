Given a triangle \(ABC\) inscribed in circle \((O)\). Let \(P\) be a point on the perpendicular bisector of \(BC\). Let \(Q\) be the isogonal conjugate of \(P\) with respect to triangle \(ABC\). The lines \(AQ\), \(BQ\), and \(CQ\) intersect \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) at points \(D\), \(E\), and \(F\) respectively. Let \(J\) be the circumcenter of triangle \(BQC\). Let \(PI\) be the diameter of the circumcircle of triangle \(BPC\). The line \(DI\) intersects \(QJ\) at \(M\). Let \(N\) be the circumcenter of triangle \(PEF\). If \(OM\) intersects the circumcircle of triangle \(ABC\) again at \(K\), find the ratio \(\frac{OK}{OM}\).
