Suppose $X$ is a compact topological space and $A \subset C(X)$ is a linear subspace of the space of continuous functions on $X$. Assume that for any $x, y \in X$ with $x \neq y$ and any $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$, there exists a function $f \in A$ such that $f(x) = a$ and $f(y) = b$. Additionally, assume that if $g \in A$, then $g^3, g^4, g^5, \ldots \in A$. Determine whether it is true that $g^2 \in A$ for all $g \in A$. Provide a justification for your answer.
