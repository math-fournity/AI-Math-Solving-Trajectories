In a remote logistics hub, two dispatchers, Alberto and Barbara, are managing a cargo shipment sequence. The process begins with a single crate labeled "1" on the loading dock. They take turns adding crates to the queue. If the current total number of crates is $n$, the person whose turn it is can choose to increase the total to either $n+1$ or $2n$. However, there is a strict safety limit: the total number of crates cannot exceed a fixed capacity $N$.
