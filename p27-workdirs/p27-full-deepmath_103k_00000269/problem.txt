In a game played by two players, Q and A, at each time point i, player Q selects a set $Q_i \subset \mathbb{R}$, and player A responds by choosing $A_i$ such that $A_i = Q_i$ or $A_i = Q_i^c$. Define $C(i) = \bigcap_{k < i} A_k$ and $C(\infty) = \bigcap_{k \in \mathbb{N}} A_k$. Player Q wins if either $C(i)$ has only one element for some finite i, or $C(\infty)$ is empty. Player A wins in all other cases. Determine which player has a winning strategy.
