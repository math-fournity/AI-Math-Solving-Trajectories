Given two circles $\odot O_{1}$ and $\odot O_{2}$ intersecting at points $A$ and $B$, and the midpoint $M$ of the minor arc $\overparen{A B}$ of $\odot O_{1}$ lies inside $\odot O_{2}$. Determine a point $P$ on the major arc $\overparen{A B}$ such that the points $P$, the intersection $S$ of $P M$ with $A B$, and the two intersection points $R$ and $Q$ of $P M$ with $\odot O_{2}$ form a harmonic quadruple.
