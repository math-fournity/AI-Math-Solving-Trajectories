Consider the region $T = \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}: x \geq 0, y \geq 0\}$. Let $f$ be a continuous function that vanishes outside the disk of radius $R$, i.e., $f(x, y) = 0$ for $x^2 + y^2 > R$. Suppose $u$ is a bounded solution to the equation $\Delta u = f$ in $T$ with the boundary conditions $u(x, 0) = 0$ and $u_x(0, y) = 0$. Determine whether $u \to 0$ as $x^2 + y^2 \to \infty$. Provide a justification for your answer.
