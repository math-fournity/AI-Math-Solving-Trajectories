Consider the power series \( \sum_{n,m = 0}^{\infty} a_{n,m} x^{n} y^{m} \) with real coefficients, which is absolutely convergent for every real \( x, y \) such that \( |x| < \alpha \) and \( |y| < \beta \), where \( \alpha > 0 \) and \( \beta > 0 \). Rearrange the series in ascending powers of \( x \) as \( \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty} f_n(y) x^{n} \), where \( f_n:(-\beta,\beta) \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \) are analytic functions. For every fixed \( y \in (-\beta,\beta) \), let \( R(y) \) be the radius of convergence of the series in \( x \). Is \( R(y) \) a continuous function of \( y \)?
