Let \(ABC\) be an acute triangle with orthocenter \(H\) and feet of the altitudes \(D, E, F\) from \(A, B, C\) respectively. Let \(N\) be the nine-point center of \(\triangle ABC\) and \(T\) be the orthocenter of \(\triangle DEF\). Let \(D', E', F'\) be the reflections of \(D, E, F\) in \(N\) respectively. Calculate the ratio \(\frac{TH}{HP}\), where \(P\) is the point of concurrency of \(AD', BE', CF'\).
