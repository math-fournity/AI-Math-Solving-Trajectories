A magician and an assistant perform a trick. There is a round drum with $n$ sectors. In the absence of the magician, the audience writes either a $0$ or a $1$ in each sector. The assistant then covers one of the numbers with a black card, and the drum is spun so its orientation is unknown to the magician. The magician enters and must guess the number under the card based on the remaining $n-1$ visible numbers in their circular order.
