Let \( K/\mathbb{Q} \) be a cubic number field that is Galois with class number 1. Thus, \( \text{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}) \) is a cyclic cubic group, and \( \mathcal{O}_K \) is a principal ideal domain (PID). Let \( p \) be a rational prime that does not divide the discriminant of \( K \). Then \( p \) either splits completely or is inert in \( K \). Consider the unit group \( \mathcal{O}_K^* \). Does there exist a unit \( \epsilon \) in \( \mathcal{O}_K^* \) such that \((\pi)\) divides \((\epsilon -1)\) but \((\pi^2)\) does not divide \((\epsilon -1)\), where \( \pi \) is an unramified prime over \( \mathbb{Q} \)?
