Let \(ABC\) be an acute-angled triangle. Let the altitude from \(B\) intersect the circle with diameter \(AC\) at points \(X_1\) and \(X_2\) (with \(X_1\) closer to \(B\)). Similarly, define \(Y_1\) and \(Y_2\) with respect to the altitude from \(C\) and the circle with diameter \(AB\). Let the perpendicular dropped from \(A\) to \(Y_2X_1\) intersect \(Y_2B\) at \(Z_1\). Similarly, define \(Z_2\) symmetrically. Let \(CZ_1\) intersect \(BX_1\) at \(P_1\) and \(BZ_2\) intersect \(CY_1\) at \(P_2\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{BP_1}{P_1X_1}\) if \(AB = 13\), \(BC = 14\), and \(CA = 15\).
