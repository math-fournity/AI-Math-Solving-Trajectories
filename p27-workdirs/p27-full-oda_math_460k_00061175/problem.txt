Given an acute triangle \(ABC\) with altitudes \(AD\), \(BE\), and \(CF\) concurrent at \(H\), two circles passing through \(E\) and \(F\) are tangent to \(BC\) at \(X_1\) and \(X_2\), where \(X_1\) lies between \(B\) and \(C\). Similarly, define points \(Y_1, Y_2, Z_1, Z_2\). Let \(P\) be the point of concurrency of the lines \(AX_1\), \(BY_1\), and \(CZ_1\). If the area of triangle \(ABC\) is 60 and the radius of the circumcircle of triangle \(ABC\) is 10, find the length of segment \(AP\).
