Let \(S_1'\) be the symmetry point of \(S_1\) about \(AB\), \(O_1\) and \(O_2\) be the circumcenters of the equilateral triangles \(\triangle ABD'\) and \(\triangle ABD\) respectively, and the circumcircle of \(\triangle CD'D\) be \(Q\). Let \(P = S_1O_2 \cap BC\). Calculate the ratio \(\frac{BP}{PC}\) if \(AB = 13\), \(BC = 14\), and \(CA = 15\).
